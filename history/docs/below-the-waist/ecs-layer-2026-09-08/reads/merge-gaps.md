**Referee account: the gaps pair**

The expectation held for Claude's early shape-making, but broke as a clean division between a literal Codex and a generalizing Claude. It also failed to predict the length of the all-three list: Claude has ten rows; Codex has eight. Both readers generalize. They generalize in different directions, and sometimes count different things under similar names.

This account compares [Claude's read][C] and [Codex's read][X] against the supplied [baseline][B], [Place / Inside write-up][P], [Workspace / Anatomy handoff][W], and [Vantage account][V]. Those six documents are the evidence. References below point into those documents; their linked code, receipts and other files were not opened. This account leaves selection of the next layer to the later reading of all three pairs.

**Where they agree.** Both locate the repeated addition in a client that connects records, ongoing input, computation, presentation, source inspection, editing and working context. They preserve the inherited executor, rendering and storage machinery as baseline. Both put the completed durable wire in Workspace and Vantage, inspectable activity records in Place and Workspace, and authoring layers, actual agent turns and imported code exploration in Vantage alone. Place's wire remains explicitly unfinished. These are counts of what the builds supplied; Place's unfinished wire also records a need. [C3–38][C3] [X13–49][X13]

The documentary consequences of those differences are these:

- **The first two rows expose the cost of Claude's compression.** The shared pattern of a working client is visible in all three accounts. Three independently implemented copies of one layer are not established by that pattern. Workspace expressly distinguishes shared ancestry and worktree activity from interchangeable features, and reports other-lane Vantage/Region3D edits present during its close. Its systems are explicit functions and commands, with no automatic dependency graph. Claude's own “no input rule is stated” also limits its opening “declared inputs in every build”. Codex preserves that limit. [W7] [W86] [W144]
- **The text difference is chiefly the unit being counted.** Claude counts native text joined to an application; Codex counts making text an executor tool. The baseline already supplies native text layout and rendering. Place and Vantage explicitly add the executor connection; Workspace describes using native text. The table cells substantially agree, while the headings put the work at different levels. Claude's categorical “not as a tool record” is stronger than Codex's documentary “does not describe”. [B51] [P72] [W90] [V56]
- **The shared claim of ancestry and exact return exceeds the supplied Workspace account.** Place and Vantage describe predecessor/parent navigation. Workspace describes saved context and exact revision references; an entity's parent revision does not establish a navigation ancestry mechanism. Codex's broader working-context label preserves that difference. [P272] [W58] [W64] [W81] [V81]
- **The read-work row changes the claimed incidence of a particular mechanism.** The baseline's DESIGN-1 §12 inheritance concerns transient computation custody plus store-owned request/result work under full declared inputs. Workspace describes that connection. Vantage describes completed-layout treatment and a pointer answer checked against snapshots. Its account does not document the corresponding durable read-work connection. The supplied texts support Codex's separation; Claude's two-build heading joins distinct read boundaries under the stronger custody label. [B93] [W54] [W82] [W102] [V62] [V162]
- **The context and construction rows show different choices of what deserves a noun.** Workspace and Vantage both document readable context-producing tools, which Codex isolates while Claude uses Workspace's preview chiefly to delimit the agent claim. Claude isolates Inside/Anatomy as a repeated construction-explanation mode; Codex mentions them to delimit actual code exploration. Neither difference is evidence that the other reader denied the underlying feature. [W60] [V130] [P131] [W108]
- **The sphere row separates repetition from generality.** Both recognize the connection. Codex explicitly limits what recurrence of the same specimen proves. Claude proceeds to select a particular implementation. That selection is additional architectural judgment beyond documenting the repeated addition.
- **Both readers begin choosing an answer to the later question.** Claude openly selects an envelope, rerun/delivery approach, wire, sphere skin, pointer combination and text tool. Codex ranks a common authoring vocabulary as the most consequential gap. Claude goes further by claiming that Vantage's storage already fits Workspace's envelope, although neither account supplies that compatibility demonstration. Codex's vocabulary gap is directly supported by the three write-ups; its priority over other gaps remains the reader's judgment. [C65] [X55] [P342] [W131] [V258]
- **Claude's specific executor merge claim outruns its own declared uncertainty.** Its silence item 5 asks whether Vantage changed the executor file or only the surrounding value boundary. Place names an executor source change; Vantage names a resolved-read boundary without specifying that file mutation. That permits a behavioral difference to be recorded, but leaves the asserted two-tree executor-file merge unestablished. Nor can the four documents identify actual conflicts. [C46] [C54] [P90] [V62]
- **Claude's performance shape is broader than the measurements' stated reach.** Workspace says its delayed view adoption does not rerun the brush and that no end-to-end latency breakdown was established. Vantage excludes network, GPU packing and frame latency from its typing measurement. Place separately records bounded raster work and surface-comparison changes. Those limits allow the reported local explanations to be retained; they do not establish Claude's universal exclusion of rendering or its claim that Workspace's unstated rerun rule costs the reported stall. Codex carries the cases without that universal causal conclusion. [W104] [V62] [P241] [P278] [C50]

The predicted **early shape** is plainly present: Claude opens with one world that “now exists three times”, gives it “The change owner”, and makes the performance cases an argument about that owner's essential problem. The shorter-Claude-list signal does not reliably describe the artifact. This is a finding about these two reads, not a general model ranking.

**Two qualifications to the reading itself.**

Both reads leave the baseline's starting-point qualification uncarried. The baseline ties Place and Vantage to `9f28121`, then says “Other builds' starting points have not been checked here.” Workspace supplies its own later checkpoints, but these six documents do not establish its exact starting-snapshot equivalence. The documented abilities can be compared; a precise attribution of every Workspace delta to that same starting tree remains qualified by the baseline. This is a common unmarked limit in the pair, and cannot be settled within the permitted documents. [B8] [W7] [W139]
