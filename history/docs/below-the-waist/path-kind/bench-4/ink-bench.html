s is the signed distance in world units: negative inside, zero on the boundary, positive outside. The cursor value is computed on the CPU with the same formula the shader uses, under the chosen stroke rule, and it also shows what the other rule says at that point. Today's triangle road is the ribbon rule by construction. Presets marked harness are the path family's own golden fixtures; the others are the shapes named in the question.
