# Economy on the ledger

**Law.** Attention, trust, and credit are three currencies materialized as
views over the same provenance log that carries everything else. The
attention machinery works for the mind whose attention it is — nothing in
the commons is ever optimized to spend it.

**Why it binds.** A commons with no credit answer gets silence or spam; a
commons that spends its members' attention for its own metrics is a feed,
and feeds are the disease. Because every act is signed and every basis is an
edge, citation is structural rather than ritual — the ledger pays you back.

**Horizon note.** The exchange deliberately withheld this clause from
product architecture: at one human, the currencies "are not product
architecture yet — the horizon that becomes load-bearing the moment repair
crews exist" ([exchange-04](../provenance/exchange-04-meta-syntheses.md),
synthesis 1, the dilution filter). Its first product organ is nonetheless
placed: check-strength arithmetic lives in the graph economy
([settlement-publication-custody](settlement-publication-custody.md), A4
placement).
