# Custody floor

**Law.** Custody rides declared blast radius over a non-declarable floor.
Every family's citizenship declares checker tiers with teeth proportional to
what a wrong value can break — blast radius is a declared field per medium,
not a second architecture. Declarations are data; the floor is what checks
declarations. The never-migrating floor — the executor and its mathematics,
the grammars and compilers, the funnel, the escape gestures — is the
absolute the declarations stand on, and no declaration can reach it.
Admission of a declaration — either citizenship form — is itself a
floor-tier act. Inside authored things the code/data line is mechanical:
closed selection and shaping under admission-bounded cost are data (the
interpreter total, the cost bounded); open terms — computation — are code,
forever.

**Why it binds.** If blast-radius tiering were declarable all the way down,
masquerade returns one layer up: a medium declaring itself low-blast is a
weak checker wearing soundness's uniform, at the citizenship layer. So the
tiers are declared, but admitted by the floor — confirmed in ratification
"with the reason stated so it binds."
