# Convergence is an event

**Law.** Convergence is an event, not a state. The classifier is two-sided:
schema fixes coexistence at the funnel — the write-side geometry of how acts
live together (plural keyed rows, accumulating edges, registries, minted
conflict rows; rentable, CRDT-grade) — and demand mints convergence read-side,
as decision rows: signed views citing plural inputs, deleting nothing. The
resting state of knowledge is plurality and articulation; the unresolved span
is a first-class object. The commons never converges; it accretes
convergences.

**Why it binds.** Every predecessor ran one law over all conflict classes and
was brittle for it. The write path must be decidable in the millisecond an
act lands, without consulting the demand graph's philosophy; convergence
belongs to dependents at cuts — a ship date, a verdict, a settlement. A
decision is a view that took responsibility: derived content at act-grade
provenance, citable and load-bearing at once. A conflict shelved today is
resolvable by a better mind in ten years only if it was kept as structure.
