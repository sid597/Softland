# Structural guarantee (meta-law)

**Law.** No clause calls a guarantee structural because a record exists. A
structural guarantee names its producer, its transition owner, its obligated
consumer, and its clearing event.

**Why it binds.** This is the kin blind spot the falsification round caught,
from two model families independently: all three kin sessions assumed that
emitting an addressable record at one seam also proves its lifecycle and
downstream consumption. The counterexamples were concrete — a fail-open lens
invocation behind a "structural" basis guarantee, an addressable hole with
no resolving transition, a coverage flag nothing was obliged to read
correctly. Claude's sealed pass found the same generator three times by
running the falsification checklist's ownership/lifecycle/consumer
questions; Codex named it as law. This is the checklist ascending into the
constitution.

**Marked meta-law.** It governs how every other clause may speak, on the
essays' precedent of [no-exits](no-exits.md) as a named meta-clause. Its
placement as the thirteenth entry of the signed span's constitution is the
meta session's default with Sid's veto standing
([decision](../decision-2026-08-15-span-signed.md)).

**Enacted by.** Every part. Codex's missing-organ table is this law run over
the span; it folds into each architecture file's open custody questions, and
Codex's "receipt lifecycle organ" proposal is the law's organ form — direct
input to the part contracts.
