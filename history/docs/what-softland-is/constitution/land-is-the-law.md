# The land is the law

**Law.** The law's quantities reach humans as material physics: staleness as
patina, load-bearing depth as mass, darkness as enclosure, presence as
weather, an opposes-edge as a visible seam, the basis-diff as morning light.
The renderer is a kernel; the skin is a lease. Every material property
derives auditably from the ledger — press the patina, get the basis. Ground
truth is shared; salience is plural. The renderer is the attention
allocator.

**Why it binds.** Perception is the only channel that scales: preattentive
vision and spatial memory are the highest-bandwidth channels humans own and
exactly the channels a ledger can't reach — so the feel is not presentation
on top of the law; it is the law's delivery mechanism to the human half of
the commons. And a renderer that decides what every human perceives,
preattentively, is the most powerful judgment-holder in the system — so it
is brought inside the law it delivers: mechanism as trusted kernel,
calibration as leased, auditable data. Skins forked all the way down would
dissolve deixis into private hallucinations of a common land — hence ground
shared, salience plural.
