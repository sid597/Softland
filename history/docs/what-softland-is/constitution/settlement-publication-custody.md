# Settlement, publication, custody

**Law.** Settlement and publication are two axes under three-way custody.
The settlement profile is medium-declared — candidacy | committed | op-log: how
input accretes into acts, and what the act quantum is (the profiles are
fields of the [citizenship contract](../architecture/citizenship-contract.md)).
The publication shape is universal, owned by no medium: every medium's acts
cross audience boundaries through the one decision-event shape — signed,
scoped, grounded, rollback standing — with the workshop as the scope between
subject and world. The publication cadence — quantum × darkness — is place
policy, per workspace. Undo stratifies: recordless below the act quantum
(input, not acts — that is what the settlement quantum means);
profile-shaped in the workshop (abandon the candidate / inverse splice as a
new act / inverse op); above the publication boundary undo does not exist —
only the compensating public act.

**Dark ground and porosity.** No public act stands on silent dark ground.
Declared darkness is legal: it publishes as a hole — first-class and
addressable — and it is priced: anything standing on dark ground is
discounted, so dark-based claims cannot mint strong verification. Every hole
names its resolution owner; publication of a formerly-dark thing closes
matching holes by an explicit append-only transition that owner runs — that
transition is the discount-lift trigger. The pricing itself (check-strength
arithmetic) is a placed organ of the [graph
economy](../architecture/graph-economy.md), versioned and receipted. The
dark-closure check at publish runs over incrementally maintained direct
holes — acts carry basis at act time — and never walks the transitive graph.
An occurrence query into a foreign workshop answers nothing by default;
whether it may answer with a count-only presence signal is the place's dial.

**Why it binds.** Fusing the axes pins the darkness dial at zero — every
authoring act becomes a world-visible publish, premature settlement forced
by architecture. A medium that dictated publication cadence would be a
medium dictating social settings — a custody error. Hard closure has its own
pathology — a forced-march un-darkening that overrides the very dial the
custody split protects — while undeclared dark ground is the essays' poison:
unauditable load-bearing trust. Porosity composes the two.
