# No exits (meta-clause)

**Law.** A commons of many minds needs the second closure:
every authority of judgment — checker, repairer, renderer, briefing,
decision — is substance on the same ledger it judges, at a strength it must
declare, under a lease it can lose. The one designed outside stands at the
human terminus. The five refusals — not an oracle, not a converger, not a
feed, not headless, not a museum — are this clause in negative: all five are
exit-shapes.

**Why it binds.** It is the constitution's generating clause: every
amendment in the destination exchange had the same form — a judge about to
stand outside gets pulled inside. The Gödel caveat still binds (no closure
completes; something must stand outside), so the necessary outside is placed
deliberately at the species that bears the risk. That placement is what
alignment is in this architecture: not a property proven about the system,
but a choice about where its necessary outside lives.
