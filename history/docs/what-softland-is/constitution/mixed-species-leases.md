# Mixed-species leases

**Law.** The two kinds of mind are constitutionally asymmetric, and the law
says so rather than discovering it in court. Authority terminates at humans:
humans originate ends and bear risk. Agent authority is always leased —
scoped, budgeted, expiring, revocable, on the record — and attenuates
monotonically down every delegation: no child lease wider than its parent.
Credit flows in lineages, down as attribution, up as stake.

**Why it binds.** Under this clause the oversight identity stops being an
aspiration and becomes bookkeeping: oversight is what the substrate does when
it's just running; seeing is the substrate's job, and governing — grant and
revocation of the leash — is the one clause that stays irreducibly human.
Monotone attenuation is what makes agents-spawning-agents safe: authority
only ever shrinks as it flows away from the human terminus. Reserved seat,
named honestly: how the terminus pluralizes arrives with the second human —
the system achieves full closure over every machine judge, provides record,
leases, and governance vocabularies among humans, and stays agnostic about
which politics any commons adopts.
