# Checkers and depth

**Law.** A commons moves at the speed of its cheapest trusted checker, and
check-strength is the depth budget each foundation publishes. Depth is
bought with fidelity — one budget, two corruption channels that add: base
drift, repaired by leased crews whose mends are independently checked or
break loudly; and check leakage, bounded by strength. Checker strength is
computed calibration — measured on the ledger, correlation-discounted, never
declared. Entry is by sponsorship; transitive trust exists only at soundness
or audited independence.

**Why it binds.** The poison is masquerade: an error-prone checker wearing
soundness's uniform mints trust that buys derivation depth the fidelity
cannot support — and declared strength just moves the masquerade up a level,
so strength must be computed from track record, where it becomes
self-extinguishing. Correlation is the other half: three checks from one
model family are approximately one check. And unchecked repair converts
visible breakage into invisible wrongness at machine speed — repair
throughput counts toward depth only multiplied by check-independence; when a
mend's check-confidence is low, the lawful outcome is to break loudly.
