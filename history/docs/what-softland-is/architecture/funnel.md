# Funnel

**Duty.** The one *logical* act envelope and decision protocol — physical
depots are family-owned (A11). A claimed gesture becomes a durable act at
the funnel's mouth; input dispatch (the courts) is distinct from the act
funnel. Every family's settlement rides it. Per-member accept/reject is what
the funnel means — individually refusable acts, per-request. It enforces the
write-side coexistence geometry
([convergence-is-an-event](../constitution/convergence-is-an-event.md)) and
refuses stale closures: a closer whose cited endpoint revisions have moved
is refused, the question stays open or reopens (A7).

**Seams.** Consumes claimed gestures from
[dispatch-and-courts](dispatch-and-courts.md) at the mouth — the gesture→act
seam A11 names. Carries the request envelope's causal legs. Fans
[set-acts](set-acts.md)' per-member acts. Serves settlement to every
family's containers; the publication decision-event rides it
([workshops-and-publication](workshops-and-publication.md)).

**Existence: exists** — as the logical envelope over family-owned depots,
which is exactly the split A11 legislates: "Instance modules own their
depots, topologies, PStates… This namespace keeps the common request/event
envelope" (`core.clj:5-9`); the envelope carries
`:causal {:correlation/id :intent/id}` (`core.clj:85`, `:150-186`). The
per-member machinery embryos (stale guards, counted batch receipts) are
banked with [set-acts](set-acts.md). Before A11 the words permitted one
global physical depot (Codex F11); the receipts never did.

**Open.** The exact gesture→act seam is named at law (the funnel's mouth);
its spec is contract-level work from A11's origin. The stale-closure refusal
(A7) has no implementation — the debt lifecycle it guards is
[debt-queue](debt-queue.md)'s missing organ.
