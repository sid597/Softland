# Set acts

**Duty.** Acts over sets, at three grains, all kept. The *intent* — query +
scope + verb, one signed act — answers why. The *resolution* — the frozen
membership at pinned basis, content-hashed, itself a claim with an asserter,
because resolving "everywhere this concept occurs" is sometimes mind-work —
answers what was meant. The *per-member acts* — individually refusable —
answer what happened. Preview renders the resolution. Commit references the
resolution hash; members whose lineage moved between preview and commit fail
individually on the stale guard with a named reason, never silently
re-resolved. Partial failure settles as per-member decisions plus a batch
receipt with counted outcomes. Rollback is a new set-valued act over the
accepted per-member act record (A5) — never a re-run of the query; members
that moved since surface as conflicts, not reverted. Heavy scopes escalate
through the cost rungs: synchronous-bounded → receipted background sweep as
a derived worker → refusal, every rung receipted.

**Seams.** Rides the [funnel](funnel.md)'s fan (per-member accept/reject).
The resolution is a lens output promoted to material by signing — K6 takes
it without strain (exchange-03 R4). Semantic resolutions ride

human-confirmed, then the mechanical splices run). The address-set noun
arrives from family selection verbs
([universal-verbs](universal-verbs.md)). Sweeps publish their progress as
substance.
