# Workshops and publication

**Duty.** The universal publication shape, and the place between subject and
world. Publication is one decision-event shape — signed, scoped, grounded,
rollback standing — owned by no medium; the workshop is the scope between
subject and world, where draft-ness lives as visibility scope on acts (a
draft placement, edge, or derivation is the same row at workshop scope, not
a new status). The cadence — quantum × darkness — is the place's dial. The
undo strata run here: recordless below the act quantum; profile-shaped in
the workshop; compensating-act-only above publication. At publish, the
dark-closure check runs over incrementally maintained direct holes — publish
the closure, land declared darkness as holes, or refuse with the named dark
dependencies; publication of a formerly-dark thing triggers the hole owner's
resolution transition. An occurrence query into a foreign workshop answers
nothing by default; count-only presence is the place's dial.

**Seams.** The decision event rides the [funnel](funnel.md). Settlement
profiles feed it from below ([citizenship-contract](citizenship-contract.md)).
Holes and their resolution transitions live in the
[relation-kernel](relation-kernel.md); the darkness pricing in the
[graph-economy](graph-economy.md). Every family's acts cross audience
boundaries through this one shape.

**Existence: embryo — complete exactly once.** The activation event runs for
the policy medium: signed, scoped, grounded, rollback standing; rollback
re-wears, never deletes or rewrites history; scope admits exactly two values
with the written note "richer scopes wait for a need that names itself"
(`activation_event.cljc:52`, `:59-64`) — the workshop scope is that need,
named, and is not yet in the form (open register). The container's
`visibility` field exists with no enforcement reader — a hook, not a working
system (`object_container.clj:105`, `:1603`; open register). `signed` is
attribution today: `valid-actor?` checks a nonblank label, the constructor
defaults to "system" (`activation_event.cljc:74`, `:99`, `:107-108`,
`:136-141`) — the word stays as the want; at n=1 attribution degenerates to
signature (Codex S1, ruled PARTIAL).
