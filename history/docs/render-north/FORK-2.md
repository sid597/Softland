## 1 · The question

Fork 2 (NORTH.md §10, deliberately left the founder's): does the gentle slope
break at the Rama/Electric/WebGPU seams — **one substrate or three?** In
plain form: is Softland one material viewed three ways, or three materials
glued at the edges? The three "ways" are the projection families: the
**world** (camera, dots, trails, continuous zoom), the **islands** (panels,
cards, the editor — box layout, text flow, clipping), and the **token face**
(what agents read).

## 2 · The ruling

**One substrate: three projection families over one address space.** One
scene store (keyed `(view-instance, address)` per the H6 birth law), one diff
pipeline, one camera loop, one spec grammar; world, island, and token faces
are projections over that single substrate. The seams are where projections
change, never where architectures change.

Sid's countersign, verbatim: **"ONE SUBSTRATE TO RULE THEM ALL"**.

## 3 · Grounds — the asymmetry the sitting was ruled on

The sitting reframed the fork at Sid's ask: not "which is cheaper" but *which
visions or bets become IMPOSSIBLE under each ruling*.

**Three-substrate foreclosures are vision-shaped and permanent** (impossible
by structure, not merely expensive):

1. "One land at every zoom" fails literally: the camera stops at the island
   border; the zoom-100 editor becomes a docked app, not bedrock (wall W4's
   warning realized).
2. Transclusion across seams becomes a synchronization protocol → eventually
   inconsistent → the map can lie about itself; the fan-out magic (sign in a
   room, the ground dot glows the same frame) dies at the seam.
3. The move-then-speak agent flow fragments: "what am I looking at" gets a
   stitched three-dialect answer; H2/H3 (handoff cost,
   tokens-to-orientation) measurably worsen.
4. Agent-authored on-the-fly UIs need two languages plus glue for any view
   that mixes families — and glue is where LLM reliability dies; model
   improvement compounds on one grammar, never on seams.
5. N1 (specs are assertions) is quietly readmitted-around: a DOM/Zed-shaped
   island engine makes the most-touched UI layer code-outside-the-log again
   — the rejected html-slop stack returning through the side door.
6. Self-hosting (Regime 2) loses its object: a third of Softland's own body
   becomes foreign material the land cannot address into; the
   code-not-as-files future loses its first native material (registry
   entries as addressable semantic units).
7. The modular-code requirement inverts: three hit-tests, three focus
   systems, three clip paths, plus glue — the retro's drift-bug class,
   institutionalized.

**One-substrate foreclosures are cost-shaped and dated** (recorded honestly):

1. UI maturity can never be bought off the shelf (no Monaco, no component
   libraries, no platform text stack). The text pit (H9) is ours alone; the
   "others can visit" vision (IME/CJK visitors) pays this fork's toll.
2. Foreign live surfaces (video players, arbitrary web apps) stay
   second-class — compositable as textured rectangles via a controlled seam,
   never native material. No current bet depends on them; the wish is named
   so it is on record at the ruling.
3. Schedule risk concentrates: one discipline gates everything; no
   just-use-a-div escape hatch ever exists.

Sharpener accepted at the sitting: the better-LLMs-in-8-months premise
shrinks one-substrate's main cost (building UI machinery against our own
forms gets cheaper every month) while doing nothing for seams — glue does
not get more honest with smarter models, only faster to write.

Modular-code note (Sid's standing requirement, named at the sitting): one
substrate ≠ one program. The composition ladder (NORTH §7) is the modularity
story — two thin registries of small, independently-addable entries,
everything else data; those registry entries are the natural first
experiment for code-as-addressed-material when the code-ingestor question
returns.

## 4 · Evidence on file at ruling time

- Both seams carry load without breaking: Rama→client has a verified diff
  protocol, now measured (PROBE-10K, 2026-07-05: 10⁴ elements viable at
  60fps for change/append/tail-shrink shapes, apply ≤4.2ms p95;
  permutation-shaped diffs must never carry the change signal — routed
  around by MECHANICS §2's order-as-data, so reorders travel as `:change`
  on rank fields).
- The reactive→GPU seam has a named boundary (the scene store) that mature
  systems independently converged on (WebRender's Scene, Rerun's store,
  Figma's retained tree).
- The editor already ships on the same five GPU pipelines the land needs —
  as-built evidence that the island family lives in the substrate.

## 5 · The alternative, recorded (the road not taken)

From NORTH §10 and the sitting: **three substrates at the seams** — the
island layer as its own engine (DOM- or Zed-shaped, inheriting a mature text
stack: IME, shaping, component libraries nearly free), the world layer as a
clean map engine, the token face as a separate serializer. Each layer
optimizes independently; failures isolate; UI maturity is bought, not built.
Its costs, as laid at the sitting: addresses stop at seams; transclusion
across seams becomes synchronization; the token face reads N structures or
drifts; three input systems with handoff protocols; collaboration and
versioning built per-substrate; the editor stops being *in* the land.

## 6 · Standing falsifier — the reopening clause

Pre-registered in NORTH §10, carried here unchanged: **if the island grammar
(editor-grade text interaction) ever demands renderer-level machinery the
world grammar cannot host — e.g. sub-pixel text shaping at arbitrary zoom
under IME — that is Fork 2 reopening with evidence.** Reopening requires that
form-break evidence; never a new argument, research round, or model opinion
(D-001 discipline applied to this ruling). H9's cost tag governs the
eyes-open entry into any such text machinery regardless of fork state.
