## The model

Two lines, braided:

- **world-line** — the succession of artifacts: code, docs, running things. Git already force-mints it (statement + parents at every commit).
- **sense-line** — what got asked, claimed, contested, decided, killed, opened — and on what grounds. Today it is *said in the river and evaporates after the session*.

An **episode** is one turn of the loop: tension → proposal(s) → deliberation → verdict-with-grounds → yield (what it settled, opened, killed). Feel-grounds ("it felt dead when I imagined using it") are first-class grounds. Sessions and files are **containers**; episodes are the **sense units**. Episode ≠ session: one session can hold several episodes; one episode can span weeks.

The symmetric diagnosis (Sid's): organizations have a lossy sense-trail and no world-trail; agent-era solo work has a total world-trail and an unminted sense-trail. Each has half. Softland is the join.

**The center finding** — arrived at independently ~5 times across two deliberately isolated tracks: *expensive failures live where nothing forced the mint; you can only cheaply kill what has been minted.* The 2026-07-07 dissolution was a refutation with no minted claim to land on. Git proves both halves of the lesson: forced minting works (commits are the only population that threads), and artifact-only minting is insufficient (`git log` still says nothing about *why*).

## The architecture bet (Sid's sketch, held at altitude)

Everything flows **through** the land — the medium is interposed between the human and every working agent. A reader in the medium proposes marks over the flow. Nobody ever stops to deposit — the no-ritual constraint: *what requires discipline will not happen* (evidence: the yield already gets said, and evaporates precisely because depositing is extra work).

- **Marks are proposals over an immutable log — never truth at write.** *Silver* = machine-proposed: total coverage, cheap, fallible, displayed as what it is. *Gold* = Sid-ratified or corrected. Sid's ordinary in-flow reactions ("love iittttt", "this is shit") are the ratification gestures — zero added ceremony. Moving-on raises a mark's *confidence*, never its *tier*: silence and signature stay visibly different facts. Corrections feed the marker's eval set — **the river generates its own benchmark.**
- **Two tempos, riding the zoom axis.** Cheap in-session marking at block grain, no interruption of flow; stronger consolidation at episode/day/week grain, reading *closed episodes*, not raw river. The consolidator is the management function — the org chart rebuilt out of models on clocks. Episodes close late; yield is only visible at closure.
- **Log-primary buys two properties:** taxonomy late-binds (re-marking is re-projection — query the labels, re-run a better model, nothing downstream breaks), and **the log appreciates** — every better model re-reads the entire past. The archive compounds instead of rotting. No organization has ever had this.
- **The return path is half the architecture.** The land must *brief*, not just remember: assembling the next prompt for any mind from the sense-line — the live tension, the standing verdicts, the dead-ends this idea resembles — at the altitude that mind works at. A briefing is where a lying map is most dangerous, because briefings tune minds; the register-pollution event *was* a mis-briefing performed by raw docs. Without the return path the sense-line is an archive; with it, it's the organization's working memory.
- **Views are standing answers to recurring questions** — *"the views are the answers to some of the questions."* Which questions recur is empirical, not imagined: the openers corpus (79 session-openers / 30 days) is their inventory. The most-repeated opener across four months — "read up on next-prompt and lets get started" — is the fossil of the missing morning answer.

## The flow, as lived (compressed)

Sit down → the morning answer is already current: what's live, what settled, what died, what's owed. Open a thread → the land drafts the briefing at the right level. Work → marks accrete silently — footprints, not a stenographer; big replies arrive pre-chunked into individually addressable claims. Hit a snag → the tension lands *on* the standing claim it collides with; overturns become oxbows with dates, never erasures. Get up → the slow pass closes the episode into proposals awaiting a glance, absorbing the baton ceremony. Weekly → a stronger mind writes the week's sense over closed episodes, every line drillable to the span that grounds it. Visitors → artifacts travel with their folded warrant; objections land on exact joints, preserved until synthesis is real.

Essence: **a one-person organization that remembers like an institution and moves like an individual — and, with many people, an organization that remembers like a single mind.**

## Fixed points

Held fixed in this direction; they move only by Sid's explicit word, or a used form breaking against them:

1. The sense-line drives; the world-line evidences.
2. Interposed, no-ritual capture — never ceremony, never discipline-dependent.
3. Marks as proposals; silver/gold strata visible; the map must not lie.
4. Two tempos on the zoom axis; episodes close late.
5. Log-primary ⇒ late-bound taxonomy ⇒ the log appreciates.
6. The return path (briefing) is half the point.
7. Labeling is emergence: description first, names earned by recurrence, Sid names.
