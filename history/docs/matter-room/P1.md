  mode wins when both are supplied and retains its pre-P1 canonical bytes.

  `:entity/found? true`, `:entity/id` equal to the master id, kind/facet/floor
  metadata, `:portal/errors []`, exactly one `:portal/masters` key, and all
  seventeen questions answered. Unknown masters remain total and answered.
- The JVM-only `matter-room/room-id` derives a UUID-v3-shaped address from
  exactly the UTF-8 master-id bytes. CLJS cannot derive one and consumes the
  served `:portal/room {room-id master-id}` mapping. The finite reverse table
  is server-authoritative.

  across sites; recipe wearers, wearer answers, and blast subjects are all
  filtered to wearers that actually carry the anchor master.

  distinct. Omission produces `:no-wearer-snapshot-at-anchor`, section and
  per-master `:blast/counted-over nil`, and a rendered basis row. A supplied
  current-scene snapshot may honestly report an evidence-backed zero.
- Entity-relative `*-here*` values use
  `:not-applicable-at-anchor`. The masters-card boolean reader now uses
  `true?`, so the truthy sentinel cannot mint “PINNED.”
- Experience is called with `[master-id]` and the explicit room
  `episode-object-key`; the explicit material ids/address survive a
  sub-serve fallback. The relation plan remains one batched roundtrip.
- The client exposes `await __portal.openMaster('fm:attention')` without
