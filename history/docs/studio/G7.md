## Line-by-line: the script vs what it was underneath

1. **"Draw a component"** — press-drag births a real durable block. But
   "component" was vocabulary over a lie: what was born was an ordinary
   block wearing a rectangle costume; no new component could exist.
2. **Tap + type** was the whole of "make it do something." The drawn
   frame and the block's native text were TWO bodies that never fused —
   text hung outside the frame; only the text line dragged.
3. **"Duplicate"** — sibling with copied bytes; no visible thread saying
   these are iterations OF something.
4. **"Change color"** — recolored the invisibly-selected draft
   (auto-follows newest; re-select was console-only). "Nothing happens"
   and "where does this attach" were one defect: the subject of every
   verb was invisible.
5. **"Test and accept / put back"** — a world-scale action as row four of
   a floating menu beside an empty rectangle. Never meaningfully reached.

The real use case the page served was the substrate's own: prove
draft→candidate→test→promote→rollback on a living surface. As a human
task: "restyle the default look of every block, safely" — an
administrator's act dressed as the creative one. The creative act he sat
down for — make a new thing — this surface could not do at all.

   ambient on the notes canvas. The pleasurable notes land is itself a
   revealed requirement to protect.
2. **Verbs before meaning** rejected regardless of what the verbs do;
   consequence and effort were inverted (world-scale accept one tap from
   an empty rect; making itself three impoverished verbs).
3. **Subject illegibility** — invisible selection; code-confirmed root of
   three of his four mysteries.
4. **Execution defects**: two-body specimen; a transient gesture ghost

5. **The script itself was substrate-shaped** — a lifecycle tour, no act
   he wanted. Even a defect-free build might have failed the morning.
   Future lived gates are cut from a task he already wanted that week.
6. **Untested, not rejected**: the variant field and the test-gated
   accept were never reached; the test-gate concept is Sid's own ruling
   and stands unjudged.
7. **Laws re-read under the gate**: law 1's two horns (pencil-in-world vs
