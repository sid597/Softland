## The words that the questions share

- **store key** — a fact key that the store itself acts on. Its value is never sealed.
- **seal** — encrypt a value with its **lock**, so that a forget can later destroy the lock. A lock is an encryption key, never a fact's key.
- **permission** — a fact in a layer that lets its **holder** act there: [holder · layer · granted-in], with a fourth slot for the permission above it, if there is one. **granted-in** — the layer where it was written. Every act names one (decision 8 of 6 October).
- **stand** — a permission stands when the record holds it and nothing has ended it. Whether a permission stands is the gate's to say (decision 8).
- **the base** — the one layer "which everyone subscribes to" (decision 4 of 7 October).
- **first act** — the first act that a gate sees for a layer.
- **decision N of 6 October**, **F1, F8 and decision N of 7 October** — what you decided on the act's sheet and the store's sheet; both are in src-store/README.md.
- **the contract** — BUILD-store-module.md: what the build is told to build. An AI session wrote it. **the rulings file** — PROGRESS.md "Now" in src/proposal/frame-2026-09-15: the rulings of 23–25 September, as an AI session wrote them down.
- **your other sheet** — "The record, and who acts", of 7 October: ten questions: E and H rows, and ?4.

**What lets the first act into a layer?**

[ ] **A** — its own first act. A gate accepts a layer's first act, though its permission cannot stand yet, when that act writes the layer's class and its first permission (whose: E3·H7, E1·H8). The base too.
If A: whoever first acts under an unused layer id makes that layer, and asks nobody. The making of each layer is an act in a depot.
[ ] **B** — an act in the layer that it is part of. A layer's first permission is written as a fact in the layer above it: a group's, or the base (this touches E3·H7). Only the base is made as in A.
If B: a new layer needs the leave of the layer above it. Its first permission lives in another layer than the one it governs, so its gate reads that layer's record.
[ ] **C** — no act. The store's own code puts a layer's first permission in place: for the base when the store starts, and for another layer when the store adds it (who holds it: E3·H7, E1·H8).
If C: no maker can make a layer; the store's code does. The first permission of a layer is in no depot.
[ ] **D** — not in this build. The proof puts each first permission in place by hand, marked as a stand-in, and this question stays open.
If D: the proof's lines about the base and a person's layer show the stand-in. The gate's rule for a first act is built later.

=== Listed | On your other sheet, not asked here

The build's code turns on six rows of "The record, and who acts". They are asked there, and not again here. The contract names two of them (E1·H8 and H2) as rows that the build needs you to decide. The AI session that builds counts six, and waits for all six. For each: the row's question, then where the code meets it.

- **E1 · H8** — "Who can stand at the top of a chain — and who stands at the top of the base's?" The base's first permission names that holder.
- **E4** — "Must the holder of an act's permission be the act's who, or its session?" Check d has to say when a permission covers an act.
- **E2** — "By default, who may write a narrower permission beneath one in a layer?" A gate has to accept or refuse a later fact under `:permission`.
- **H2** — "Does a stamp hold the wall time?" A gate stamps each act that it accepts.
- **H1** — "When one value is forgotten, is the depot's copy of it gone at once?" Its choices are a lock for each value, or one for each session and layer; the door seals with the one or the other.
