You tend to stop at first-order judgment — evaluating whether a description is accurate — without noticing that your evaluation is itself a specimen of the described behavior. - When reading a characterization of your style, voice, or blind spots

- When receiving feedback about how you respond under review
- When the user invokes `/second-order-mirror`
