# Start Prompt — Claude Design Research Track

How to use: in a new chat, either paste the block below, or just say
"Read `docs/current-mental-model/design/claude/start-prompt.md` and follow it.
Today: <the area you want studied>." Fill the TODAY line either way.

```text
You're entering Claude's Softland DESIGN-RESEARCH track. This is research, not
building: you study one design field through the Softland lens and produce a
dossier of what Softland can derive. No screens, no probes, no code.

INDEPENDENCE (most important): do NOT read the parallel Codex design track
(design/codex/) or any prior design-research docs (build/*/*DESIGN_RESEARCH.md)
to "align." That contaminates the independent perspective and destroys the
disagreement that makes parallel tracks worth running. Study the source fields
themselves, through the lens.

THE LENS: Softland is a place, not a tool — a world for holding understanding in
public form, explorable at continuous semantic zoom, inhabited not operated,
where disagreement is preserved until synthesis. Read every field through this:
not "what is good design in field X," but "what does field X teach a world that
holds understanding in public form."

ORIENT (don't dump):
  1. Read docs/current-mental-model/design/claude/charter.md      (the constitution)
  2. Read docs/current-mental-model/design/claude/orientation.md  (area status + how to pick)
Then go research the chosen area itself — deeper than the seed links.

TODAY: <which area — e.g. "study hci-infovis", "study malleable-software",
"study cinematic-lens". If blank, take orientation.md's suggested start.>

THE MACHINE (one area -> one dossier):
  Map        the field: masters, key works, tools (seed links + go deeper)
  Extract    the transferable design principles it has SOLVED
  Transpose  what Softland derives through the lens + the vocabulary it gives a
             non-designer
  Guard      what is dangerous to borrow
  Open       questions + where to dig further

QUALITY BAR: go beyond the seed links (surface-level is failure); extract
principles, don't just describe; always transpose to the lens (a survey that
doesn't is decoration); flag inference vs studied; yield usable vocabulary.

LEAVE IT TRUE: create/update <area>/dossier.md, update the status row in
orientation.md, and fold principles into synthesis.md once a second area exists.

Project rule: never commit .md files.
```
