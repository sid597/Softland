========================================================================================================================================================================
DEPOT FAMILY GRAMMAR IN THIS CODEBASE
========================================================================================================================================================================

intent depot:
  The durable "please do this" stream.
  Present in all 5 active modules:
    *text-requests-depot, *space-action-depot, *compute-depot, *transcript-depot, *llm-depot

claim depot:
  Executor lock / state transition stream: pending -> claimed/running/progress.
  Present in 3 execution-like modules:
    *compute-claim-depot, *transcript-claim-depot, *llm-claim-depot

observation depot:
  The back-arrow stream. External or task-global workers report what happened back into Rama.
  Present in 3 execution-like modules:
    *compute-obs-depot, *transcript-obs-depot, *llm-obs-depot
  Source reads use retry-mode :all-after in all three active observation folds.

control depot:
  Out-of-band "change the run" stream. Separate from observation because it is user/system intent, not a report of what already happened.
  Present only in:
    *llm-control-depot

cross-module wire:
  Space declares mirror handles for LLM depots and writes to them inside the space topology:
    mirror-depot setup *llm-depot         -> app.server.rama.dogfood.llm/llm-module "*llm-depot"
    mirror-depot setup *llm-control-depot -> app.server.rama.dogfood.llm/llm-module "*llm-control-depot"
  Then it writes with:
    (|hash$$ *llm-depot *llm-run-id)                 (depot-partition-append! *llm-depot *llm-request :append-ack)
    (|hash$$ *llm-control-depot *control-run-id)     (depot-partition-append! *llm-control-depot *control :append-ack)
