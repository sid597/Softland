## 1. The convergence — five tracks, one center, one disagreement

Read together for the first time, the tracks agree on far more than their
authors could have known:

| Track | Its own words | The shared center |
|---|---|---|
| Settlement (this session) | "a settlement, not a globe"; candidates judged in context; geography grown | locality, trust, calibration, ratification |
| Codex view-track | "The world is entered locally"; "the first serious view is probably not the global knowledge map"; views as epistemic instruments | same |
| Imported-topology research | "This is not a graph problem, it is a trust problem"; trust inspector before topology; Five Laws | same |
| Giants program (deep research) | ZUI graveyard, "zooming is hard unless the data is fundamentally spatial", incremental formalization as the only escape, "the traversal is a first-class artifact" | same |
| Kernel creed (context-map) | "DerivedUnit graduates on touch"; ActionRequest asks / Decision answers / Event happened; back-arrow | same, in code |

The **one genuine disagreement** is the knowledge-earth: the CT research
commits to a six-level continuous spatial ZUI (0.001 Knowledge Earth → 100
Source/Bedrock); the charter even bakes "continuous semantic zoom" into its
lens. Meanwhile the giants deep-research — the same program's own evidence arm —
documents the fifty-year failure of automated semantic zoom, Bederson's
post-mortem, Google Earth as brute-force precomputed tiles, and names the risk
"it will be Prezi at planetary scale." The corpus disagrees with itself, and
the evidence side of the corpus agrees with the settlement.

**Resolution (the main act of this synthesis): keep the knowledge-earth's laws,
retire its camera.** What the CT doc actually contributes is not the globe — it
is the *lawfulness machinery*:

- **Zoom maps must be functors** preserving six invariants: identity, relation,
  confidence, time, agency, **open questions**. This is a falsifiable test
  distinguishing real semantic zoom from page-flipping — and it transfers
  intact to the derivation axis (strata), where geometry is *given* (a stratum
  is made-from the floor below, with provenance) rather than inferred.
- **Conflict as non-commuting diagram** — two paths that should agree and
  don't, with the divergence point, assumptions, and repair evidence navigable.
  This is the structural form of "conflicts are good for progress."
- **Synthesis as colimit, not summary** — a constructed object with inputs,
  gluing decisions, discarded assumptions, preserved conflicts, and seams. The
  LLM-track's multi-parent reconciliation already implements the shape.
- **Context as fibration** — context changes what operations are valid; the
  same object affords different actions in different rooms.
- **Papers as morphisms** (before-model → transformation trail → after-model) —
  the categorical form of "a paper is a curated room over a trail."

The six zoom levels survive **as strata, not camera distances**: Knowledge
Earth / Domain Atlas / Topic / Local World / Model / Source is a derivation
ladder — each level LLM-drafted from the level below, anchored, calibrated,
ratified — not a continuous spatial surface. The "Knowledge Earth at 0.001"
visual arrives late, as grown geography and frozen census instruments, exactly
as the settlement prescribes. One correction the settlement makes that the CT
doc *needs*: it treats AIs as peer map-builders with **no ratification
mechanism anywhere** — which contradicts the kernel's own creed. Agents
propose; inhabitants ratify; that asymmetry is what keeps the map honest.

And one correction running the other way: the CT doc's invariant list includes
**"open questions" among what zoom must preserve** — a law the settlement did
not have and adopts, because it fuses with the frontier research below into
this synthesis's single most important new law (§4).

## 2. The social claim, under the graveyard pattern

Sid's scale-up — "not a single person maintaining anything... a vibrant
ecosystem of humans and agents" — is the claim every predecessor died on. The
research pass found the deaths cluster on a small set of structural variables,
with brutal numbers:

**The graveyard** (all gated publication on prior ratification by a privileged
group): Nupedia — seven-step expert review, ~24 articles in three years, while
its own side project produced ~20,000 in one. Citizendium — expert gate
approved <1% of its own articles. Scholarpedia — curator sign-off per edit,
seven articles in 2024. Cyc — ~$200M and ~2,000 person-years of staff-ratified
assertions, "the knowledge pump never primed." Mundaneum — staff-authored,
centrally classified, killed by one government's withdrawal. Semantic Web as
universal program — up-front ontology, authoring burden with no immediate
payoff, survives only as niche plumbing.

**The survivors** (publish first, repair after): Wikipedia — unit = the live
edit; watchlists and recent-changes make post-hoc repair scale. OSM — 1.5M
mappers, instant-live changesets, folksonomy tags documented post-hoc.
Wikidata — community refused a funder rather than surrender schema control;
conflicting statements coexist with references and ranks ("verifiability over
truth"); absorbed Freebase when Google conceded.

Six survivor properties, and the settlement formula maps onto them 1:1:

| Survivor property (evidence) | Softland mechanism |
|---|---|
| Tiny contribution unit, instantly live | ActionRequest → accepted event; ObjectContainer atomicity; marks land live |
| Ratify after, never before — repair via cheap watching | **Judgment changes calibration, never visibility** (see law below); rooms as watchlists; read-wear; trail diffs |
| Stable addresses precondition watching | ObjectContainer identity = the QID; "recomputed projections can be read but never held" — the citation-map failure is the inferred-layout failure again |
| Schema grown by use, documented post-hoc | Folksonomy edge/kinds with paved-path documentation; grown geography is the spatial special case of this general law |
| Plurality stored structurally | Preserved disagreement; Wikidata's ranks ARE calibration states; multi-parent reconciliation (implemented in LLM track) |
| No single kill switch | Open governance fork — Sid's decision (§7); Rama self-hosting makes federation structurally possible |

**The precision this forces on the substrate's language.** "Candidates until
accepted" must never be read as a publication gate — that reading is Nupedia,
and Nupedia is the graveyard. The correct reading, which the spec supports:
candidates are *published immediately* — visible, addressable, usable — and
judgment transitions their **calibration status**, not their existence.
Wikidata's deprecated/preferred ranks, not Citizendium's approval queue.

Combined with the alert-fatigue evidence (volume kills judgment; repeats are
auto-overridden), this yields a new two-sided law the session's evidence
demanded from both directions:

> **The asymmetric admission law.** Human contributions land live and ungated,
> carrying calibration marks. Machine proposals enter ranked, capped, and in
> context — and a judged-rejected proposal never returns unchanged. Gate
> humans and the map starves (graveyard). Flood humans with machine candidates
> and judgment collapses into heuristics (fatigue data). The Wikipedia
> precedent for the machine side already exists: bot policy.

Honest caveats the analyst flagged, kept: only ~3 survivors (six shared
properties cannot be statistically isolated); and OSM's ground truth — "go
look at the street" — has no full epistemic analogue. Softland's partial
analogues: the SourceAnchor ("go look at the source," checkable by anyone —
which is what makes ratification parallelizable by non-experts) and
replication-record edges. Wikidata is the proof that claim-shaped content can
still survive on verifiability-over-truth; contested synthesis remains harder
than streets, and that residual is real.

## 3. The interface claim at scale — strata on machinery that exists

The layer/vantage model (zoom over LLM-derived strata; aggregate vs translate
operators; vantage = altitude + aspect + task) lands on an LLM track that is
already a green-tested MVP. The reuse map, from the implemented contracts:

- **A stratum is generated by a dedicated LLM run** whose frozen ContextBundle
  — immutable, content-hashed source refs, exact rendered input — *is* the
  stratum's provenance certificate: it cites precisely what it summarized, by
  construction.
- **The output enters as a proposal** (the patch/note proposal-decision
  pattern, invariant "tool approval ≠ world acceptance"), so strata are
  candidates-until-ratified with zero new architecture.
- **Ratification-by-touch is already implemented** as lazy catalog promotion:
  raw material earns a citable identity when cited, sliced, commented,
  derived, or bookmarked — atomically with the touching action.
- **Drift detection is free**: slice contracts carry span + source
  content-hash + snapshot, surviving edits and compaction; a stale stratum is
  detectable without being destroyed.
- **The compaction model is the precedent**: summary above, full trail
  preserved beneath, marker visible. A stratum generalizes compaction from
  context-windows to understanding.
- **Two small additions needed**: a `:stratum-of` / `:summarizes` edge type in
  the typed artifact graph; and arrangement-as-WorldTurn (one request-type
  row) so authored room arrangement becomes durable memory.
- **One contract decision**: machine-derived-then-ratified content needs an
  authorship role — current derivatives are contractually user-authored. The
  cheap path (stratum = raw items of a summarization run + proposal cycle)
  reuses everything at the cost of a run per stratum; cost rollups for exactly
  this already exist per-thread.

The strata preservation contract, merging the CT invariants with the session's
evidence: every stratum map preserves **identity** (anchors), **relation**
(edge images), **confidence** (a stratum's calibration is bounded by its
sources' — a summary may never be more certain than what it summarizes),
**time** (staleness visible), **agency** (who/what derived it), and **open
questions** (§4). Aggregate (same vocabulary, coarser) and translate (another
consumer's vocabulary) are distinct operators and are labeled as such — the
CEO's altitude-2 and the engineer's altitude-2 are different *aspects*, and
personal assistants are translate-operators bound to a person, while the
default map AI is the aggregate-operator bound to a stratum.

## 4. The epistemic claim — the question is the product

Sid's organizing question — known knowns / known unknowns / unknown unknowns —
turns out to sit on a genuine structural white space. The frontier research
pass found:

- Every system that ever converted unknown-unknowns into known-unknowns **at
  scale** made absence *computable over a structured substrate*: a normalized
  entity layer + a corpus pipeline + the gap stored as a **first-class,
  addressable object with status and provenance**. Swanson's ABC pattern (A
  links B, B links C, A–C never asked) computes candidate unknowns from
  structure; CiteSpace detects research fronts as temporal anomalies; Hilbert's
  numbered problems organized a century *because each unknown had a stable
  address*; the replication databases made absence-of-confirmation a durable
  record only by deliberate schema-building.
- **Blank space does not work.** The Atlas-of-Science tradition renders only
  known-knowns; gaps as negative space are invisible, unaddressable,
  uninhabitable. Do not "display the frontier" — **reify it**.
- LLMs newly extract *implicit* gaps from unstructured prose (the
  GAPMAP/SciMON generation) — the same authoring-wall collapse as everywhere
  else — but generation now vastly outruns validation, so any gap-surfacing
  system must rank and triage or it floods its inhabitants. (The asymmetric
  admission law again, derived a third independent way.)
- **Nobody stores the open question as a first-class object.** ORKG models
  completed contributions; nanopublications model assertions; the scholarly
  graph for *questions* is unbuilt. Softland's protocol literally begins with
  Q. The analyst's words: "a real research gap and a defensible product
  wedge."

So the three epistemic states are projections of the discourse graph:

- **Known knowns** — ratified claims with evidence trails (calibration: fact).
- **Known unknowns** — Question objects: first-class citizens with identity,
  provenance ("what implied this question"), status (open / under-attack /
  partially-resolved / reversed), and edges to the structure that implies them.
  Plus preserved disagreements — the D of the protocol.
- **Unknown unknowns** — unlistable by definition; *manufactured into known
  unknowns* by situators running Swanson-pattern and gap-extraction passes,
  whose outputs are ranked candidate Questions, judged in context. Fog-of-war
  renders coverage honesty (where no one has looked); reified Questions carry
  the actionable frontier.

And the law that fuses this with §1 and §3 — the session's single most
important new sentence:

> **No projection may compress away the question marks.** Open questions are
> among the invariants every zoom map and every stratum must preserve. At
> every altitude, the unresolved is visible. A summary that drops the open
> questions is the lying map.

This is also what makes Softland's map different in kind from Wikipedia: the
encyclopedia's unit of value is the settled article; Softland's unit of value
is the **well-posed question with its evidence neighborhood** — "we judge the
value of science by the ignorance it defines" (Firestein). The metascience
vantage ("what is now unlocked to build on") is then a stratum over Question
objects and their resolution events — and the builders' loop is the same
kernel one floor down: the compute track and the knowledge track are strata
families over one event-sourced world, requests flowing down, capability
flowing up.

## 5. The merged law set

Consolidated from all tracks and both research passes; provenance per law.

1. **The log is truth; every view is a deterministic projection.** (Convergent
   attractor — independently built by four traditions; implemented.)
2. **Identity survives projection; stable addresses everywhere.** (Five Laws;
   Data Mountain; QIDs; "projections can be read but never held" otherwise.)
3. **Publication is never gated; judgment changes calibration, not
   visibility.** (Graveyard pattern.)
4. **Asymmetric admission: humans land live; machines enter ranked, capped,
   in context; rejected proposals never return unchanged.** (Graveyard +
   alert-fatigue + frontier validation-bottleneck — three independent
   derivations.)
5. **Structure is suggested, never demanded: one-gesture confirm, forever
   deferrable, tailorable after.** (Incremental formalization — the only
   escape the 70-year argumentation lineage left standing.)
6. **Geography is grown: arrangement is data, layout is a candidate, and
   ratify-by-touch must cost approximately nothing** — Bederson's
   user-maintained-convention cost is the named risk bound. (Spatial
   hypertext + ZUI post-mortem.)
7. **Zoom is lawful only on given axes — time and derivation — and every zoom
   map is a functor preserving identity, relation, confidence, time, agency,
   open questions.** (Settlement + CT track, merged; cartographic operations
   select/simplify/aggregate/displace are the how.)
8. **No projection may compress away the question marks.** (CT invariant +
   frontier research, fused.)
9. **The question is a first-class object** with identity, provenance,
   status, and edges. (Frontier white space; the protocol's Q.)
10. **Strata are runs**: frozen ContextBundle as provenance certificate;
    calibration bounded by sources; proposals until ratified; staleness
    marked, never silent. (LLM-track contracts + provenance-summarization
    evidence: machine drafts ~66%, humans supply the why.)
11. **Trust before topology; the world is entered locally.** One object's
    source-thread must work before any map. (Imported-topology + Codex Law 5.)
12. **Uncertainty never masquerades as truth; truth-states encoded
    redundantly, never color alone.** (Five Laws; ~13 truth-states already
    enumerated.)
13. **Watching must be cheaper than contributing.** Rooms are watchlists;
    presence and read-wear are visible; this is what makes ratify-after scale.
    (Survivor mechanics.)
14. **Fold, never sever.** (Recoverable compression — everywhere.)
15. **The half-built map must already be the most useful tool of its own
    builders.** (The survivors' hidden property; the giants program's central
    wager; the dogfood direction's reason.)

## 6. The path — unchanged, strengthened, extended

The scale-up does not change the build order; it confirms it from three more
directions (the giants digest independently chose "History/causal-braid sketch
first"; the imported-topology research independently ordered Trust Inspector →
scoped explorer → local topology; the survivors' law 15 demands dogfooding).

1. **Trust Inspector first** (imported-topology prototype 1): one imported
   object → identity, source span, transformation, relations, truth-state —
   the braid's honesty spine is this instrument. Capture requirements flow
   backward into ingestors NOW: commit-metadata lane, decision joins,
   claim provenance — no capture, no braid.
2. **The braid** ("How Softland Got Here"): three lanes, time-zoom,
   cross-lane candidates judged in place. The felt test stands as the
   experiment on the central under-evidenced claim.
3. **Strata over the braid**: first summarization runs through the
   proposal-ratify loop; then the first **Question objects** — "what is
   unresolved in this project" — Softland's own known-unknowns, the frontier
   primitive demonstrated on the corpus where its truth can be felt. (§7 of
   the project's docs already maintains open-pressure lists by hand; this
   slice makes the hand-list a projection.)
4. **The DG corpus** (Roam import): questions-as-objects native to the
   protocol; claims-and-people; the translate operator earns its keep
   (different consumers, same floor).
5. **Multi-mind**: share a room; watchlist mechanics; asymmetric admission
   live with a second human; agents already populate the presence layer from
   step 1.

Each ring adds one social scale; the loop is identical at every ring — which
is Sid's fractal claim, earned the only way it can be: by the smallest ring
working first.

## 7. Open forks and the decisions only Sid can make

Carried forward, now with owners:

1. **Governance / kill-switch** (new, from the graveyard): survivors put
   ownership in a foundation + community whose members are the primary
   consumers. Mundaneum, Freebase, and Cyc died of single owners. When
   Softland goes multi-mind, what owns the canonical world? *Rec:* design for
   federation-readiness now (Rama self-hosting helps), decide governance at
   ring 5, not before. *Cost of late:* low until multi-mind; existential after.
2. **One substrate or three** (Fork 2, still the load-bearing technical
   question): does the gentle slope break at the Rama/Electric/WebGPU seams?
   Untouched by everything above.
3. **The synthesis trigger** (Fork 4): candidates-until-ratified preserves
   plurality; the colimit mechanism that turns N preserved perspectives into
   a new artifact — and *when it fires* — remains undesigned. The CT doc's
   colimit-with-seams is the best available shape.
4. **The rediscovery dial**: the deskilling RCT vs structure-transfer results
   pull opposite ways; where each inhabitant sets do-the-work vs
   build-on-structure cannot be a global constant.
5. **Doorway scent** (the literature's hardest open problem, inherited): how a
   room or stratum honestly advertises its epistemic contents — value AND
   calibration — before entry. Unsolved everywhere; Softland needs it at
   every door.
6. **Measurement**: "the braid reduces private reconstruction cost" wants a
   falsifiable head-to-head (vs prose+git) or it reproduces the QWAN problem —
   every field's inability to measure its own success.

Strongest attacks on this synthesis, answered honestly: *(a) five-track
convergence might be shared-author contamination, not independence* — partially
true (Claude instances + one user), which is why §2 and §4 rest on external
evidence (graveyard numbers, frontier infrastructure) that no internal track
produced. *(b) OSM's ground truth has no epistemic analogue* — conceded as
residual risk; SourceAnchors + verifiability-over-truth are the partial
analogue, Wikidata the partial proof. *(c) strata economics unproven* — every
stratum costs a run; derive on semantic events, bound by cost rollups; the
wager is that LLM cost collapse holds. *(d) asymmetric admission could
second-class the agents* — their work lands live as raw material; only their
*structural claims* enter as proposals, which is exactly Wikipedia's bot
policy, and it is the agents' own trails that populate presence from day one.
