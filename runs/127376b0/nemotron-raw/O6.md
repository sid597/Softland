**North** is defined as *Sid’s commitment* – the projected destination of the Softland project, written as a “projection” that is taken verbatim from **`vision/LOG.md`** (Sid’s own words, append‑only) and captured in the documentation [CLAUDE.md → “North — Sid's commitment”] and repeated in **[BETS.md → “North — Sid's commitment”]**.  

It describes a *land made of software* that is “a place you inhabit, not a tool you use,” a world for holding understanding in public form, with trails that don’t fade, a map that doesn’t lie, and an ecosystem of humans and agents on multiple layers.  

Because it is a **commitment** (a projection) rather than a testable hypothesis, it **has no kill‑conditions**. Kill‑conditions are defined only for *bets* (the route hypotheses in the ladder), not for the North projection itself.
