The binding decision log `docs/current-mental-model/decisions.md` governs.  
Its “Operating rules” section states that a decision is *closed* only when Sid countersigns it, and that a closed decision can only be reopened by new evidence from a used form breaking against it—not by a later‑written plan or baton. The `next‑prompt.md` file is a mutable work‑session placeholder; when it conflicts with a closed decision, the conflict must be resolved by revisiting the decision (or by amending the decision through the formal re‑opening process). In short, **decisions.md takes precedence**; the baton must be adjusted to align with the established decision, not the other way around.
