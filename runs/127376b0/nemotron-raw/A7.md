**Precedence hierarchy (as documented)**  

1. **Decision log – `docs/current-mental-model/decisions.md`** – *the binding decision log* (see **CLAUDE.md → “Decision Log — BINDING”**).  
   - It is the *supreme* authority: every architectural, scope, or sequencing decision must be read from this file before any change.  
   - A decision is *PROPOSED* until Sid countersigns it; once **CLOSED**, it may be reopened **only** when a *used form* breaks against it (never on a new argument).  
   - Consequently, any contract or baton that would conflict with a closed decision is *not permitted*.

2. **Binding contract(s)** – e.g. `docs/current-mental-model/build/git-spine/CONTRACT.md`, `docs/current-mental-model/build/trail-view/CONTRACT.md`, etc.  
   - These files are marked **“BINDING”** and are *countersigned by Sid*, but their binding force is *contingent* on not overturning a closed decision.  
   - The contract can be amended only through the process described in the decision log (i.e., a new proposal, evidence, and Sid’s countersign).  

3. **Baton / hand‑off file – `docs/sessions/next-prompt.md`**  
   - This file is the *active work handoff* and is **not** a binding decision; it merely records the next session’s prompt.  
   - It carries no authority to override the decision log or any binding contract.

**In short:**  
`decisions.md` > binding contract > baton (next‑prompt). Any amendment to a contract or progression to a new baton must first respect the current state of the decision log; a conflict with a closed decision is prohibited.  

*References:*  
- **CLAUDE.md**, “Decision Log — BINDING” (states that the decision log is the binding decision log and must be read before proposing or discussing architecture, scope, or sequencing).  
- **Contract files** (e.g., `build/git-spine/CONTRACT.md`) – marked “BINDING” and countersigned by Sid, but their effect is subordinate to the decisions logged in `decisions.md`.  
- **Baton file** (`docs/sessions/next-prompt.md`) – described only as the *active work handoff*, with no binding status.
