**Is North a hypothesis with kill-conditions?** No.

From `docs/current-mental-model/BETS.md`, section "Authority rules":
> "**North** is Sid's commitment — where this project is going, against all odds. It is NOT a hypothesis: no kill-conditions, no evidence requirement. Only Sid rewrites it."

Kill-conditions and pre-registered evidence apply only to **Bets** (route hypotheses toward North), not to North itself: "**Bets** are route hypotheses toward North. Anyone may propose one; only pre-registered evidence may kill or confirm one. ... Killing a bet never touches North — it demands a better route." North also sits at the slowest, founder-owned pace layer: "Pace layers: North (slowest, founder-owned) → bets (evidence-paced) → work packages (contract/gate-paced...)."

**What North commits to**, per the "North — Sid's commitment" section of the same file (drafted by Fable from Sid's own words, expanded 2026-07-03):

- "Softland is a land made of software — a place you inhabit, not a tool you use. A world for holding understanding in public form: trails that don't fade, a map that doesn't lie, one land at every zoom from the knowledge landscape down to the code that renders it — visitable by other minds, human and machine, and renovatable by its inhabitants down to bedrock. It should exist even if the route is long: first the wall made alive, then a settlement, then a world."
- **The question the land exists to answer, about any xyz:** "what are the known knowns, the known unknowns — and where do the unknown unknowns announce themselves?" Rendered as a "knowledge earth": zoomed out, topics are nations with visible borders/connections; zoomed all the way in, the ground is made of questions, claims, evidence, sources — "Every zoom level is a level of understanding, with AI holding the context of that level ... Symbiotic, not AI-dependent: machines carry the boring weight; humans at every level of understanding explore and contribute at theirs."
- **Not one person's tool:** "a vibrant ecosystem of humans and agents on different layers — Rama the scalable ground truth, Electric the reactive tissue, WebGPU the visible world ... LLMs and humans in symbiosis. A social network for collective intelligence."
- **Why it must exist:** "the map humanity has of what it knows is fragmented, hard to evaluate, and loses track of its own progress. Sid wants a reasonable attack on the outcome of **humans living healthy forever** — the map is the instrument; longevity is the first territory it must serve."
