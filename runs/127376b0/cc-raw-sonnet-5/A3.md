**No.** A CLOSED decision cannot be reopened by a new argument or a research round.

Per `docs/current-mental-model/decisions.md` (Operating rules, top of file):
> "A CLOSED decision may only be reopened by **new evidence from a used form breaking against it** — never by a new argument, research round, or model opinion."

This is corroborated by `CLAUDE.md` (## Decision Log — BINDING):
> "CLOSED decisions are settled: reopening requires evidence of a *used form breaking against them* — never a new argument, research round, or model opinion."

And restated in `CLAUDE.md` (### Behavioral Rules):
> "Don't re-litigate closed decisions. If a discussion drifts toward reopening something CLOSED in `docs/current-mental-model/decisions.md`, name it, cite the decision, and ask for form-break evidence instead of arguing the merits again."

So the only valid path to reopening a CLOSED decision is evidence that the decided form actually broke in daily/actual use — a new argument, a new research round, or a model's opinion is explicitly insufficient.
