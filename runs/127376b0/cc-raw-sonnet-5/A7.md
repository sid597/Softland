## Question
What is the precedence order among binding docs for a build package (contract vs baton vs decision log)?

## Answer

The material states the ordering explicitly and repeatedly, and it is unambiguous: the **baton (`docs/sessions/next-prompt.md`) is subordinate to both the CONTRACT and the decision log**, which are the two binding sources; the baton is never a source of truth in its own right.

**1. Baton is explicitly the lowest tier — "never outranks contract or log":**
- `docs/current-mental-model/build/relation-kernel/RETRO.md`, section "Mechanisms that earned their keep": "STANDING/NOW split with the **precedence rule** (baton never outranks contract/log) — it absorbed the '10 vs 11 gates' drift without a session even pausing."
- `docs/current-mental-model/decisions.md`, "Open questions queued for ruling" (succession-document spec, item 2): "how to open a work package — contract with traps ledger + acceptance gates + input manifest, next-prompt.md STANDING/NOW baton, **precedence rule (baton never outranks contract or log)**."
- `docs/sessions/next-prompt.md`, STANDING section for both active packages (trail-view WP1 and view-MVP WP-B2), verbatim in each: "This file is a baton, not a source of truth; **if it contradicts CONTRACT.md or decisions.md, those win** — flag the discrepancy in NOW."
- `docs/sessions/next-prompt.md`, NOW entry (2026-07-05, Track A): "STANDING's 'one phase per FRESH session' line is superseded (**precedence: decisions.md wins**; STANDING left unedited per protocol)."

**2. Contract and decision log are both binding, and conflicts between them are resolved through the decision log:** every CONTRACT's stop clause (e.g. trail-view CONTRACT.md §12, git-spine CONTRACT.md §9) directs that a genuine binding-doc conflict be classified (implementer-fixable vs policy fork) and **escalated to `decisions.md` Open Questions as PROPOSED** — "Never improvise policy." This makes `decisions.md` the place where any unresolved contract-vs-other-binding-doc tension is finally adjudicated and recorded (countersigned by Sid to CLOSE it), i.e. it functions as the terminal arbiter in the hierarchy. Consistent with this, `decisions.md`'s own header states it "governs closures," and CLAUDE.md's Decision Log section calls it "the binding decision log ... Read it before proposing or discussing architecture, scope, or sequencing."

**Resulting order (highest to lowest authority):**
1. **Decision log** (`docs/current-mental-model/decisions.md`) — binding; the log to which contract/binding-doc conflicts (policy forks) are escalated and ruled.
2. **Contract** (`build/<package>/CONTRACT.md`, once Sid-countersigned) — binding; implements/derives from decisions.md rulings.
3. **Baton** (`docs/sessions/next-prompt.md`, STANDING/NOW) — not a source of truth; purely a handoff/orientation record; explicitly "never outranks contract or log," and yields to either on contradiction.
