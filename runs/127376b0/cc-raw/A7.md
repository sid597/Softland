# Answer

**Precedence order (highest → lowest): the decision log (`decisions.md`) → the contract (`CONTRACT.md`) → the baton (`docs/sessions/next-prompt.md`, its STANDING/NOW entries).**

The material states this as a two-tier rule plus a supremacy relation:

## 1. The baton never outranks the contract or the decision log (explicit rule)

- The precedence rule is named verbatim in `docs/current-mental-model/build/relation-kernel/RETRO.md` ("Mechanisms that earned their keep"): *"STANDING/NOW split with the **precedence rule** (baton never outranks contract/log) — it absorbed the '10 vs 11 gates' drift without a session even pausing."*
- Restated in `docs/current-mental-model/decisions.md` (Open questions queued for ruling → succession-document spec, item 2): *"how to open a work package — contract with traps ledger + acceptance gates + input manifest, next-prompt.md STANDING/NOW baton, **precedence rule (baton never outranks contract or log)**."*
- Operationalized in the baton itself. Both active-package STANDING blocks in `docs/sessions/next-prompt.md` say: *"This file is a baton, not a source of truth; if it contradicts CONTRACT.md or decisions.md, those win — flag the discrepancy in NOW."* (WP1 trail-view STANDING; identical clause in WP-B2 view-MVP STANDING.)

So the baton (STANDING/NOW in `next-prompt.md`) is the lowest tier; both `CONTRACT.md` and `decisions.md` outrank it.

## 2. Between contract and decision log, the decision log wins

- Worked example, `docs/sessions/next-prompt.md` (WP1 trail-view NOW, 2026-07-05 Fable entry): *"STANDING's 'one phase per FRESH session' line is superseded (**precedence: decisions.md wins**; STANDING left unedited per protocol)."* (This is a log-over-baton case, but it names the tie-breaker as `decisions.md`.)
- The conflict-resolution mechanism makes `decisions.md` supreme: every package stop clause routes genuine binding-doc conflicts *to* the decision log rather than resolving them locally. Trail-view `CONTRACT.md` §12 / `decisions.md`: *"binding-doc conflict → classify (implementer-fixable vs policy fork) → escalate policy forks to decisions.md Open Questions as PROPOSED. Never improvise on binding docs."* The git-spine `CONTRACT.md` §9 stop clause is the same, and it opens *"Binding together with `decisions.md`."*
- The precedent this created: the relation-kernel F2 idempotency conflict was escalated and **ruled in `decisions.md`, after which the CONTRACT was amended to match the ruling** (`decisions.md` D-006 evaluation notes, 2026-07-03; relation-kernel `RETRO.md`). Rulings amend contracts, not the reverse — so `decisions.md` sits above `CONTRACT.md`.
- CLAUDE.md frames `decisions.md` as *"the binding decision log,"* and CLOSED decisions can only be reopened by a used-form break — a contract cannot override them.

## Summary

1. **`decisions.md`** — the binding decision log; supreme authority; rulings drive contract amendments and are where binding-doc conflicts get resolved.
2. **`CONTRACT.md`** — the package's binding contract; outranks the baton; implements/binds together with the decision log.
3. **Baton (`docs/sessions/next-prompt.md`, STANDING/NOW)** — explicitly "not a source of truth"; never outranks the contract or the log; on contradiction, flag in NOW and defer.
