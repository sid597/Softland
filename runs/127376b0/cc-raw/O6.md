## What North commits to

North is **Sid's commitment — where the project is going, against all odds** (BETS.md, Authority rules). Its content (BETS.md, "North — Sid's commitment") is:

- **Softland as a land made of software** — "a place you inhabit, not a tool you use"; a world for holding understanding in public form: **trails that don't fade, a map that doesn't lie, one land at every zoom** (from the knowledge landscape down to the code that renders it), **visitable by other minds** (human and machine), and **renovatable by its inhabitants down to bedrock**.
- **A staged route it commits to existing even if the route is long:** "first the wall made alive, then a settlement, then a world."
- **The question the land exists to answer, about any xyz:** "what are the known knowns, the known unknowns — and where do the unknown unknowns announce themselves?" — rendered as a zoomable "knowledge earth" (topics as nations at zoom-out; questions/claims/evidence/sources as the ground at zoom-in), each visitor arriving with their own assistant, machines carrying the boring weight while humans explore and contribute at their level.
- **Not one person's tool:** a vibrant ecosystem / "social network for collective intelligence" — Rama as scalable ground truth, Electric as reactive tissue, WebGPU as the visible world, LLMs and humans in symbiosis.
- **Why it must exist / terminal purpose:** because humanity's map of what it knows is fragmented, hard to evaluate, and loses track of its own progress; Sid wants "a reasonable attack on the outcome of **humans living healthy forever** — the map is the instrument; longevity is the first territory it must serve."

## Is it a hypothesis with kill-conditions?

**No.** BETS.md, Authority rules, states this explicitly: North "is NOT a hypothesis: **no kill-conditions, no evidence requirement.** Only Sid rewrites it."

The kill/confirm machinery belongs to **bets**, not North: "Bets are route hypotheses toward North... only pre-registered evidence may kill or confirm one." And: "Killing a bet never touches North — it demands a better route." (BETS.md, Authority rules.) The reviews structure corroborates this split — the "evidence review" asks whether the active bet survives, while the "vision sitting" asks whether North has moved, and that is "Sid's authorship only."
