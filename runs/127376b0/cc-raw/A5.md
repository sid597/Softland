**Current decision statuses** live in `docs/current-mental-model/decisions.md` (the binding decision log). **The single 'now / current work' state** lives in `docs/sessions/next-prompt.md` (the active work handoff / baton).

(FILE: docs/current-mental-model/decisions.md, "Companion file" note; FILE: docs/current-mental-model/BETS.md, "The ladder")

## Summary of the split

- **Statuses of decisions** (PROPOSED/CLOSED) → `docs/current-mental-model/decisions.md` (binding).
- **Statuses of bets / direction** → `docs/current-mental-model/BETS.md` (companion, holds the one-ACTIVE-bet ladder).
- **The single "where are we right now / current work" state** → `docs/sessions/next-prompt.md` (the baton, not a source of truth — decisions.md and the CONTRACTs outrank it).
