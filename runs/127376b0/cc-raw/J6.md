# Answer to J6

**Question:** For the relation-kernel CONTRACT.md, which session produced it — and is this answerable today?

## Short answer

Yes, it is answerable from the provided material — but only at the granularity of **author + date**, not a unique session identifier. The relation-kernel CONTRACT.md was produced by **Fable, on 2026-07-03**, in what the material calls the "**contract session**." The material never assigns unique session IDs/names, so no finer-grained "which session" identity is derivable.

## Evidence (with citations)

1. **The contract's own header states its author and date.**
   From `docs/current-mental-model/build/relation-kernel/CONTRACT.md`, header line ("RelationEdge Contract — relation-kernel-module"):
   > "Status: DRAFT v1 (Fable, 2026-07-03). Implements decision D-004; serves D-002 (trail view) and D-005 (view-first sequencing)."

2. **The decision log names a distinct "contract session."**

   This explicitly identifies a Fable "contract session" as the producing session.

3. **The retro attributes authorship and package-opening to Fable.**
   From `docs/current-mental-model/build/relation-kernel/RETRO.md`, "The package in one paragraph":
   > "Fable wrote CONTRACT.md (D-004) and opened the package with a STANDING/NOW baton."

## Is it answerable today, and to what precision?

- **Answerable:** Yes — the material consistently attributes the relation-kernel CONTRACT.md to a **Fable session on 2026-07-03** (the "contract session"), corroborated across three files (CONTRACT.md header, decisions.md D-006 token ledger, RETRO.md).
- **Limit of the material:** Sessions in this snapshot are referred to only descriptively (e.g., "the contract session," "same session as the gate review," "the work-package/recheck session"). No session ID, session-log filename, or unique session name for the contract session is provided. So "which session" is derivable as *"the 2026-07-03 Fable contract session"* — but not as a uniquely-keyed identifier.
