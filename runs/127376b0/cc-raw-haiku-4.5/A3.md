# Answer to A3

**Question:** Can a CLOSED decision be reopened by a new argument or research round?

**Answer:** No.

## Source and Citation

From CLAUDE.md, "Decision Log — BINDING" section (opening paragraph):

> CLOSED decisions are settled: reopening requires evidence of a *used form breaking against them* — never a new argument, research round, or model opinion.

## Supporting Evidence

This principle is reinforced in the "Behavioral Rules" section of CLAUDE.md:

> Don't re-litigate closed decisions. If a discussion drifts toward reopening something CLOSED in `docs/current-mental-model/decisions.md`, name it, cite the decision, and ask for form-break evidence instead of arguing the merits again.

## Implication

The only grounds for reopening a CLOSED decision is evidence that a *used form is breaking against it* — meaning the decision, when applied in practice, has encountered a real failure in daily use. Theoretical arguments, new research, or further reasoning from AI models are explicitly insufficient to reopen a closed decision.
