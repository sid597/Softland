## The Pre-registered Conditions

### KILL Condition
Two weeks after the view first renders (relations + timeline working), new thinking panels still start on paper **AND** wall photos still get pasted into LLM chats.

*Grace clause:* Form-break fix cycles are the design (D-005), not failure — the clock measures *where new thinking starts*, not polish.

### CONFIRM Condition
The photograph-and-paste ritual is replaced by context bundles from the view; at least half of new panels/threads start inside Softland.

## Arming Event (Clock Start Condition)
The clock does NOT start at first render. It arms at an **event** (not a date): the first render showing ≥1 typed relation over real material — import-derived or sid-via-agent (D-008) both count.

This arming event exists to avoid ambiguous kills: starting the measurement when zero typed relations are visible would make it unclear whether form failure or missing nouns caused the kill, spending the window without buying evidence.
