# Answer to: What does North commit to, and is it a hypothesis with kill-conditions?

## What North commits to

Per `docs/current-mental-model/BETS.md`, North commits to:

1. **Softland as a land, not a tool**: "Softland is a land made of software — a place you inhabit, not a tool you use. A world for holding understanding in public form: trails that don't fade, a map that doesn't lie, one land at every zoom from the knowledge landscape down to the code that renders it — visitable by other minds, human and machine, and renovatable by its inhabitants down to bedrock." (BETS.md, §North — Sid's commitment)

2. **Answering the core epistemic question**: The land exists to answer "what are the known knowns, the known unknowns — and where do the unknown unknowns announce themselves?" (BETS.md, §North)

3. **A social substrate for collective intelligence**: "Not one person's tool: a vibrant ecosystem of humans and agents on different layers — Rama the scalable ground truth, Electric the reactive tissue, WebGPU the visible world [...], LLMs and humans in symbiosis. A social network for collective intelligence" (BETS.md, §North)

4. **Terminal purpose**: "Sid wants a reasonable attack on the outcome of **humans living healthy forever** — the map is the instrument; longevity is the first territory it must serve." (BETS.md, §North)

## Is it a hypothesis with kill-conditions?

**NO.** North is explicitly NOT a hypothesis. Per `docs/current-mental-model/BETS.md`, §Authority rules:

> "**North** is Sid's commitment — where this project is going, against all odds. It is NOT a hypothesis: no kill-conditions, no evidence requirement. Only Sid rewrites it." (BETS.md, lines 393-397)

By contrast, the project does operate with *Bets* (routes toward North) that ARE subject to pre-registered evidence and kill-conditions, but North itself stands independent of such conditions.
