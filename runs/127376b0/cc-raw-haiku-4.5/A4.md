## Who may rewrite North, and what evidence can kill it?

**Only Sid may rewrite North.**

From `docs/current-mental-model/BETS.md`, Authority rules section:

> "**North** is Sid's commitment — where this project is going, against all odds.
> It is NOT a hypothesis: no kill-conditions, no evidence requirement. Only Sid
> rewrites it. Visioning sessions are legitimate work; their output lands here.
> North is a *projection*: the verbatim source is `vision/LOG.md` (Sid's words,
> append-only) — capture there first, synthesize here second."

**No evidence can kill North.**

North is explicitly NOT subject to kill-conditions or evidence-based falsification. It is Sid's commitment, not a hypothesis. By the file's own statement, North has "no kill-conditions, no evidence requirement."

This contrasts sharply with Bets, which ARE evidence-paced and have pre-registered kill-conditions (per the same Authority rules section). North operates at a different layer: it is aspirational, not falsifiable.
