The **decisions.md** file is the governing authority.  
According to the operating rules in that file, decisions are *binding* once Sid countersigns them, and any conflict with a session‑log (such as `docs/sessions/next‑prompt.md`) must be resolved in favor of the decision. The session log is merely a record of work; it can be updated or corrected, but it does **not** override the binding decisions. If a conflict cannot be resolved from the existing evidence, it must be escalated for clarification (e.g., to Sid).  

**Citation:** `docs/current-mental-model/decisons.md` – “A decision is **PROPOSED** until Sid countersigns it… **CLOSED** decisions may only be reopened by new evidence from a used form breaking against it… Every decision records the evidence it rests on…”  

So, when `docs/sessions/next-pprompt.md` conflicts with `decisions.md`, **`decisions.md` governs**.
