**Answer**

The first time any markdown (including the Softland documentation) and the Claude/Codex transcript files were ingested into the **object‑container kernel** was during the **initial bootstrap sweep** that runs when the trail‑view runtime starts.  

- During that initial sweep the `ingest_watchers` future reads the directories listed in `:roots` – which, at boot time, are **`docs/current-mental-model`** and **`vision`**.  
- All files under those directories (Markdown, design docs, design‑converter sources, etc.) are processed by the markdown‑adapter and ingested into the object‑container depot.  
- **The large transcript corpus (the ~1.2 GB `transcript/` directory) is *not* ingested during this first sweep.** It is deliberately deferred; the ingest job will only pick it up later when the watcher reaches the path configured under `:transcript‑roots` (an “offset‑incremental re‑read” that occurs after the initial bootstrap).

Thus, the **time window** that first ingested the markdown files is the **initial boot‑time ingestion window** (the first call to `initial‑sweep!` → `start‑ingest‑watchers!`), while the **Claude/Codex transcript ingestion is deferred** and happens later when the watcher processes the `transcript` roots.
