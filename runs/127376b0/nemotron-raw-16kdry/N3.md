1. **Treat the contract and its input‑manifest as binding.**  
   - The contract is *countersigned* by Sid and marked **BINDING**.  
   - Any change to the factual premises (e.g., adding a new dependency, altering an idempotency rule, changing the scope of the idempotency key) must be **explicitly amended** in the contract and recorded as a new version.  
   - Unauthorised modifications are considered a violation of the “no‑edit‑without‑evidence” rule and must be escalated.

2. **All implementation decisions must be traceable to the listed sources.**  
   - Every gate, trap, and acceptance criterion in the contract must be satisfied by referencing the exact lines or sections from the listed documents.  
   - When a decision is made (e.g., “relation‑scoped idempotency”), the supporting evidence must be quoted from the relevant section of `CONTRACT.md` or the cited `INPUTS.md` file.

   - If a builder believes a rule must be relaxed (e.g., to satisfy a performance constraint), they must first **record the proposed change**, attach the supporting rationale, and obtain a formal amendment.  
   - Until that amendment is merged, the implementation must not proceed; otherwise the work is considered *non‑compliant* and must be rolled back.

   - The halt must be announced in the session log and the change must be tracked in the verdict log.

   - The *RETRO.md* file for the git‑spine package, the *GATE_REVIEW.md* artifacts, and the *DIFF_FALSIFICATION_* documents are considered **verified evidence** that the implementation meets the contract.
