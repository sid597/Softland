No. According to the operating rules in **`docs/current-mental-model/DECISIONS.md`**, a decision that has been marked **CLOSED** can only be reopened if **new evidence** appears that comes from a *used form* that breaks against the decision. Simply introducing a new argument or launching a new research round is not sufficient to reopen it.  

**Citation:**  
- *Operating rules* section of `docs/current-mental-model/DECSIONS.md` – “A CLOSED decision may **only** be reopened by **new evidence from a used form** breaking against it — **never** by a new argument, new research round, or model opinion.”
