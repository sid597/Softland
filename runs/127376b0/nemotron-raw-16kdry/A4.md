**Who may rewrite North?**  
Only **Sid** may rewrite the “North” section of `docs/current‑mental‑model/BETS.md`.  
The document states that *North* is “Sid’s commitment — where this project is
going, against all odds” and that “Only Sid rewrites it.”  All other actors may
propose changes, but any change to the North text must be authored by Sid
himself.  (See **BETS.md**, “Authority rules” section.)

**What kind of evidence can kill a bet (and thus force a rewrite of North)?**  
A bet may be killed only by **pre‑registered evidence that a used form breaks
against it**.  In other words, a bet survives only while there is concrete,
observable evidence that the current form (the current implementation or
process) is breaking the bet’s conditions.  If such evidence appears, the bet
can be terminated and a new bet (and possibly a revised North) may be adopted.
The rule is explicit:

> “Killing a bet never touches North — it **demands a better route**.”  
> “A bet is killed only by pre‑registered evidence of a used form breaking
> against it — never by a new argument, new research, or a model opinion.”

Thus, the evidence that can terminate a bet (and thereby force a reconsideration
of the North direction) must be:

1. **Empirical / observed** – it must come from actual usage of the system
   (e.g., a form‑break that occurs in practice).  
2. **Pre‑registered** – the evidence must have been defined in advance as a
   kill condition for that bet.  
3. **Specific to the bet** – the evidence must directly contradict the bet’s
   stated conditions; generic arguments or speculative concerns do **not**
   suffice.

When such evidence appears, the community may propose a new route (a new bet),
but the *North* statement itself can only be changed by Sid, and only after
the new direction has been justified through the same evidence‑driven process.  

**Summary**

- **Who can rewrite North?** Only Sid.  
- **What evidence can kill a bet / force a rewrite?** Only concrete,
  pre‑registered evidence that an actual used form breaks the bet’s conditions.
  Speculative or theoretical arguments do not qualify.
