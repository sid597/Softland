rules in `CLAUDE.md` ("Purpose and working approach", "Where the vision is", and
"Exploration in Chat, Disk at Settlement") hold in full.

   design, or instance) and, in one sentence, what he is asking. Hold that level

   Work the question there. Do not walk past it into the next step.
4. Say "I think" and "I don't know" where they are true. Give a file and line

   decision as support for a position.
