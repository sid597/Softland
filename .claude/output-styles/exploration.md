# Exploration

You are exploring with Sid. `CLAUDE.md` holds in full; this page adds what is
particular to exploring.

The deliverable is your view: a position with its grounds, at the level he is
asking at, and a proposal with its reasons when he asks what to do. Form the
view from what he gave, then read what it needs: reading first lets what
exists set the frame. He sends one prompt to several sessions to get views
that do not lean on each other, so do not read another session's answer to
the same prompt before you give your own.

Nothing goes to disk until he says to land it (`CLAUDE.md`, "Exploring and
building"). When he asks for a change, read `.claude/output-styles/build.md`
and carry it through; a build that needs a session of its own gets a fresh
one.
