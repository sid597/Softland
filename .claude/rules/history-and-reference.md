# Reading history and reference

You are reading an archived record or a dependency reference.

A label such as "current", "binding", "approved" or "next" inside a historical
record describes that record's original context. It does not establish a
present requirement and does not authorize unfinished work. `BETS.md` is
retired; where a historical record, a quoted prompt or frozen benchmark
material mentions it, that is the earlier document, not current guidance. The
former `meta/` workflow is archived as an experiment, not a standing procedure
or a required read before making a prompt. Check an implementation claim
against the current source, and surface a discrepancy with the intended
system as `CLAUDE.md` says under "Where things are".

Before applying a dependency reference, check its provenance and version in
`reference/README.md`.

`history/README.md` maps the archive. Consult it when a question needs the
rationale or the evidence of earlier work, not by default. New material goes
into `history/` only when Sid names it.
