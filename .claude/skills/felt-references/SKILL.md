# Felt references

Sid has no designer. These artifacts solved real epistemic-UX problems under
real users. **Play them.** This file is pointers, not a substitute — and it
carries zero authority (standing law: references are scrap, Sid decides).

## The shelf — what each one proved

- **Outer Wilds** — a knowledge graph with *"there's more to explore here"*
  markers: shows the shape of what you don't know without its content. Two
  projections over one truth (rumor graph ↔ spatial map). Reading in place at
  the artifact. Every door open from minute one; only understanding gates you.
  *The Nomai:* a signed, in-place, threaded sense-line with disagreements and
  failures preserved — a stranger finishes their project 280,000 years later.
  (Their cautionary half: writing outlived them, tools didn't — they couldn't
  renovate.)
- **Return of the Obra Dinn** — hypotheses stay fluid until corroboration
  locks them, three at a time. Silver→gold ratification, shipped.
- **Chants of Sennaar** — you propose meanings for unknown glyphs; the system
  ratifies at checkpoints. Cheap wrong guesses, deferred confirmation.
- **Her Story** — search-as-exploration over a raw corpus; the query trail is
  the understanding.
- **Tunic** — a manual that becomes legible as you learn. Readability itself
  has progression.
- **Blue Prince · The Witness** — inference from your own notes; a grammar
  taught wordlessly by example.

## Probe questions

Use these to interrogate a surface, not to justify one.

**On borrowing.** Is this reference *worn* (shipped, played by millions —
evidence) or *drawn* (a mockup — someone's guess)? Both have zero authority
over what Softland should be; only one is evidence that a flow works at all.

**On hiding.** These games withhold. Which kind is it here?
- withheld *for delight* → invert it; the morning answer is never an adventure
- withheld *to test understanding* → maybe keep it. Softland's own QC already
  runs on instrumental concealment: dual-read anti-anchoring, fresh-context
  validation, pre-registered kill conditions.

**On testing.** *"How do I know I understand something if I can't test on
it?"* (Sid, 07-26). These games never quiz — the door opens iff your model is
right. Assessment by use. Three ways that already exist here:
re-derive (hide a recorded verdict, rebuild it, compare) · predict (record
what you expect before opening a result) · build (author a revision that does
what you intended and survives activation — the land is the grader).
Splits along masters ≠ kinds: mechanism has a correct model the land can
grade; meaning doesn't, so its tests are transfer and prediction.

**On failure.** When someone's model is wrong here, does the failure say
*where*? An error card naming what the value should have been teaches;
"invalid" only protects.

**On specs you can write without drawing.** *"The trail view should feel like
rumor mode, not a file browser."* Point at a worn artifact, name the
inversion. That's a falsifiable spec from someone who doesn't draw.

## Blind spot

Nobody writes in these games. Strong on orientation and verification, silent
on composing, versioning, preview, blast radius, reversal, provenance,
plurality. Nearest existing family there is version-control UX, which is bad —
open field.

Also: authored, finite, single-player. Softland is none of those. Borrow
flows, never the world model.
