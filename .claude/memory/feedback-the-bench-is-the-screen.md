When the judge card or a starter says "what Sid sees on screen", it means the existing prototype artifact (the bench) and the client's own output as files, not a page the builder makes for the occasion. Sid, 2026-09-06, on the offer of a page showing the client's rendered scenarios beside the bench's numbers: "bro no one is asking for a view that has to be on screen i am not review it on sloopy ui you going to make that is already in the prototype artifact".

**Why:** The bench is already the felt thing, built and measured over twelve sessions; a second surface made in an afternoon is a sloppier copy that adds a fabrication to judge instead of the work. "Bring him things he can feel" ([[working-agreements]]) is satisfied by the artifact that exists; it is not a licence to make another. The client's pictures and numbers are receipts, and receipts are files (`dump_result.mjs`, PNGs in a directory), not a UI.

**How to apply:** For any "make it judgeable" ask, land scripts that produce every number and picture from the client itself into files, plus a REPL recipe for change-it moves by hand, and point at the existing bench for the expected values. If a picture must be looked at, look at the PNG (Read it) and say what it shows. A departure would be Sid asking for a page in words. See [[feedback-durable-work-lands-in-docs-not-tmp]] for where the scripts live and [[reference-two-chairs-workflow]] for the card.
