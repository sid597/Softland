Live fire 2026-08-16, reconciliation round. Four sessions (fenced episodes /
record / code) answered Sid's commonality question with a use-case
nomination — "hold design conversations inside Softland, agent places
blocks." Sid: "I did try to use Softland in Softland… I arrived at 'how do
I modify this more?' and I hit a red roadblock… no ECS possible… no
smalltalk kind of VM… I was asking what is the common thing that needs to
be built out? What it needs to be built out of? … I don't think you got it
at all." His commissioning message had said it: "finding what the
commonality is **and how to make it in the system**" — the second clause
got dropped in prompt construction. (Sid)

**Why:** His questions ride his build history. The wall he personally hit
(cannot modify a block from inside; anatomy hardcoded; renderer coupled;
Electric on a single atom with no thousand-note story) set the question's
LEVEL: substrate/anatomy, not product/arrangement. The rig carried his
purposes and law but never his build-failure arc, so every session
answered one level too high — and "go live in the product" reads to him
as "return to the exact artifact that failed you."

**How to apply:** When Sid asks for commonality/unlocks/"what to build",
answer at build-derivation level: name the common THING and its
composition (what exists, what's missing, what it rides on). Any
derivation session's input must include his lived build-failure arc (G7,
the ECS shock "block anatomy is still hardcoded", the scared night /
Electric misuse) alongside purposes and law — the LOG holds it all.
