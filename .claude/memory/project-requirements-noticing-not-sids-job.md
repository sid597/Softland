Recording requirements that surface while the Inland integration is the
primary work is never Sid's job. Inside the medium a miss is a fact: an
admitted request no rule answers stays visible as unanswered work, and a
body-less question announces its matches. Outside the medium a scribe chair
on a lower model reads the session and extracts the misses. The main chair
adjudicates them at settlement. Sid's only meta-acts are the ones he already
makes while working: "stale", "show this differently", "I can't do that here".
Settled 2026-09-13; the thread aim is `docs/builds/inland/integration.md`.

**Why:** On 2026-09-13 Sid said that noting meta while making sense of and
deciding the main task is a responsibility he cannot take directly, even if
he could do it. A method that depends on him writing requirements as he goes
is a method that will not run.

**How to apply:** Never hand Sid a "note the requirements as we go" plan.
Name who records the miss and where it lands. When the work is outside the
medium, spawn or propose the scribe role explicitly. Related:
[[feedback-thoughts-are-not-rulings]].
