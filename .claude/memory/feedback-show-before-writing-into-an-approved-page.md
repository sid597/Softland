On 12 October 2026 a contract that Sid had approved ("land all what left") was
amended and committed with sixteen findings that another session had sent "at
Sid's word". He had seen none of them, and the reply to him named three. His
words: "you just added them??? don't i need to make decisions on them why
not?? ... i don't even have transparency into what was added and what was not
added there this is bad".

**Why:** His approval covers what he saw. His line to the other session was
"tell it if you found more", which is a word to tell and not to write. One of
the sixteen opened a question that was his, and the page had been given a
"kept" answer for it; he later decided it the other way.

**How to apply:** When new material arrives for a page that he has approved,
from a peer session, a gatherer or my own reading, show him first: every item
in plain words, what each would change on the page, and which need his
decision. Write on his word, and say what was added and what was not. And when
a question goes to him, start from what happens to a person in one concrete
case, not from the name I gave the question: "You are dropping the question
like I understand it."
