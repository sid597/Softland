When summarizing, road-mapping, or briefing an arc Sid drove through his
own chats, his original terms (e.g. "Electric-native", "host", "Softland
as target", "Missionary") must appear alongside any vocabulary later
sessions minted for the same thing ("the render seam", "doors", "fenced
views"). (Sid, 2026-08-03.)

**Why:** The render-seam roadmap answer spoke only in session-minted
names; Sid — who had spent many chats deriving the Electric question —
read the entire arc as deleted ("its nowhere to be seen what
happened?????"). The work was all on disk; only the words had changed.
Renaming is a [[meta-failure-generators]] fluency move: fresh names feel
cleaner to the session and cost the owner his map. The durable cure for
that arc is the "In Sid's words" block atop decisions.md "The render
seam"; this memory is the general rule.
**SECOND LIVE FIRE 2026-08-13**, same generator, this memory on disk and
unconsulted: the return-arc CLOSE SUMMARY presented Sid's architecture
ruling ("electric should drive the rendering engine and vice versa") as
"keyed wire / store-contract slice / demand fabric," scattered across
road steps behind a sitting — Sid: "where the fuck is electric backed
machinery???? wtf happened to this." The warning line "renaming the arc
makes it invisible to Sid" sat in the constitution the whole time.

**How to apply:** Before sending any status/roadmap/briefing — and a
session's CLOSING SUMMARY is always a briefing, no exception — check:
does each arc appear under the name Sid uses for it? The structural
test for roadmaps/topologies: Sid's ruling sentence must be able to sit
as a HEADING in the structure — if the structure can't absorb his
verbatim sentence, the structure buried his ruling; reorder, don't
relabel. If a settled doc carries only session vocabulary, add the
translation to the DOC itself (an "in Sid's words" line), not just the
chat. When it fires anyway: repair the DOC's structure same turn —
his words as the spine — never just apologize in chat.
**Register refinement (Sid, 2026-08-13, closing the second fire):
"product language is more useful."** The briefing register is PRODUCT
language — what each step gives the inhabitant — with Sid's verbatim as
the headings and session/tree vocabulary subordinate in parentheses. A
road step is fully briefed only when it carries both: his words + what
it does for him. Third data point of one law (with D-28's
MVP-grammar-reads-as-optionality and the 08-02 product-level mapping
that "read true"): a briefing's language LEVEL carries commitments, not
just information.
