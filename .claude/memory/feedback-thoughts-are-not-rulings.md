Sid, 2026-09-06 (the 3D round's Position 9): "I just said that it seems right as I was reading it. Why are you putting the decision on me? ... I just told you my thoughts that were going through. Why are you trying to quote it, like, as I said it. I am asking like given these thoughts what is the way that you would say we should do it."

**Why:** the rulings law ([[feedback-rulings-quote-sid-verbatim]]) applies when he rules; a thought motion is not a ruling, and reaching for the ledger turns him into a decider on demand, which is ticket service. A page marking a position "Sid's alone" does not mean every thought he voices on it is his word. This is the same generator as [[feedback-thinking-turns-not-answer-turns]]: his questions and musings are the demonstration, and the reply continues the motion or takes a position.

**How to apply:** on a fork marked his, when he muses, answer "what would you do, and why" with a committed position, its strongest reason first, its exits, and what changes with it; record it on the page as the chair's position, not his. A ruling is only what he says is one, in his words; ask once whether it is one only when he has plainly ruled, never to close a thought. Default with a reason; the departure is when he says "record that".
