**The incident ledger (2026-08, dates are the fact):** a 28.7s frame /
35.7s visual settle. Three static analyses across two lanes (Fable +
Codex) produced three DIFFERENT dominant mechanisms. A TRUE finding — the
cluster-bounds quadratic, later measured at 24.39s ≈ 68% of wall — was
killed for a full cycle by a refutation that inspected the wrong file, and
the refutation was inherited by every later document. The claimed dominant
cost (HarfBuzz shaping) measured 1.2s of 28.6s. One 30-minute read-only
profile settled everything — and the refutation's fix direction would have
closed the correction with ~24s of cold cost intact (confident wrongness
minting false settled ground: the real cost class, 10–100× wasted
analysis). Review value flipped sign at the receipt: pre-receipt
cross-review multiplied wrong narratives; the single post-ruling bounded
review caught two real contract holes.

**Why (the distilled regularities):**
- Every consequential ERROR was a magnitude/attribution claim made by
  reading; every durable pre-receipt FIND was a structure claim made by
  reading. Static analysis is a structure instrument misused as a
  magnitude instrument — hence the claim-type law.
- Naive "no analysis before measurement" would have suppressed the
  incident's two best finds (the quadratic hypothesis, the confirmed hover
  prediction). Depth is legal; the DELIVERABLE TYPE is what's fenced
  pre-receipt (probe + pre-registered predictions, never a verdict).
- A second reader is correlated noise with confident affect;
  identity-prestige ("the code model said so") is how the wrong refutation
  outranked the right claim. Model identity = zero evidence; escalate
  modality.
- Prompt shape predicted output quality better than lane: verdict-inviting
  breadth prompts → confident wrongness from every lane; receipt-anchored
  prompts → the strongest artifacts from every lane. Lane-capability
  beliefs are retro-noted hypotheses, never law.
- The forest asks themselves paid off (the SEAM non-close ruling, two
  contract holes) — only their CONCURRENCY with attribution failed.
  Breadth queues behind the receipt; it never shrinks.

**How to apply:** load the skill at any live failure, regression, or
disputed causal claim; run probe → ruling → one bounded review; ship
tripwires (violation-triggered, owner-naming, law-tied) as contract
