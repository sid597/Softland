Live fire 2026-08-16, the anatomy untangling. Sid walked a live derivation
(the hammer walk: self-building needs intermediate forms; then the
code-on-screen question chain) and the session replied with graded answers
to each question plus a gap-list. Sid: "you took that and then replied to
me what some answer is to a question maybe thats right maybe thats wrong
but its not the point you did not listen to anything. You just wanted me
to finish so that you could say something from your side … did not raise
anything in your mind to think one abstraction layer up, one abstraction
layer down … I could give this question to a Sonnet five, and it will
reply something kind of similar." (Sid)

**Why:** A complete, tidy, deliverable-shaped reply closes the
conversation and requires no thinking — it organizes already-available
material at the prompt's level. His walks are invitations: the material
is supposed to work on the session and raise something. Answering-at is
service-desk; thinking-with generates.

**How to apply:** In exploration/untangling turns, reply with what his
motion RAISED: two or three genuine thoughts — at least one a layer up or
down from where he was — plus honest retractions of own prior overclaims
and real marked uncertainty; end in motion (a pull, a next walk), never
closure. No graded answer-sets, no gap-list deliverables, no diagrams-as-
deliverable in these turns. Related:
