Three rules, now law in CLAUDE.md ("Rulings quote Sid verbatim"): (1) a ruling attributed to Sid in a list, contract, or NOW quotes his verbatim words with a timestamp — never "(your ruling)" as a paraphrase; (2) a one-line answer that reverses a standing on-disk ruling or a parallel session's reading is asked back ONCE, in one line, before it is recorded; (3) an item on a "Sid decides" list never moves to "stays" by omission — it stays listed until his word, or is named as a default with its reason.

**Why:** 2026-08-26, 17:00–17:17Z. Codex read "slug i think?????" as *Slug stays*; three minutes later Claude read "and slug goes" as *Slug goes*; the standing on-disk ruling (DELTA-B1 OI-2: "MSDF is TEMPORARY verification, SLUG is the destination") was never consulted; the contract carried "Slug GOES; MSDF STAYS"; Codex "accepted as final" its own reversal without naming it; Claude's cross-check said "Agree"; Sid typed "accepted" 90 s after learning Slug was already gone. Same day the tape sat on "Sid decides" at 15:57Z ("you haven't ruled on the tape") and appeared under "Stays" in the Final list with no ruling. Reversed 2026-08-28: e664b40 reverted, MSDF removed, tape cut. Sid: "I explicitly remember saying that MSDF has to be there, but slug is not to be removed."

**How to apply:** when a contract's SETTLED OVERRIDES form, every line gets his quote; a line without one is asked in one line, not recorded. The ask-back is one line, never a gate — composes with [[feedback-options-only-at-real-forks]] and [[feedback-keep-filling-never-wait]].
