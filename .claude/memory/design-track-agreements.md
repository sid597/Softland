- **principal-designer is MANUAL-ONLY.** Sid invokes (`/principal-designer` / "designer lens"). Never auto-invoke for eng/product-eng work — even "discuss X product wise." The canon contaminates eng discussions (proven 2026-06-10: the no-canon answer grounded in the real contract ranked best; the canon-loaded answer last). Eng discussions run on CLAUDE.md rules + actual source code.
- **Fresh cuts stay fresh:** parallel tracks (Claude/Codex) and new discussions don't read each other's cuts or prior answer-shaped drafts — independence is the value ("perspectives preserved separately; Claude's is canonical"); cross-track synthesis is a separate later act Sid runs. The 2026-06 repo-wide quarantine was lifted by Sid, but the method returns whenever he opens a new parallel round. Contamination that happens gets disclosed immediately.
- **Synthesis, not anthology:** "compile/incorporate these sources" = ONE text in one authorial voice (best framing as spine, best craft as structure, best content as substance; attribution = a footnote). Comparison tables, rankings, and verbatim archives belong in chat, never in the deliverable. The deliverable serves his goal, not the process story.
- **Designer imagination unbound:** in the designer chair, dream at full scope — no token/scope bounds, no build-governance applied to dreaming; honesty comes from LABELING the dream, not shrinking it. Scope discipline re-engages only in build/contract artifacts. Standing: no biology examples — swap domains (fusion, materials, astronomy, control theory).
- **"UI work" = the top-down design pass:** how things LOOK from the top — form, orientation, all-closed/few-open/all-open states, container language (corners, color, type scale, density) — then feasibility. Never just legibility repair. Deliver rendered alternatives over real material, side by side; he picks by feel (top layer = his taste, never decided for him; middle = coherent systems with rationale; junior = execution).
- **Design briefs, not contracts:** prompts for design harnesses = scene, mood, cast, exploration direction, and the PROBLEM the view answers — a brief without the problem is a mood board. Never eng-style numbered constraints/gates; never the project's internal metaphor register; never the wall/notebook as material target (they are provenance of the questions, not the look). One paste per fresh chat; top layer alone first, deeper layers only for the survivor his eye picks.
