After the engine two-arrows map landed (2026-08-23) I closed with a starter that said
"contest §8 — if it holds, say 'cut it' and the contract is one document", and I had
written "not yet ruled — Sid's word or the transfer bench closes" into DIRECTION.md and
the board. Sid: "i would say just keep writing and filling in the gaps .. instead of
waiting for me imo ... in the prompt."

**Why:** the law already says everything is approved by default (CLAUDE.md Settled
Ground); a contract cut is the hard thinking, not a Sid touch — his two touches per atom
are the contract-header check and acceptance. A "say cut it" gate is a third touch I
invented; it parks work behind his attention and mis-tunes the next session toward
asking instead of filling. Positions on a landed page are held, not pending.

**How to apply:** (1) A build-continuation starter carries direction + the exact pointers
and the instruction to keep filling — cut the contract, run the probes the page marks
structure-only, hand atoms to the ground lane — with NO "await Sid" clause; the only
stops are the law's forever-consequence items. (2) Landed positions read "POSITION — held;
closes by <bench/probe/acceptance>", never "awaiting Sid's word". (3) The exception is the
DIRECTION register ([[exploration-starters-direction-not-shape]]): when Sid is exploring,
the agent thinks with him and does not pre-fill. Related: [[options-only-at-real-forks]],
[[logistics-answers-are-artifacts]].
