Sid's lens, verbatim (relaying Codex's falsification of the twelve-clause
span): "beware of how gpt talks and its on your wise wisdom to accept ...
NOTE that the architecture is not to be rooted in what is BUT what we want it
to be and you are the one that will have to keep that very very well
protected." (Sid)

**Why:** Codex graded the span UNSAFE largely because current code fails the
laws — three of its four WRONG verdicts were want-vs-is conflations (fail-open
lens, unresolved holes, coverage-in-practice). Accepting the verdict word
would canonize the is over the want; rejecting the findings wholesale would
lose real repairs (the coverage-composition law, five cross-confirmed organ
gaps). Both failure directions are accommodation.

**How to apply:** At ingest time split every finding before ruling: does it
hit the WANT (internal contradiction, cross-clause contradiction, or a
scenario where following the law produces the harm it forbids) or only the IS
(code doesn't do it yet → build-gap/open-register entry with a named owner)?
Reviewer verdict vocabulary never transfers into rulings — re-derive the
verdict from the receipt. Re-verify load-bearing cites first-hand. A
want-finding amends the law; an is-finding files debt. Run
[[falsification-review-protocol]] and the second-order-mirror before drafting
any response; adjudication of a live-code claim keeps investigation-fence
labels (verified structure vs unexecuted inference). See also
[[meta-failure-generators]].
