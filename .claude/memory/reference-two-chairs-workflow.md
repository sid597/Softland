`docs/below-the-waist/two-chairs.md` (committed 2026-09-06, at Sid's word after the path round's fourth attack) is the workflow for the two chairs: the composer holds the picture, contract, page and bench; the definer holds the definitions and writes `attack-N.md` with records and numbers; Sid opens a round with its essence, carries paths, rules positions verbatim on the page's ledger, decides boots. The cycle has two phases (attack the picture; attack the contract) and the tell is whether an attack adds an output no piece has. Prompts are fence, essence, terrain and the two riders, pointing at the document rather than restating it ([[feedback-exploration-starters-direction-not-shape]], [[feedback-continuation-starters-hold-the-view]]).

Two stances Sid ruled the same day that every future round carries: the best in the field is a datapoint, never the thing to copy; nothing existing is carried forward because it exists. Preliminary: the 3D round's feedback is not yet read against it; phase B's smaller fold has run zero times; the cold-mind attack has not run.
