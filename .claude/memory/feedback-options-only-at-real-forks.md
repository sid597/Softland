Six "open questions" were laid before Sid (2026-08-12, the return sitting);
only two were genuinely his to rule (faces-as-data, reuse posture). The
other four were spec-work or probe-decided engineering dressed as
questions — he answered "i dont know choices you are giving me or what do
i do ??" and "not sure how this matters", then named the law himself:
"what i like is to have the options ifff there are any like dont make up
just for the sake of it."

**Why:** A question he can't act on burns his attention and mis-tunes the
session toward survey instead of movement. It is the AskUserQuestion
discipline applied to prose — and it composes with the sense-line doc's
own style ("Open — sorted by what closes each").

**How to apply:** Before presenting any open-questions list, tag each item
with WHO closes it: Sid's word · spec-sitting output he redlines · probe or
receipt · contract. Only Sid's-word items get options — real ones, with
stakes and a lean. Everything else appears as a position with "closes by",
or not at all. Related: [[logistics-answers-are-artifacts]],
[[exploration-starters-direction-not-shape]].
