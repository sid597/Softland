In an exploration phase, rank outputs on whether they tell Sid what to paint and how to compose: the framing, the way of thinking handed over, and whether the picture is whole. Detail errors (a wrong equivalence, an overstated law) are fixable and must not drive the ranking. Correctness-of-commitments is the criterion for the contract/build phase, not for exploration.

**Why:** Sid, 2026-09-05, on the path-kind ranking round after eight peer rankings had penalised Session 8 for one conceded geometric error: "fuck the correctness and penalising it … details can be fixed but the current goal is for me let the full thorough picture its like starting a painting .. what is more important knowing what to paint? how to compose? … it will get solid and fixed as boots move to the ground … framing and how to think is much more important in exploration phases."

**How to apply:** Before any ranking or verdict pass, name the phase. Exploration ⇒ score framing, how-to-think tools, thoroughness of the picture, positions with exits, things he can feel; list detail errors separately as a fix list, never as rank penalties. Contract/build ⇒ [[falsification_review]] and correctness govern. Watch the family bias: this criterion favours position-taking output shapes (Claude's), the correctness criterion favours qualified prose (Codex's); say which criterion is in force.
