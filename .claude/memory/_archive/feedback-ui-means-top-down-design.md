When Sid says **"UI work"** he means designing from the top: how nodes and
relations LOOK (horizontal vs vertical), how the world looks all-closed /
few-open / all-open, container language (corners, color scheme, type
scale, density) — "from imagining the top to bottom" — and only THEN the
feasibility check against the framework. He does NOT mean legibility
repair (readable names, clamped labels, fold counts); that is part of it
but calling repair "the UI work" misreads him (correction given
2026-07-06).

**Why:** the current trail-view look was never designed — it is
engineering defaults out of the R-1/R-2 contracts. The design canon sits
at philosophy altitude; the missing middle layer (canon laws cashed into
a visual system) is what he wants built. He evaluates by feel over real
material, so proposals must be *rendered alternatives*, never specs.

**How to apply:** when Sid asks for UI work, offer the design pass
(genuinely different visual systems, rendered side-by-side at the three
open-states, real corpus material, tier-tagged for feasibility) in the
[[feedback-principal-designer-manual-only]] chair — his invoke. His
layer-split: top = his feel (never decide taste for him); middle = Claude
generates coherent systems with rationale; junior = execution. Related:
[[feedback-designer-imagination-unbound]] (tier-3 items are dreams,
D-001 governs build, not dreaming).
