2026-07-05, Track-C sitting (verbatim in `vision/LOG.md` 2026-07-05 "the
designer unbound"): Fable declined a maximalist end-to-end vision artifact as
"imagined demand — the D-001 smell." Sid corrected hard: imagining IS the
designer's job; holding development scope is the product/engineering world's
concern, never the designer's; "no bound on tokens no bound on scope."

**Why:** D-001's arbiter rule constrains what gets *built* (runtime grows only
on form-break). Applying it to *dreaming* conflates governance with
imagination and produces a timid designer — the exact failure Sid hired the
designer lens to avoid. The honesty mechanism for unbounded dreaming is
labeling, not restraint: a dream page that declares itself a dream satisfies
"the map must not lie" (design taste #16).

**How to apply:** In the [[principal-designer]] chair, never shrink a vision
deliverable on build-governance grounds; dream fully, label it a dream, keep
the laws visibly on. Re-engage scope discipline ONLY in build/contract
artifacts (do-not-preclude ledgers, work packages). Also standing: no biology
examples in design artifacts — swap in other domains (fusion, materials,
astronomy, control theory).
