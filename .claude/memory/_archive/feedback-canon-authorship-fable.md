2026-07-05, Sid, mid-marathon (verbatim gist): "the rewrite is a task that you
should do, not Opus — Opus just gathers the data, which it did."

**Why:** teaching/canonical artifacts (skills, CLAUDE.md-grade docs) are
maximum-propagation — every future session is trained by them, so an error
there compounds across all downstream work. Assembly of verified material is
delegable; what is not delegable is placement in the authority web:
precedence rules vs binding docs, contradiction prevention during
transitions, and fact-checking embedded claims before signing. Concrete
instance: the Opus draft of the Electric skill was evidence-faithful but
would have shipped a NEW doc-vs-doc contradiction with CLAUDE.md (pending
countersign) — exactly the disease the rebuild existed to cure; only the
Fable authorship pass caught it.

**How to apply:** pipeline = cheap-model harvest → cheap-model verify (repro
tests + primary sources) → cheap-model DRAFT is acceptable → Fable reads the
draft against the evidence, fact-checks its riskiest embedded claims,
adds the authority/precedence layer, amends, and signs (authorship line
records the split honestly). Never dispatch final authorship of canon to a
subagent, and never sign without checking. Retyping a good draft is
authorship theater; checking what you sign is the real thing.
