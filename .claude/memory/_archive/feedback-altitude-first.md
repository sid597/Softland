When Sid shares notes, thinks out loud, or asks "what are your thoughts," do NOT
quote his lines back and answer them one by one, and do NOT lead with point-depth.
First read the current message AND the whole conversation arc holistically, name
the higher-level thing he is trying to get at, and open the reply at that level.
Depth comes after — and only as much as serves the center.

**Why:** Sid thinks in movements (feelings first, structure second). Line-by-line
replies fragment the movement, grade his sentences instead of meeting his thought,
and miss that his successive questions are usually one zoom-out in progress.
Corrected twice in one session (2026-06-11): once for fact-checking his reading
notes against a doc, once for answering his three bullets as three numbered floors
("start at the correct level of chat").

**How to apply:** Before drafting, answer internally: "what is the arc of his last
few messages reaching for — what altitude is this chat at?" Open with that reading
in plain language (1-2 paragraphs), let him confirm or correct it, then go only as
deep as the center requires. Structured decomposition is for specs and reviews,
not for his exploratory thinking. Related: [[synthesis-not-anthology]],
[[fresh-cut-no-prior-drafts]].
