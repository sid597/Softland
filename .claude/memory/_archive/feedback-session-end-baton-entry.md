Every session — build or thinking — must end with an assertion-grade entry in
the NOW/baton section of `docs/sessions/next-prompt.md`: what was decided,
verified, doubted; findings verbatim on any FAIL; judgment calls flagged for
the next reader; the next step.

**Why:** Adopted by Sid 2026-07-04 (Roam card answer: "yes adopt this") as the
solo-operator minimum viable discipline — the answer to "what is the
commit-message-quality equivalent for one human + agents." Two grounds from
lived evidence: (1) when the relation-kernel PLAN_VALIDATION FAIL artifacts
were overwritten, the baton's verbatim entries were the only surviving record;
(2) stances/adjudications die with sessions unless written — the verdict is
the unit that dies in the chatbox.

**How to apply:** Before ending any session, append the entry to
`docs/sessions/next-prompt.md`. Not optional hygiene — an adopted rule,
recorded in `decisions.md` Open Questions (2026-07-04). Related:
[[fable-operating-model]].
