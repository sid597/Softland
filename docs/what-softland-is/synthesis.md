## What the drawing exposes

**Tension — headless vs form-open** (standing, flagged in the-name): the
essays refuse headless; Sid's substrate list includes "a backend-only
service". Both stand as testimony; Sid rules the seam or leaves it.

**Split, kept visible — economy law vs marketplace bet**:
economy-on-the-ledger is constitution; the lived marketplace was split
DOWN to a bet (2026-08-10 ruling, BETS C3, "never constitution"). The
vision file holds the testimony either way; this map just keeps the split
from being forgotten.

**Gap — sovereignty has no law**: whose data, whose models, what may be
trained on your land — no constitution file's INDEX line names it
(mixed-species-leases governs *authority*, not custody of data and
models; total-provenance's redactable-payload seat is the nearest
neighbor). Read from the constitution's INDEX lines, not every file —
minutes to falsify. If real: a candidate reserved seat or future clause,
Sid adjudicates. Until then the stance rides the LOG and session law.

**Center of gravity — absorption**: one thread runs purpose → law → land
→ build. The interface question ("what is the interface to agi or to
alien or to god") IS the absorption ceiling (mixed-species) IS what the
felt land exists to deliver (the-land / land-is-the-law) IS what
candidate-self-hosting tests first. Most feels-level arrows lean on it;
it is the only place where testimony, law, felt design, and a nominated
build already form one unbroken chain. If the reconciliation session
wants the greatest unlock, the arrows already point here.
