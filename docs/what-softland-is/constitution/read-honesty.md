# Read honesty

**Law.** Citizenship's second form. Every lens declares its cut — as-of is a
mandatory dimension of every lens declaration, `current` is a legal value,
and the cut resolves once, at invocation admission, to a pinned revision
vector or declares itself non-atomic. Every lens names its lacks. Byte-equal
across species, precisely: equal at the shared projection surface; packagers
extend *what* a mind sees, never mint a machine-only *where*. Every lens
opens into its basis. Lens declarations are admitted by floor-tier acts, as
material declarations are; an undeclared lens does not activate. Coined
lenses ride the data lane down to closed selection + shaping; computation
stays code. Time enters the architecture exactly once, as the cut dimension.
A mind-made derivative is a lens at invocation and material at deposit; the
invocation receipt — its declared cut and basis — is the deposited
material's birth record: its `:based-on` edges at pinned revisions. An act
summoned without its lens may proceed, but its deposit cannot silently claim
basis: it lands dark-based under
[settlement-publication-custody](settlement-publication-custody.md), or is
refused. (Enforcement point: Sid closes.)

**Why it binds.** A lens that lies is a lying map; declared cut, named
lacks, and auditable derivation are what turn map-must-not-lie from
aspiration into the lens citizen's grammar. Admission by floor-tier acts
keeps the masquerade from returning one layer up. The receipt-as-birth-record
composition makes ambient basis capture structural rather than a discipline:
nothing can be deposited through a lens without its basis record, because
the lens could not activate without declaring it. And a declared lack is
honest today, a violation only when a lens claims otherwise — the law turns
comments into obligations.
