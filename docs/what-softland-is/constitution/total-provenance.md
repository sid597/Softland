# Total provenance

**Law.** Every act carries who, basis, and when. The log is the asset; every
view is disposable. The commons keeps everything — today's exhaust is
tomorrow's ore, designed for readers that don't exist yet. Payloads are
redactable; act-existence is undeniable.

**Why it binds.** Provenance is the one sentence that can never be
retrofitted: acts that carry author, basis, and identity are weavable later;
acts that don't, never are. The PDB lesson prices the "keep everything"
clause — substance deposited with discipline for fifty years met a strong
enough reader and paid out nonlinearly. The redaction asterisk is the
constitution's honest crack: someone will eventually need a thing not merely
superseded but unsaid — the record keeps that something was said and by whom,
while the what becomes provably removable.
