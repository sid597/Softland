# Identity law

**Law.** Every thing has a durable, medium-blind address. Native, authored,
and declared distiller-grain parts are born at import or authoring; an
addressing act demand-mints a previously unrepresented subpart, idempotent
by (parent-address, medium-typed region-identity). A family's
region-identity declaration states its normalization — what counts as "the
same region"; sameness beyond it is co-reference, never a second identity.
Anchors are single links to the immediate parent; chains exist only at
resolution. Occurrences are per-view and ephemeral; routes are evidence.
Basis rides acts and receipts, never names.

**Why it binds.** A route worn as a name mints one identity per viewing —
the duplicate-identity bug the container kernel already fixed once for text;
the composed address died as "the occurrence record wearing identity's
clothes." Basis embedded in the name fragments identity across revisions —
two marks on the same bolt at different revisions become marks on different
names, and the edge graph shatters; pointing into history is an as-of read,
a lens dimension, not a different identity. Single-link anchors make
re-parenting rewrite exactly one anchor and zero references — the strongest
form of survivability. And the two-tier birth clause repairs the
compression that contradicted the graph producers: import distillers create
declared-grain parts before any mind points.
