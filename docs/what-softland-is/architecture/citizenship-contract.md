# Citizenship contract

**Duty.** The declaration a family answers to enter the record — one
contract, two forms, not two architectures.

*Material citizens* answer settlement laws. The declaration: surface form;
distiller; anchor type + re-anchor law + dangling policy; region-identity
and its normalization; settlement profile — candidacy | echo | op-log — with
the echo profile defined by A2: per-edit-quantum committed acts, pending
input local until the durable decision, the materialized pull the only echo,
echoes never driving the pending buffer (the profile's name is Sid's call);
edit vocabulary under the profile; packager; checker tiers sized to declared
blast radius. Publication is not in the medium contract
([settlement-publication-custody](../constitution/settlement-publication-custody.md)).

*Lens citizens* answer read-honesty laws: declared cut, named lacks,
byte-equal at the shared projection surface, auditable derivation
([read-honesty](../constitution/read-honesty.md)). A coined lens is a lens
citizen authored as material — no authority over any substance, but
authorship, provenance, the candidate → activate lifecycle, worn at
addresses like a face; its selection and shaping ride the data lane,
its computation stays code.

Record-side and render-side membership are orthogonal: one contract for
being on the record, one registry for being on screen; a medium may hold
either or both. Declarations of both forms are admitted by floor-tier acts
([custody-floor](../constitution/custody-floor.md)).

**Seams.** Admissions run through the [code-floor](code-floor.md).
Settlement profiles feed the workshop strata
([workshops-and-publication](workshops-and-publication.md)). Packagers are
declared here, owned by [packagers](packagers.md). Distillers are
[graph-economy](graph-economy.md)'s first stratum; lens invocation receipts
feed its deposit bridge. Checker tiers give the floor its teeth.
