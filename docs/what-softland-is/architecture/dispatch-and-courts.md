# Dispatch and courts

**Duty.** Input custody. Adjudicate every input sample at every containment
boundary, innermost-first; the first claimed or consumed decision terminates
outward travel; every walk is receipted (A9). The court walk mints
occurrences and hands the [identity-service](identity-service.md) its mint
raw material — which part-geometry, in which parent. Carry the binding
grammar and its two reservation strengths — site-scoped and universal — that
protect the reserved verb tier; the courts themselves are kernel machinery
under the floor, not entries in it
([cage-or-silence](../constitution/cage-or-silence.md)).

**Seams.** Serves the [funnel](funnel.md) — claimed gestures cross into acts
at its mouth (A11's seam). Serves [identity-service](identity-service.md)
(mint requests) and [universal-verbs](universal-verbs.md) (reservation
enforcement). Court receipts are the explanation surface navigation
consumes — "how did this gesture land where it landed" — already served as
binding lint.

**Existence: embryo.** The resolver requires claims "INNERMOST FIRST" and
stops at the first decision (`binding_material.cljc:394-422`); the wheel
court grants region custody before ground, with a standing receipt counter
for dual dispatches (`region3d_pointer.cljc:102-121`); the two reservation
strengths exist — camera site-scoped (`binding_material.cljc:134-148`), meta
universal across every site and tier (`:150-161`). Receipts are not yet
uniform: ordinary binding decisions are returned values; only the wheel
court keeps a standing receipt (Codex F9 — `ground.cljs:3731`,
`scroll.cljs:15`). One court instance exists; the law wants one at every
containment boundary (round-0: "embryo: one instance (wheel)").
