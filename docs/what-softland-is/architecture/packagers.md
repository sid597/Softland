# Packagers

**Duty.** The mind channel's packaging law. Each material family declares
how its material becomes context a mind can read and what that packaging
costs: a declared budget and degradation ladder — full render + slice →
thumbnail + census → census only → refusal — receipted like rung divisors,
refusals loud. Packagers extend *what* a mind sees, never mint a
machine-only *where*; the shared projection surface stays byte-equal across
species. Packaging cost is the record-side sibling of render-cost rungs: two
species, two briefing channels, both budgeted, both declared. Coverage:
packagers propagate, never recompute, child coverage (A10).
