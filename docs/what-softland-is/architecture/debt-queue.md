# Debt queue

**Duty.** System-filed debt as durable, addressable material — one queue
surface, one address grammar for work items, three species with three
closers and explicit who-closes custody
([consequence-modes](../constitution/consequence-modes.md)). The species:
*commissions* — grammar refusals naming what the code lane owes;
*stale-marks* — basis-diff naming what moved under a derivation;
*re-examination questions* — moved-since-affirmation on a co-reference
group, filed as the semantic half of a mechanical detection. Consumers:
residents walking the queue — the repair economy's input.

**Seams.** Producers: the checkers' refusals (via
[citizenship-contract](citizenship-contract.md) grammars), basis movement
and co-reference movement (both detected over
[relation-kernel](relation-kernel.md)). Closers are acts through the
[funnel](funnel.md), which refuses stale closures (A7). Re-affirmation
strength inherits the pricing organ
([graph-economy](graph-economy.md)); the seam-plus-decision closure
terminates at the human
([workshops-and-publication](workshops-and-publication.md) owns the
decision-event shape).

debt lifecycle is present in the allowed source census." Precedents only:
