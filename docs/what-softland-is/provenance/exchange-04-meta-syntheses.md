**The simple story**

1. The two sessions did not do the same work twice — they cut the problem along two different planes, and both planes are real.
2. One-shot answered your commission as written: how a mind addresses, asks, links, navigates, and trusts its material across views. Its verdict: the showing machine is already general, the record machine only knows text, so build the record's missing half (its five pieces).
3. Multi-turn, steered by your opening question about natives and editors, answered a different question: how the system authors its own things from inside. Its verdict: a citizenship contract per family plus a small shared kernel, with a draft regime so that authoring isn't publishing.
4. They converge independently on three things, which is the strongest evidence the exercise produced: the general thing is a contract families answer, never a shared shape; addresses and edges are the only glue between media; and the one genuinely new noun is the address.
5. But their two address designs disagree: multi-turn's is a containment path minted by the input walk, basis-stamped in the name; one-shot's is a durable identity with separate anchor and occurrence legs, demand-minted when a mind points.
6. That collision is the most valuable single output of running them in parallel — it sits under every other organ both of them designed, so it's the one place that needs a joint settlement before anything is built.
7. Each session also owns organs the other's architecture silently depends on.
8. One-shot has no draft/publish boundary at all — every act in its walkthrough is a world-visible publish; multi-turn's settlement-vs-publication split and workshop scope are the repair.
9. Multi-turn has no time story, no navigation verbs, no basis/staleness economy, and no packagers — all four of which one-shot specced, and two of which (time, briefing) are in your commission's own projection list.
10. Three axes neither session worked: global manipulation as set-valued acts; who mints the edges the whole cross-projection vision runs on; and contradiction between two authored peers (spec says M8, the model's bolt becomes M10, neither derives from the other — staleness covers none of it).
11. One smaller shared miss: projection authoring — your list of projections is open ("such as…"), and nobody said who coins a new lens, in which custody lane.
12. I sent each session six items: the sibling's load-bearing organs it lacks, plus the both-missed axes routed to whichever frame owns them — phrased as falsification targets with source receipts, never as the sibling's worldview.
13. Before sending, I spot-checked the eight mechanisms the exchange hinges on (refine!, targets-by-address, wheel-route, :draft-settle, autotag, the "richer scopes" note, trail's "NO as-of", asserted-by); all exist where claimed.
14. Their adjudications will land as normal turns in their own chats; I told both not to message each other — routing stays with you and me.
