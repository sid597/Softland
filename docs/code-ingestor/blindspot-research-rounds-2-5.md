## Round-2 Blindspot Map — Softland Object-Container Kernel

### 1. Orientation

This round was built to do one thing the kernel's own authors cannot do from inside: find the literatures that would call the kernel **wrong, naive, or expensively over-committed**, by searching by *problem* rather than by *system* and mining negative space (post-mortems, "considered harmful," abandoned designs). Thirty sources were read adversarially across six angles (concurrency-without-central-truth, the operation/driving layer, incremental computation, event-sourcing/temporal DBs, provenance/argumentation, preservation/authenticity/frame-break).

**Headline finding:** the kernel is not wrong about versioning — it is wrong about versioning's *scope*. Versioning genuinely survives as the root of **change in the truth substrate** (immutability, accumulate-only history), independently re-derived by Datomic, Certificate Transparency, Perkeep, nanopublications, and Jujutsu. But the kernel routed **five other jobs** through versioning that each have a stronger native root: **identity** wants *addressing* (content-addressed, self-certifying, transcludable handles a foreign mind can verify offline); the **log→state→UI path** wants *incremental computation* (its own dependency DAG, trace store, early cutoff, verification-time); **reasoning trails** want *provenance + defeasible argumentation* (attributed statements with defeat edges and non-monotonic standing); **preserved disagreement and semantic zoom** want *preservation* (capture economics + a significant-properties contract); and the **back-arrow (C3)** wants *trust* (authority as a verifiable artifact, not a location). The single most-strained phrase in the entire kernel is C1's "**stable container + chain of immutable revisions**": "container" smuggles in copy-on-membership (killed by addressing/transclusion), and "chain" smuggles in a single ordering authority (killed by DAG / op-log / CRDT-fold). The deepest unaddressed fact, from the oldest source, is Sun et al.'s 1998 proof that an arrival-ordered log gives you **an order, not a merge** — convergence is not intention preservation.

### 2. New Framings of Our Problem, Ranked by Closeness to "Public Form Inhabitable by Another Mind"

**Rank 1 — Self-authenticating identity: authority as a property of a verifiable artifact, not a location.** This is the most direct realization of "inhabitable by another mind." A foreign mind (or a parallel offline LLM agent) cannot inhabit your understanding if it must *trust* that your log faithfully recorded the trail — it must be able to *verify* the trail it received is the trail that was committed and was never silently rewritten. RFC 6962 Certificate Transparency (https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/rfc6962) supplies signed tree heads + O(log n) inclusion/consistency proofs + cross-party gossip; nanopublications (https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/1809.06532) make identity the cryptographic hash of immutable content (trusty URI) so the artifact travels with its own integrity proof and is checkable offline across fifteen mirrors; Perkeep (https://github.com/perkeep/perkeep/blob/master/doc/schema/permanode.md) mints identity as a signed random nonce — offline, parallel, collision-free, with no commit and no central authority. Every Softland id today is minted *by* Rama and meaningful only *inside* Rama (the back-arrow literally defines truth as "in Rama"). This framing says identity must be a content/address handle, not a kernel-owned row.

**Rank 2 — Convergence-as-truth-without-an-arbiter, and intention preservation as distinct from agreement.** The central Softland concurrency case (multiple LLM agents editing in parallel) is governed by distributed-consistency theory, not versioning. Sun et al. 1998 (https://www.cs.cityu.edu.hk/~jia/research/reduce98.pdf) prove the load-bearing fact: serializing operations into one agreed order achieves *convergence* but provably *destroys intention* (the ABCDE→A1CDE counterexample — every site agrees, every site is wrong). So the back-arrow gives an order, not a merge; a plain append-ordered Rama log + deterministic ETL fold can converge on something no agent intended when edits are concurrent and context-divergent. Local-First (https://www.inkandswitch.com/essay/local-first/) and CRDTs: The Hard Parts (https://martin.kleppmann.com/2020/07/06/crdt-hard-parts-hydra.html) reinforce: *convergence is not correctness* — even a successful merge can produce irreversible interleaving garbage. The kernel has no slot for "truth as a mathematical property of a merge function" nor for the **multi-value register** (eager *structural* plurality), distinct from C8's late *human* synthesis.

**Rank 3 — Perspectival / aspect-relative identity as a non-hierarchical lattice.** "Public form inhabitable by another mind" requires two minds to hold *the-same-thing-differently* without one overwriting the other. PROV-DM (https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-dm/) models exactly this: one real-world thing legitimately spawns many coexisting entities, each fixing a different set of aspects, related by `alternateOf` + `specializationOf` — a lattice with no privileged center ("a car in Boston may be a different entity from the same car in Cambridge"). Micropublications (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4530550/) independently arrives at the **holotype** with observer-relative "manufacturing tolerance" ("your similarity may not be my similarity"). The kernel's "one canonical container = one identity" (C1) and "crisp enumerable membership" (C6) cannot express graded, reader-relative sameness.

**Rank 4 — The capture problem: whether the public form accretes at all.** Softland's whole bet — "agent reasoning trails are first-class material" — rests on the unpriced assumption that the trail accretes for free. Conklin's 15-year gIBIS retrospective (http://www.cognexus.org/conklin-ht01.pdf) and Horner & Atwood (https://research.cs.vt.edu/ns/cs5724papers/1.motivatingreuse.tpgap.atwood.drationale.pdf) document that structured reasoning systematically *fails to accrete* because capture happens at the moment of peak cognitive load, the producer pays the cost while a later reader gets the benefit (Grudin's disparity), and there is no feedback signal that any preserved trail earned its keep. They name **facilitation labor / the technographer** as a first-class costed role with no kernel slot. Mitigant the run itself flags: an LLM may be the cheapest technographer ever — but that covers only the transcription half; the depersonalization/negotiation half is irreducibly social and the kernel has no model for who performs it across parallel agents.

**Rank 5 — Preservation contract / significant properties: lawful compression made into a first-class object.** Softland's own axiom is "semantic zoom is lawful compression — identity, provenance, and relations must survive across all three axes or the zoom is fake." NARA's Significant Properties framework (https://www.archives.gov/files/era/acera/pdf/significant-properties.pdf) shows that *is a significant-properties claim* — a transformation is authentic iff it honors a declared, typed, RANKED contract of which properties (content/context/structure/appearance/behavior) MUST survive and which are KNOWINGLY sacrificed, plus rationale. An immutable byte snapshot becomes unreadable as formats/environments drift, so "keep all the revisions" is necessary but explicitly NOT sufficient. The kernel has no first-class object asserting, per zoom/compression/migration, which dimensions are load-bearing — and the JIT transformation channel emits conversions but records no lossiness profile per tool.

**Rank 6 — Relevance & retrieval over preserved records: faithful ≠ usable.** Late-bound synthesis (C8) assumes preserved material is *findable and intelligible* at the later moment synthesis is attempted. Horner & Atwood marshal Wilson's situational relevance ("relevance is independent of truth"), Belkin's anomalous state of knowledge, and Karsenty's empirical ~41% ceiling on late-bound rationale retrieval. A maximally faithful trail can score near-zero on usefulness; worse, **disagreement carried by silence/omission** cannot be represented in an append-only log of *authored* events at all — so the kernel preserves the disagreements that were spoken and silently loses the ones that mattered most.

**Rank 7 — Defeasible acceptability semantics: a claim's standing is computed, not stored.** Micropublications models a claim's standing as a non-monotonic function of a live typed argument graph, with two distinct attack types — **rebut** (attack the conclusion) vs **undercut** (attack the warrant that licensed it, per Pollock; resolved by Dung-style acceptability semantics). We searched "discourse graph data model"; we should have searched "how is the acceptance status of a claim computed from an argument graph." The kernel can store and even preserve disagreement (C8) but has no notion that the graph *determines* a dialectical status that flips when a sibling claim arrives.

**Rank 8 — Patch theory / the algebra of merges: synthesis as evaluation.** Jujutsu's conflict model (https://docs.jj-vcs.dev/latest/technical/conflicts/) treats a conflict as a typed value `Merge<T>` (start term + alternating +/- diff pairs) of the *same type* as resolved content, so rebase/merge/diff work unchanged on it and conflicts **compose and cancel** — synthesis becomes a lawful mechanical consequence of simplification, not only a human act. This is the Darcs/Pijul patch-theory lineage and resonates directly with the project's category-theory worldview. It unifies C2 (projection) and C8 (preserved disagreement) the kernel currently keeps separate: a conflict *is* a projection of an algebra of diffs.

**Rank 9 — Schema/ontology evolution for immutable logs: the bytes are immutable but the meaning drifts.** Greg Young (https://leanpub.com/esversioning/read), Doomen (https://www.dennisdoomen.com/2017/06/the-ugly-of-event-sourcing-projection.html), and Snodgrass (https://www2.cs.arizona.edu/~rts/pubs/EDC.pdf, schema-vs-extension versioning) converge: a committed event is only as durable as the schema needed to read it. The kernel treats the log as self-interpreting bedrock; interpretation is a versioned, negotiated, expensive artifact (upcasting, tolerant reader, consumer-driven contracts) that ages out from under the data.

**Rank 10 — Operability of authenticated logs: get the truth node out of the read path.** Let's Encrypt's RFC 6962 EOL plan (https://letsencrypt.org/2025/08/14/rfc-6962-logs-eol) and Kafka compacted topics (https://www.conduktor.io/blog/kafka-compacted-topics-as-database) document that serving reads from the live truth node is what breaks at scale; the fix interposes immutable, content-addressed, CDN-cacheable tiles with embedded inclusion proofs. "UI reads Rama / back-arrow to one truth node" has no slot for a served, frozen, verifiable projection tier replicable by parties who don't run Rama.

### 3. Kernel Claims That Took Real Damage (genuine/partial only; stretches and manufactured strains excluded)

**C1 — "Identity = a stable container; change = a chain of immutable revisions." SEVERELY DAMAGED.** This is the most-strained claim in the run, hit genuinely from six independent directions:
- **PROV-DM (genuine, "costs"):** `wasInvalidatedBy` is a first-class primitive — an entity can be retracted/destroyed with *no successor and no derivation edge out*. A revision chain has no slot for "this is now wrong, nothing replaces it"; it must fake retraction as a tombstone revision, conflating "superseded by v2" with "invalid, full stop."
- **Datomic (genuine, "costs"):** change is recorded at *datom* (E-A-V-T) grain by accretion, with the entity *emergent* from datoms sharing an E — there is no stored container object at all. Container+revision granularity carries write amplification and false coupling of independently-changing fields that datom-grain avoids.
- **Perkeep (genuine, "strains"):** change is an unordered *set of signed claims folded last-write-wins by claimDate* — a per-signer LWW register, not a chain. The "chain" metaphor smuggles in a single ordering authority Perkeep deliberately omits; with multiple signers there is no canonical chain, only a trust-scoped fold.
- **CRDT-JSON / Figma (genuine, "strains"):** element identity is a per-op Lamport tuple over a *partial order*, not a position in a chain (Kleppmann); Figma keeps only the latest value per property with *no revision chain*, and undo/redo *mutate* history present-relatively.
- **nanopub (genuine, alternative):** identity *is* the content hash — there is no container, nothing to mutate, no enclosing handle that persists across change.
- *Flagged as overstated:* the jj-glossary "refutes" and Micropublications "refutes" verdicts were rated **partial** by the verifier (jj maintains predecessor links the cited glossary doesn't show; Micropublications' defeasibility argument is misattributed to C1 — it belongs to the orthogonal acceptability-semantics concept). The genuine residue from those two is narrower: divergent (1:many) identity and fuzzy holotype identity. *The container half of C1 largely survives; the linear-chain half and the no-retraction assumption are what break.*

**C2 — "The event log is PRIMARY; all materialized state is a PROJECTION." DAMAGED (cost, not refutation).** C2's ontology survives everywhere, but its framing of projection as "free, passive, rederivable" is punctured repeatedly:
- **Materialize (genuine, "strains"):** the projection *drifts* — output changes for identical inputs across engine versions and must be actively *self-corrected* by a read-back/diff/rewrite loop. The projection is a second stateful authority with the engine version as a hidden input, not a pure function of the log.
- **Datomic (genuine, "strains"):** "accumulate-only is a SEMANTIC property and is not the same as append-only, a STRUCTURAL property." Datomic is a production proof that a fully history-preserving system can make background-maintained *sorted indexes* — not a replayable append log — its durable primary. The kernel must decide whether replay-from-log is a structural commitment or merely the semantic property it actually wants.
- **Snodgrass (genuine, "costs"):** **snapshot reducibility** is the unstated correctness law — the materialized view at instant t must equal a plain non-temporal query over facts valid at t — and the *coordinated suite* reverses priority: *semantics* is primary, the log is one representation among several.
- **salsa-rs / Build Systems à la Carte (genuine/partial):** an efficient projection carries heavy DURABLE engine state (memo tables, observed dependency graph, trace store) not derivable from the log; "state is just a projection" is the *easy half stated as the whole problem."*
- **Doomen (genuine, "costs"):** projection is the *expensive* half in production — non-linear rebuild cost, in-flight wrong-state window, per-projector checkpoint, inter-projection dependency DAG.
- **jj glossary (genuine, "costs"):** two stacked durable primaries (content-addressed immutable commits + the operation/visibility DAG); a single-primary-log kernel cannot represent content that is durable independent of the event stream.
- **CT (genuine, "strains"):** "log is primary" is worthless to an external consumer without per-snapshot signed commitments and inclusion/consistency proofs binding the projection to the log.
- **NARA (genuine, "costs"):** the projection function is *not stable over time* — re-projecting an old local world may require an emulated environment, because meaning depends on external drifting context outside the log.
- **Sun et al. (genuine substance; verdict word "refutes" flagged as overstated toward "strains"):** convergence ≠ intention; a deterministic verbatim fold is not automatically faithful under concurrency.
- **Figma (genuine, "refutes" universality):** a world-class production system makes *state* canonical and the op-stream ephemeral — "log is primary" is a choice with cost, not a law (refutes universality, not appropriateness-for-Softland).

**C3 — "Rama is ground truth; back-arrow; UI reads Rama; nothing streams to UI as truth." DAMAGED but CONTINGENT.** Genuine hits, with a critical partial-defense:
- **CT (genuine; verdict "refutes" flagged overstated → really naive/incomplete):** authority should be conferred by *external verifiability* (signed root + proofs + gossip), not by being the designated container; a compromised/forked Rama is undetectable by any consumer reading it. The back-arrow concentrates exactly the trust CT spends 30 pages dismantling.
- **Adapton (genuine, "strains"):** there is a *second arrow* the kernel has no name for — observer *demand/force* is itself a first-class input that flows BACK into the derivation substrate and decides what gets computed/materialized. "UI reads Rama" is not passive; reading *is* the event that causes materialization.
- **Doomen (genuine, "strains"):** a materialized read-model can be confidently, *visibly wrong* mid-rebuild — the back-arrow needs an in-flight/freshness/position signal it currently lacks.
- **Let's Encrypt (genuine, "strains"):** keep the live truth node OUT of the read path; serve immutable cacheable tiles with embedded proofs.
- **Local-First / CRDT-JSON / nanopub (genuine):** coordination-free convergence and reader-side trust topologies are deployed alternatives showing C3's centralization is a contingent design choice.
- *Critical caveat:* Figma explicitly *vindicates* the central-authority half ("the server is the central authority" beats P2P), and the Local-First negative space (Weidner: you can drop CRDTs *if* you have a central log-reordering server) partly *defends* C3. The honest finding is **"C3 is contingent and needs a self-authenticating verifiability tier," not "C3 is wrong."**

**C4 — "Inferred structure is never accepted as truth at ingest (declared > inferred)." MILD DAMAGE (mostly refined, not broken).** C4 largely survives, but:
- **Conklin (genuine, "strains"):** the genuine hit is that *the cost of declaration is the unpriced, load-bearing variable.* "Declared > inferred" is fine as an epistemics rule but dangerous as a *workflow* rule — demanding live well-typed declaration from the people doing the work is exactly what killed gIBIS/DR-tool adoption. If Softland forces workers/agents to emit declared structure at ingest, it re-imports the capture bottleneck. (LLM-as-declarer is a mitigation, not a refutation.)
- **jj conflicts (partial):** the useful refinement is to *split* "inferred" into (a) inferred-*resolution*-as-fact (C4 rightly forbids) and (b) inferred-*unresolved-state* recorded as a typed conflict (which a deferred-disagreement kernel actually *needs*, and a strict reading of "never" wrongly prohibits).
- *Note:* Salsa's inferred-but-verifiable dependency graph actually *vindicates* C4 for Softland's setting — multi-agent LLM inferences are precisely *not* deterministically verifiable, so "inferred is not truth at ingest" holds exactly where Salsa's verification trick is unavailable. The Horner & Atwood C4 "strain" was rated **manufactured** and is excluded.

**C5 — "Two distinct times: arrival/transaction vs committed/valid." DAMAGED — the count is wrong (under-counted) and the labels are imprecise.**
- **Materialize (genuine, "strains"):** a real projection engine owns a **write frontier** — a per-view, monotonically-advancing "everything before t is final" boundary that advances independently of source-commit time. There are at least three times (arrival, source-commit, projection-frontier), and the UI observes the *projection's* timeline, not Rama's.
- **salsa-rs (genuine, "strains"):** a **verified_at** stamp ("last revision at which I confirmed this is still valid") is a third axis, neither arrival nor valid time; backdating further decouples valid-time (changed_at) from the revision that triggered recompute.
- **CT (genuine, "strains"):** the two times must be made *cryptographically accountable* — the SCT is a signed arrival receipt and the MMD a provable deadline; without them, "I gave you this at T" is unfalsifiable.
- **Snodgrass (partial, "naive"):** the canonical source for bitemporality shows the kernel *mislabels* the axes — "committed" in the literature is transaction time (stored-in-DB), glued by the kernel onto valid time (reality time), collapsing the very distinction it claims to honor. *The verifier downgraded the Datomic "refutes" to partial: Datomic collapses arrival and commit only because it has no arbitration step, which Softland does have — so two times are justified for Softland, but the under-count to three is the genuine finding.*

**C6 — "Groupings are first-class versioned objects; version the group, snapshot the members." MODERATE DAMAGE on the membership model.**
- **Micropublications (genuine, "strains"):** the holotype/similarity-group is an *equivalence class by meaning-proximity* with observer-relative, graded membership — "version the group, snapshot the members" presupposes crisp, enumerable, single-axis membership a reader-relative class does not provide. (Cleanest single C6 landing in the run.)
- **Snodgrass (partial):** C6's extension-versioning has no slot for *schema versioning* — which ontology version a snapshot was valid under, a known hard problem Softland (with its typed declared structure) silently inherits.
- **Ted Nelson / Xanadu (partial; "refutes" overstated):** the genuine residue is that "snapshot the members" *freezes* a configuration against later correction of the cited substance, where transclusion keeps one canonical span editable-in-place. The verifier rejected the "snapshot = byte copy → provenance fork" framing (a pinned revision-id pointer avoids both duplication and fork), so the surviving point is the *absence of live, single-canonical, edit-propagating co-presence at span granularity* — a missing capability, not proof C6 is wrong.

**C7 — "Minting is at COMMIT grain, not save grain; semantic time is a DAG." DAMAGED on the commit-grain half; the DAG half survives (and is repeatedly confirmed).**
- **Perkeep (genuine; "refutes" flagged overstated → contingent):** self-certifying identity (signed nonce) mints durable, collision-free identity *offline, in parallel, with no commit and no central authority.* For partitioned/parallel LLM agents this is the sharpest threat — a Rama-at-commit-only id cannot be created while partitioned, and two agents cannot independently mint non-colliding ids without a round-trip.
- **Greg Young (genuine, "costs"):** a committed event is only as durable as the schema needed to read it — the DAG records *when* a commit happened but not *which schema generation* it speaks, so replay across a semantic boundary is undefined without a separate, versioned upcasting layer the kernel budgets nothing for.
- **jj working copy (genuine, "strains"):** save-grain is not abolished, it is *relocated* — every command mints an *operation* (recorded in the op log) distinct from minting a content commit, and there is a mutable working-copy commit between save and finalized. The kernel's binary save-vs-commit is too coarse; jj needs three grains.
- **CRDT-JSON (genuine, "strains"):** DAG-time and commit-grain minting are *separable* — you can have DAG semantic time with save-grain minting and no commit boundary.
- **Figma (genuine, "naive"):** client-minted offline IDs and a single linear order ship fine; commit-grain minting would *block offline creation* — a real named cost.
- **Local-First / nanopub (genuine, "costs"/"strains"):** decentralized commit-grain inherits unbounded history and schema-skew (Local-First); publish-grain content-derived minting is decentralized (nanopub).
- *Confirmed:* the "semantic time is a DAG" half is independently endorsed by jj operation log, CRDT-JSON deps, and Mercurial's predecessor/successor graph. *Several verdicts attacking the DAG half (Datomic total-order, salsa-rs two-clocks, Doomen) were rated stretch/partial — total commit order and a semantic-causality DAG are compatible, not opposed.*

**C8 — "Preserved disagreement is first-class; synthesis is late-bound." HEAVILY DAMAGED as a universal LAW; survives as a domain-conditional VALUE.**
- **Figma (genuine, "refutes" universality):** per-property last-writer-wins *deliberately destroys* concurrent intent ("AB or BC but never ABC. That's ok with us because Figma is a design tool"). In some domains preserved disagreement is *corruption*, and the kernel has no slot for the per-type quality judgment "this concurrent combination is semantic garbage; collapse it to one survivor." (Cleanest, hardest-to-dodge refutation in the run.)
- **CRDTs: The Hard Parts (genuine, "costs"):** convergence ≠ correctness — two valid concurrent edits can deterministically converge to *irreversible* semantic garbage every party agrees on. C8 has no slot for "the merge converged AND destroyed intent."
- **Mercurial Changeset Evolution (genuine, "strains"):** content-divergence — two living successors of one identity — is documented field experience as a *corrosive instability* the system works hard to collapse ("only one version alive and visible is a goal"). Softland must specify why deliberately-preserved divergence is *habitable* where Mercurial found per-identity divergence corrosive.
- **Conklin (genuine, "costs"):** disagreement does *not* preserve itself — capturing competing positions usably required a live facilitator who depersonalizes conflict; and "late-bound synthesis" presumes you can cleanly restructure later, but "the resultant structures were hard to change."
- **Horner & Atwood (genuine, "strains"):** faithful preservation ≠ future usability (Wilson relevance "independent of truth," Karsenty 41% ceiling), and *omission-as-signal* (withheld disagreement, often the most consequential) cannot be recorded by an append-only log of authored events.
- *Refinements (not damage):* jj conflicts and PROV Bundles *confirm* C8's value and add the orthogonal "disagreement-as-algebra" and "provenance-of-provenance" mechanisms; Micropublications (partial) adds that C8 needs an *evaluation* primitive (what the live graph currently warrants believing), not just storage.

### 5. What Remains Unknown After This Run

The run was exhaustive along *one* axis — "is the kernel's data model for identity/change/time/truth/merge correct?" — but every one of the ~30 sources is a digital-systems artifact retrieved with the kernel's own CS vocabulary, producing a **homophily trap**: every disagreement found is *internal* to the paradigm that knowledge = discrete, addressable, diffable, attributable data on a software substrate. The unknown-unknown critic identifies what the search filters structurally excluded:

- **Modality monoculture.** Every claim was tested against text/claims/code. Untested: **continuous/time-based media** (merging two waveform edits is superposition weighted by intent, not LWW/OT/CRDT — direct stress on C1's "comparable discrete revisions" and C8's "what is disagreement between two mixes?"); **spreadsheets/cell-dataflow** (the most-used end-user computation model, incremental-native but with an identity/merge model unlike any PL/build engine studied); **opaque numeric/binary artifacts** (ML weights, embeddings, notebooks — non-diffable, non-mergeable; notebooks make execution order diverge from document order, an unaddressed stress on C5/C7); **spatial/geometric artifacts** — most acutely, **cartographic generalization**, a 200-year discipline of *lawful compression of spatial information across scale*, which is Softland's flagship semantic-zoom claim, never consulted despite the project being a spatial/WebGPU world.

- **Whole disciplines that solved these problems under non-CS names.** **Law** (bitemporal authoritative text predating Snodgrass by centuries — enacted vs in-force date, amendment-vs-repeal, point-in-time consolidation, stare decisis as institutionalized defeasible precedent). **Double-entry bookkeeping** (the original append-only ledger; **the period CLOSE as a sealing boundary** and **RECONCILIATION as forced periodic agreement** — primitives with no slot in a perpetually-open log). **Textual criticism / philology** (stemmatics, the critical apparatus/variorum — "preserved disagreement, late-bound synthesis" practiced for two millennia). **FRBR/library science** (Work/Expression/Manifestation/Item — a deployed four-level identity model richer than container+chain). **Estimation & control theory / Bayesian belief** (Kalman fusion treats truth as the *uncertainty-weighted fusion* of noisy concurrent observations — the rigorous answer to the back-arrow's multi-observation problem that is neither accept/reject nor LWW nor CRDT join; the kernel only ever *orders* observations, never *fuses* them by confidence; belief-as-distribution answers C4's binary declared/inferred with a graded model).

- **Failure modes never seeded.** **Mandated erasure** (GDPR right-to-be-forgotten in direct conflict with append-only immutability; crypto-shredding is a contested workaround) and **authorization-scoped partial visibility** — C1/C2 immutability has no slot for "this must become unrecoverable now," and the run assumes a globally readable log. **Irreversible real-world side effects** (money moved, emails sent, processes spawned) — jj's "undo as a forward operation" works *only* because a repo is pure data; some accepted KernelEvents commit to physical reality no log-undo can retract.

- **Adversarial epistemic actors.** The whole run assumes a *cooperative fault model* (honest-but-buggy). Untested: **data poisoning** (an agent injecting plausible-but-false claims), **Sybil attribution** corrupting provenance/the back-arrow, and a **disagreement DoS** where C8's cheap first-class disagreement becomes a flooding attack surface that makes synthesis impossible. CT covered Byzantine log *operators* but never Byzantine *content*.

- **Pre-digital / non-Western systems that are near-exact realizations of Softland's vision.** **Aboriginal songlines** (knowledge encoded *in landscape* — literally the "place" thesis, with no log and no write). **Talmudic machloket** (disagreement preserved as sacred and deliberately *never* resolved; the daf as spatial commentary-layered projection — a counterexample to C8's "late-bound synthesis" assumption that synthesis is the telos). **Islamic isnad** (graded chain-of-transmission = provenance + per-transmitter trust-scoring + defeasibility, perfected ~1200 years ago — a working trust-scoped fold). **Indigenous data sovereignty (CARE not just FAIR)** — collective/custodial knowledge governed by kinship/initiation, against PROV's individual-agent attribution; the back-arrow's "extract all observations into one truth store" is structurally *extractive*.

- **The legitimacy critique (no angle G).** Every angle attacks *mechanism*; none attacks *desirability or governance*. Forcing knowledge into a fixed typed schema (Q→C→E→D→R→F) is an exercise of ontological power that can erase tacit/situated/embodied knowledge (Polanyi: "we know more than we can tell"; standpoint/feminist epistemology). "Minimize private reconstruction / make structure public" assumes all valuable understanding *can* be made public without distortion, and Rama-as-ground-truth centralizes epistemic authority (Wikipedia's governance wars are a live instance of contested truth-arbitration at scale).

### 7. Frame Verdict

Versioning is the right root for exactly one thing — the immutable, accumulate-only history of truth objects — and it should keep that throne, validated independently by Datomic, Certificate Transparency, Perkeep, nanopublications, and Jujutsu. But the kernel mistook a *spine* for a *skeleton*: it routed identity, projection, transformation-meaning, claim-standing, and truth-authority all through versioning, and each has a stronger native root. **Identity** wants *addressing* (content-addressed, self-certifying, transcludable handles a foreign mind can verify offline — the gap that most directly threatens "public form inhabitable by another mind"). The **log→state→UI path** wants *incremental computation* ("state is just a projection" is the easy half stated as the whole problem — it needs its own dependency DAG, trace store, early cutoff, and verification-time). **Reasoning trails** want *provenance + defeasible argumentation* (attributed statements with non-monotonic standing, not revisions of a container). **Preserved disagreement and semantic zoom** want *preservation* (capture economics + a significant-properties contract — Softland's own "lawful compression" axiom is a preservation claim with no kernel object). The **back-arrow (C3)** wants *trust* (authority as a verifiable artifact, not a location) — contingently, since central authority is also a defensible coordination trade. And the **parallel-agent merge case** is governed by coordination/consistency theory, where an arrival-ordered log gives an order but not a merge. The sharpest single correction: C1's "stable container + chain of immutable revisions" is the most-strained phrase in the kernel — "container" forces copy-on-membership (kill via addressing/transclusion), "chain" forces a single ordering authority (kill via DAG / op-log / CRDT-fold).

**Bottom line:** Versioning is not the root primitive — it is one of roughly five co-equal roots (versioning, addressing, incremental-computation, provenance, trust/preservation); demote it from THE root to the root of *change-in-content*, and give identity its own addressing primitive.

- **Change/history of the truth objects (C1 immutability, C7 append-only/accumulate-only substrate, WorldDepot revisions)** → `versioning`

Datomic, RFC6962/CT, Perkeep, nanopub, jj and Salsa all independently keep an immutable / accumulate-only substrate, and CT states the append-only Merkle structure cryptographically (a later version is a superset of any earlier one) — direct support for the immutable-revision spine. The one refinement that survived: Datomic's distinction that accumulate-only is a SEMANTIC property while append-only is a STRUCTURAL one. The kernel fuses these in 'the event log is primary'; it must decide whether replay-from-log is a structural commitment or whether only the semantic property (nothing overwritten, every assertion recoverable) is actually wanted — Datomic, Kafka-compaction and CT-EOL all show the structural live-log can be an expensive, bounded-storage liability while the semantic property is kept via background-maintained indexes / frozen content-addressed tiles.

- **Object identity (C1 'stable container'; C6 group identity / 'snapshot the members'; transferable local worlds)** → `addressing`
  - Most-strained claim of the run, attacked genuinely from three sides. Content-addressed self-certifying identity (nanopub trusty URI = hash of content; Perkeep = signed random nonce minted offline, collision-free, no commit) and jj's change-id/commit-id split show identity should be a content/address handle that travels with the bytes and is verifiable without contacting the minting authority — not a Rama-minted container row meaningful only inside Rama. For Softland's purpose ('inhabitable by another mind,' parallel/offline LLM agents), a Rama-only id cannot be created while partitioned and cannot be verified by a foreign mind. Ted Nelson's transclusion adds the missing third membership mode: by-reference co-presence (one canonical address transcluded into N contexts), against C6's snapshot-copy, so a claim cited by three agents stays ONE editable thing. 'Container + chain' forces a copy at every membership and presumes a single ordering authority that the address model does not need.

- **Log → materialized state → UI (C2 projection path; PStates / Electric rendering; multi-agent re-derivation of synthesis & summary views)** → `incremental-computation`
  - Angle C lands cleanly and repeatedly (Build Systems à la Carte, Salsa durable-incrementality, Materialize self-correcting views, Adapton, salsa-rs — multiple genuine verdicts). 'State is just a projection of the log' names the easy half; a correct AND minimal projection needs its own data-dependency DAG (distinct from the causal/commit DAG), a persistent trace store not derivable from the log, EARLY CUTOFF (stop propagation when a re-derived value is identical), and a VERIFICATION-time stamp (Salsa's verified_at, neither arrival nor valid time). Empirically Salsa pays ~300ms whole-graph revalidation on any unrelated edit; Materialize shows the projection DRIFTS across engine versions and must be self-corrected — i.e. the projection is a second stateful authority, not a free pure replay. For parallel LLM agents re-deriving syntheses over revised claims, without early cutoff every upstream edit cascades a full re-derivation. Versioning does not govern recomputation; incremental computation does.

- **Reasoning trails / discourse-graph claims & argument (Q→C→E→D→R→F; agent reasoning as first-class material; attribution & responsibility)** → `provenance`
  - Micropublications, PROV-DM and nanopublications converge: the load-bearing primitive is an ATTRIBUTED STATEMENT carrying support/challenge edges whose standing is non-monotonic (defeasible — rebut vs undercut), where change happens by issuing a NEW immutable statement that defeats/supersedes an old one rather than revving a container, plus first-class attribution/delegation (PROV actedOnBehalfOf retained-responsibility chains) and invalidation-WITHOUT-successor (wasInvalidatedBy, symmetric to creation). PROV also supplies perspectival/fixed-aspect identity as a lattice (alternateOf/specializationOf) and provenance-of-provenance (Bundles) — the trail itself is an attributable entity. A 'container + revision chain' cannot natively express defeat edges, third-party-authored disagreement, terminal retraction, or per-aspect identity. Argument/provenance is structurally not a versioning problem.

- **Preserved disagreement + late-bound synthesis (C8)** → `preservation`
  - Two genuine pressures plus one refute. (a) Capture economics (Conklin gIBIS retrospective, Horner & Atwood): structured disagreement does NOT accrete for free — capture cost falls on the producer at peak load, with no feedback on whether it was worth it; and faithful preservation ≠ future usability (Wilson relevance 'independent of truth'; Karsenty ~41% retrieval ceiling; omission-as-signal — withheld disagreement, often the most consequential, cannot be recorded by an append-only log of AUTHORED events). (b) Figma genuine refute: disagreement-preservation is domain-conditional — sometimes a concurrent combination is semantic garbage to collapse to one survivor ('AB or BC but never ABC'). So C8 is a value, not a law; it needs a preservation/curation discipline (compaction, significant-properties) AND a separate evaluation primitive (defeasible acceptability — what the live graph currently warrants believing), neither of which versioning supplies. Mitigant noted: LLM agents lower the human capture-bottleneck, but the relevance/omission gap and the eager-vs-garbage distinction remain.

- **Semantic zoom as lawful compression / the JIT transformation channel** → `preservation`
  - NARA Significant Properties is the exact unowned primitive. A transformation is authentic iff it honors a declared, typed, RANKED contract of which properties (content/context/structure/appearance/behavior) MUST survive and which are KNOWINGLY sacrificed, plus rationale and cross-reference to source — and an immutable byte snapshot becomes unreadable as formats/environments drift, so 'keep all the revisions' is necessary but explicitly NOT sufficient. Softland's own axiom 'identity, provenance and relations must survive across all three axes or the zoom is fake' IS a significant-properties claim, but the kernel has no first-class object asserting, per zoom/compression/migration, which dimensions are load-bearing. Versioning records THAT bits changed; preservation governs whether the meaning survived the change. Also exposes that the JIT channel emits conversions but records no lossiness profile per transformation tool.

- **Truth as a node everything reads/writes (C3 back-arrow: 'Rama is ground truth; UI reads Rama; nothing streams to UI as truth')** → `trust`
  - RFC6962/CT, Let's-Encrypt CT-EOL, nanopub and Perkeep: authority should be a property of a VERIFIABLE ARTIFACT (signed tree head + O(log n) inclusion/consistency proofs + cross-party gossip / content-addressed self-authentication), not of a location. For 'understanding inhabitable by ANOTHER mind,' a foreign mind (or a parallel LLM agent) must be able to verify the trail it received is the trail that was committed and was never silently rewritten — the back-arrow concentrates exactly the unverifiable trust CT was built to dismantle, and has no slot for proofs an outsider can check without inhabiting Rama. CT-EOL adds the operability warning: keep the live truth node OUT of the read path; serve immutable content-addressed tiles with embedded proofs. CRITICAL qualification: Figma and the local-first negative-space (Weidner: drop CRDTs IF you have a central log-reordering server) partly DEFEND a single authority as a deliberate consistency-for-coordination trade — so the finding is 'C3 is contingent and needs a self-authenticating verifiability tier,' not 'C3 is wrong.'

- **Parallel / offline multi-agent editing and merge of divergent histories (the central Softland concurrency case)** → `other`
  - This is distributed-consistency / coordination theory (CRDT/OT, strong eventual consistency, CALM), NOT versioning. Sun et al. (1998) prove the load-bearing fact: serializing operations into one arrival-ordered log yields convergence but NOT intention preservation (ABCDE→A1CDE — every site agrees, every site is wrong). So the back-arrow gives an order, not a merge; a plain append-ordered Rama log + deterministic ETL fold can converge on something no agent intended when edits are concurrent and context-divergent. CRDTs Hard Parts reinforces: convergence ≠ correctness, even after merge (irreversible interleaving garbage). The orthogonal primitive the kernel has no slot for is convergence-as-truth-without-an-arbiter (and the multi-value register = eager STRUCTURAL plurality, distinct from C8's late HUMAN synthesis). The kernel must either add a transform/merge layer with explicit pre/postconditions (meaning bound to generation context) or forbid concurrent context-divergent edits. Frame tagged 'other' = coordination/consistency, the field the kernel never named.

- **The ACTS of versioning themselves (undo, rebase, regroup, restore; 'view of what is currently true/visible')** → `versioning`
  - jj operation log + glossary show versioning SURVIVES here but must go second-order: a meta-log whose subject is another versioning act, where undo/restore are ordinary forward appends, identity is a stable change-id with a changing commit-id, and the op-DAG itself merges on concurrency. The genuine landing is that the kernel's single content-event log has no slot for an append-only, undoable, separately-versioned 'what is currently visible/canonical' layer — which is precisely what users actually undo, and where save-grain genuinely lives (relocated, not abolished — so C7's binary save-vs-commit is too coarse). This EXTENDS versioning rather than replacing it: keep versioning as root, add the meta/operation log.


## Appendix C — Round-3 seed queries (field-namer)

### Schema & ontology evolution for immutable / append-only data (upcasting, tolerant reader, consumer-driven contract versioning)
Recurred emphatically in Greg Young 'Versioning in an Event Sourced System' and Dennis Doomen 'The Ugly of Event Sourcing', reinforced by Snodgrass's schema-versioning-vs-extension-versioning distinction. The reads state outright the search term should have been 'event schema evolution upcasting tolerant reader' rather than anything about identity or commit grain. Angle D covered event-sourcing mechanics and temporal DBs, but none of the six addresses the event VOCABULARY's MEANING drifting over time while the bytes stay immutable — i.e. a committed event is only as durable as the schema needed to read it. The kernel (C2/C7) treats the log as self-interpreting bedrock; this field shows interpretation is a versioned, negotiated, expensive artifact that ages out from under the data, with a real cited academic anchor (Overeem et al. 2021).

> **R3 query:** event schema evolution and upcasting for immutable event logs: tolerant reader pattern, consumer-driven / message-contract versioning, ontology drift, and the cost of reinterpreting an append-only log when the meaning of an event type changes

### Compaction, tombstone GC & retention policy for accumulate-only stores
Recurred across CRDTs: The Hard Parts (tombstone GC needs consensus, ~100:1 metadata blowup), Kafka Compacted Topics (compaction as a continuously-running truth-narrowing process), Local-First (CRDT histories 'pile up, can't easily be truncated'), CRDT-JSON (presence-sets persist forever), and Datomic (accumulate-only vs append-only). None of the six angles prices bounding the history: all of A–F assume the log/revision-chain is simply kept. The reads name retention/compaction as a first-class tunable dial governing the half-life of revisions — which revisions stay forever, which collapse to head, when tombstones are physically removed — a slot the kernel has nowhere to express, and a direct tension with C1's full-revision-history-forever bet.

> **R3 query:** garbage collection, log compaction, and retention policy for accumulate-only and CRDT stores: tombstone removal without consensus, causal-metadata compaction, snapshotting strategies, bounding unbounded history while preserving queryability

### The capture problem: economics & cognitive cost of structured knowledge (incremental formalization, 'formality considered harmful', facilitation / technography, Grudin's disparity)
Surfaced powerfully in Conklin's gIBIS 15-year retrospective and Horner & Atwood's design-rationale barriers survey. This is the clearest non-six field: every one of A–F concerns how structure is represented ONCE IT EXISTS, while this discipline studies whether structured reasoning accretes AT ALL — capture happens at the moment of peak cognitive load, the producer pays the cost while a later reader gets the benefit (Grudin disparity), and there is no feedback signal that any preserved trail earned its keep. It names facilitation labor / the technographer as a first-class costed role with no kernel slot, and the Softland 'agent reasoning trails are first-class material' bet rests entirely on the unpriced assumption that the trail accretes for free.

> **R3 query:** the design-rationale capture problem and incremental formalization: cognitive overhead of formal representation, premature structuring, formality considered harmful, Grudin's who-pays-who-benefits groupware disparity, facilitation / technography labor, and whether an LLM can be the technographer

### Information science: relevance & retrieval over preserved records (situational relevance, anomalous state of knowledge, retrieval overload)
Surfaced in Horner & Atwood via Wilson's situational relevance ('relevance is independent of truth'), Belkin's anomalous state of knowledge, and Karsenty's empirical 41% ceiling on late-bound rationale retrieval. None of the six models whether preserved material is FINDABLE and INTELLIGIBLE at the later moment synthesis is attempted. This directly attacks the late-bound-synthesis bet (C8): faithful preservation does not equal future usability, and a maximally faithful trail can score near-zero on relevance — plus disagreement carried by silence/omission cannot be represented in an append-only log of authored events at all.

> **R3 query:** situational relevance and information retrieval over archived knowledge: relevance as independent of truth, anomalous state of knowledge, retrieval overload of accumulated records, and the producer/consumer relevance gap in reusing preserved reasoning

### Defeasible / non-monotonic reasoning & abstract argumentation acceptability semantics (Dung frameworks, Pollock rebut vs undercut)
Surfaced in Micropublications, which models a claim's standing as a non-monotonic function of a live typed argument graph with two distinct attack types (REBUT a conclusion vs UNDERCUT the warrant). Angle E targeted the provenance/argument DATA MODEL (the discourse-graph schema), but this is a distinct discipline: the COMPUTATION of a claim's dialectical status from the graph. The read says explicitly: we searched 'discourse graph data model' but should have searched 'how is the acceptance status of a claim computed from an argument graph'. The kernel can store and even preserve disagreement (C8) but has no notion that the graph DETERMINES a status that flips when a sibling claim arrives.

> **R3 query:** abstract argumentation frameworks and defeasible reasoning: Dung acceptability semantics, Pollock rebutting vs undercutting defeaters, non-monotonic computation of a claim's dialectical status from a typed support/challenge graph

### Aspect-relative / perspectival identity & entity resolution (identity criteria, fixed-aspect lattice, graded sameness)
Two independent provenance/claim sources converge on identity being plural and observer-relative: PROV-DM's alternateOf + specializationOf lattice (one real-world thing legitimately spawns many coexisting entities each fixing different aspects, no privileged center) and Micropublications' holotype with observer-relative 'manufacturing tolerance' ('your similarity may not be my similarity'). None of the six interrogates identity CRITERIA themselves — C1 assumes one canonical container = one identity, and C6 assumes crisp enumerable membership. This is the field of when two records are 'the same thing seen at different fixities', directly load-bearing for 'understanding inhabitable by another mind' where two minds hold the-same-thing-differently without overwriting.

> **R3 query:** perspectival and aspect-relative identity in provenance and entity resolution: PROV alternateOf / specializationOf lattices, fixed-aspect entities, graded observer-relative sameness, and identity criteria for when two records denote the same thing

### Patch theory / algebra of merges (Darcs & Pijul, conflicts as first-class composable cancelling values, category-theoretic merge)
Surfaced sharply in the jj conflicts technical doc: a conflict is a typed value Merge<T> (start term + alternating +/- diff pairs) of the SAME type as resolved content, so rebase/merge/diff work unchanged on it and conflicts COMPOSE and CANCEL — synthesis becomes a lawful mechanical consequence of simplification, not only a human act. Angle A treats merge as convergence and B as the operation layer, but neither names conflict-as-monoid / synthesis-as-evaluation-of-an-algebra. This is a distinct mathematical lineage (Darcs/Pijul patch theory, category-theoretic merge, lattice/join semantics) that strongly resonates with the project's stated category-theory worldview, and unifies C2 (projection) with C8 (preserved disagreement) the kernel currently keeps separate.

> **R3 query:** patch theory and the algebra of merges: Darcs and Pijul first-class conflicts, conflicts as composable cancelling values, category-theoretic / lattice merge semantics, and synthesis as evaluation of a merge algebra rather than a manual act

### Operability of authenticated logs: read-path / write-path serving separation (tiled transparency logs, CDN-cacheable verifiable artifacts)
Recurred in Let's Encrypt's RFC 6962 EOL plan (freeze the live Merkle log into immutable content-addressed 256-entry tiles served by dumb CDN, with inclusion proofs embedded so readers verify WITHOUT touching the authority), echoed by Kafka compacted topics (materialize-then-read; you cannot serve reads from the live log at scale) and CT itself. Angle F flagged TRUST/verifiability as a primitive but not the OPERABILITY cost of serving truth at scale. The recurring lesson is to get the truth node OUT of the read path via a third thing — a served, frozen, content-addressed, verifiable projection decoupled from the live store and cacheable by parties who don't run it — a tier the kernel's 'UI reads Rama / back-arrow to one truth node' (C2/C3) has no slot for.

> **R3 query:** tiled transparency logs and read-path / write-path separation for authenticated append-only data: static CT / tlog-tiles serving, CDN-cacheable content-addressed verifiable artifacts with embedded inclusion proofs, and getting the truth node out of the read path


## Appendix D — What Round 2 was STILL built to miss (unknown-unknown critic)

The run is exhaustive along ONE axis: "is the kernel's data model for identity/change/time/truth/merge correct?" Every one of the ~30 sources is a digital-systems artifact (arXiv, W3C, IETF, ACM, engineering blogs) retrieved by search terms lifted from the kernel's own CS vocabulary (CRDT, event sourcing, incremental computation, provenance model, transparency log). This creates a homophily trap: the literature returned shares the kernel's ontological premise — knowledge = discrete, addressable, diffable, attributable data sitting on a software substrate — so every disagreement found is INTERNAL to that paradigm. Five structural filters were operating: (1) vocabulary lock-in — disciplines that solved the same problems under non-CS names are invisible; (2) digital-substrate assumption — pre-digital and oral systems cannot appear in a digital-artifact search space; (3) modality monoculture — text/claims/code exemplars and editing/log framing bias toward discrete document modalities; (4) falsification-on-mechanism not on desirability — every angle attacks HOW the kernel represents truth, none attacks WHETHER understanding should be made public/permanent at all (there is no angle G); (5) cooperative-fault assumption — agents are modeled as honest-but-buggy, never strategic/malicious, so the only "adversarial" source (CT) is about infrastructure integrity, not adversarial epistemic content. The gaps below are what those filters excluded; none duplicates a source the run actually covered.

- **[modality]** Continuous / time-based media (audio, video, sensor signals). Merging two concurrent edits of a waveform or mix is superposition/mixing weighted by intent, not LWW, OT, or CRDT join; there is no natural diffable 'revision' or addressable 'claim'. Non-destructive editing and edit-decision-lists exist but operate over continuous content, not discrete nodes. Directly stresses C1 (chain of immutable revisions assumes comparable discrete units) and C8 (what does 'preserved disagreement' mean between two continuous mixes?).
  - *Why structurally missed:* Angle A was seeded on collaborative TEXT editing and discrete-data concurrency; the search terms (CRDT, OT, JSON/RDF datatypes, character interleaving) only return systems whose unit is a discrete, addressable, comparable element. A waveform has no character index, so it never matches the retrieval queries.

- **[modality]** Spreadsheets / cell-dataflow grids — the most widely used end-user computation model on earth. Identity is a cell ADDRESS, change is a formula re-derivation, and a 'conflict' lives in a formula dependency graph (Google Sheets concurrent edit, structural-edit-vs-value-edit collisions). It is incremental-computation-native but with an identity and merge model entirely unlike the PL/build engines studied.
  - *Why structurally missed:* Angle C framed incremental computation as 'build systems / self-adjusting computation', returning PL-and-systems literature (Salsa, Adapton, Bazel, Materialize). The spreadsheet is owned by the HCI / end-user-programming literature under different terms, so the native incremental-dataflow modality was filtered out by the systems-framing of the query.

- **[modality]** Opaque numerical / binary artifacts: ML model weights, datasets, embeddings, and computational notebooks. Weights are non-diffable and non-mergeable; identity = provenance + hyperparameters + training data (DVC, model registries). Notebooks make execution order diverge from document order with hidden mutable state — a direct, unaddressed stress on C5 (two times) and C7 (commit grain / semantic DAG: which time orders a re-run cell?).
  - *Why structurally missed:* The kernel's exemplar content is text/claims/code, and every angle's verbs (diff, merge, revise, fold) presuppose content that is comparable and addressable at fine grain. Large opaque numeric blobs fail those predicates and so match none of the seed queries; 'reproducibility' and 'model lineage' are MLops vocabulary outside the seeded fields.

- **[discipline]** Spatial / geometric artifacts: parametric CAD models (merge = constraint re-solve over geometry/topology) and collaborative GIS (OpenStreetMap's real changeset-conflict model for shared geographic truth). Most acute: cartographic GENERALIZATION is a 200-year discipline of lawful compression of spatial information across scale — which is exactly Softland's own 'semantic zoom = lawful compression' claim, never consulted.
  - *Why structurally missed:* Despite Softland being a spatial/WebGPU world, all six angles are data-consistency/provenance framed. Terms like 'map generalization', 'Topfer's radical law', 'GIS conflation', 'parametric feature-tree merge' are not in the kernel's CS vocabulary, so the closest real-world analogue to the project's flagship claim never surfaced.

- **[discipline]** Law and legislation: bitemporal authoritative text predating Snodgrass by centuries (enacted-date vs in-force-date), amendment-vs-repeal as distinct from mutation, point-in-time consolidated law (legislation.gov.uk runs a genuine production bitemporal model), and stare decisis as institutionalized defeasible precedent. The deepest existing theory of 'the meaning of a committed text drifts' (C7 / schema evolution).
  - *Why structurally missed:* Angle D searched 'temporal databases / event sourcing', a CS corpus. Legal informatics encodes the identical machinery under 'in-force dating', 'consolidation', 'commencement', 'redline' — non-overlapping vocabulary — so a field that has run bitemporal authoritative text for centuries was unreachable.

- **[discipline]** Double-entry bookkeeping: the original append-only immutable ledger (15th century). Corrections are reversal/adjusting entries, never mutation; the period CLOSE is a sealing boundary past which history is frozen; RECONCILIATION is forced periodic agreement between independent records. The 'close' and 'reconciliation' primitives have no slot in the kernel's perpetually-open log.
  - *Why structurally missed:* Searching 'event sourcing' surfaces its software re-inventions (Kafka, Datomic) rather than the 600-year-old practice they descend from. Accounting vocabulary (ledger close, trial balance, adjusting entry, audit trail) was never queried, so the pre-digital source of the entire paradigm — and its sealing/reconciliation concepts — was invisible.

- **[discipline]** Scholarly textual criticism / philology: stemmatics (reconstructing a tree from divergent manuscript witnesses), the critical apparatus / variorum edition (a structured, durable representation of variant readings), and genetic editing of a work's drafts. This is 'preserved disagreement, late-bound synthesis' (C8) and 'identity of a work across witnesses' (C1) practiced by humans for two millennia.
  - *Why structurally missed:* Angle E was scoped as 'provenance & argumentation', returning CS and biomedical claim-graph models (micropublications, nanopublications, PROV). The humanities discipline that literally formalizes preserved variant disagreement (apparatus criticus) uses philological terms and lives in a non-technical corpus the search never touched.

  - *Why structurally missed:* Every seeded angle models truth as a discrete value to be ordered, accepted, or merged — never as a probability distribution. C1/C2 immutability has no slot for 'this must become unrecoverable now', and the run assumes a globally readable log.
  - *Why structurally missed:* Garbage collection appeared only as a PERFORMANCE concern (CRDT tombstones, Kafka compaction). No angle was seeded on legal/ethical compliance or content-level security, so deletion-as-obligation and authorization-scoped visibility — failure modes that contradict the kernel's core immutability — could not surface.

- **[adversarial-position]** Strategic/malicious epistemic actors: data poisoning (an LLM or agent injecting plausible-but-false claims), Sybil attribution corrupting provenance/the back-arrow, and a 'disagreement DoS' where C8's cheap first-class disagreement becomes a flooding attack surface that makes synthesis impossible.   - *Why structurally missed:* The seeding assumes a cooperative fault model — agents are honest-but-buggy. Even the one security source (CT) is about infrastructure equivocation, not adversaries gaming the epistemic content. With no angle assuming a strategic opponent, content-poisoning and disagreement-spam attacks on C4/C8 had no entry point.

  - *Why structurally missed:* All operation-log/undo sources are VCS or in-memory pure-data systems where everything is reversible by construction. - **[non-western-or-pre-digital]** Oral, ritual, and spatially-encoded memory systems: Aboriginal songlines (knowledge encoded IN landscape — literally Softland's 'place' thesis), Vedic recitation (built-in error-correcting redundancy via multiple recitation patterns — a pre-digital ECC for cultural memory), the memory palace, and the Zettelkasten as pre-digital block-referencing/transclusion.   - *Why structurally missed:* The search space is digital artifacts (arXiv/W3C/IETF/ACM). Systems whose substrate is human memory, ritual performance, or physical landscape produce no indexable digital paper, so they are categorically unreachable by the retrieval mechanism — even though songlines are the closest real instance of Softland's spatial-knowledge vision.

Indigenous data sovereignty (CARE, not just FAIR) holds knowledge as collective/custodial with access governed by kinship/initiation — versus PROV's individual-agent attribution; the back-arrow's 'extract all observations into one truth store' is structurally extractive.
  - *Why structurally missed:* The Western-academic source pool (ACM/W3C/arXiv/IETF) is a specific cultural corpus that excludes these traditions, and 'provenance' / 'disagreement' were queried only in their CS and biomedical senses. Collective-ownership and never-resolve-disagreement framings contradict the run's implicit individualist, synthesis-eventually assumptions and so had no query path.

There is no angle G ('should this exist / who controls it / what does it erase'), so epistemological, political-economy, and tacit-knowledge objections to the entire public-form-permanent-record enterprise had no place to enter.

## R3 Appendix E — What the METHOD itself cannot reach (the reflexive finding)

A cross-disciplinary WEB-SEARCH run can only reach knowledge that has already been (a) textualized, (b) indexed, (c) rendered in a search-dominant language (overwhelmingly English), and (d) made public. That filter systematically excludes exactly the knowledge whose nature is to resist it, and the exclusion is structural, not a matter of running more searches: (1) Genuinely tacit/embodied knowledge that produces no indexable text — a master potter's hands, a trader's gut, a surgeon's feel, the actual doing of a craft transmitted only by apprenticeship. The run could read Polanyi ABOUT tacit knowledge but could never reach the tacit knowledge itself, because by definition it leaves no text. (2) Living practitioners' working knowledge, which exists in people, not pages, and is never published in the form in which it operates. (3) Knowledge that communities deliberately restrict or refuse to digitize — secret-sacred, initiation-gated, custodial (the very thing CARE, songlines, and Strauss all DESCRIBED). The web holds only the exoteric shell; the esoteric core is absent by the tradition's own design, so the run retrieves a hollowed report of it and may not even know the center is missing. (4) Negative/ineffable knowledge (apophatic theology, Zen) that resists propositional capture in any medium. (5) The not-yet — knowledge still in formation that exists as no document. The deepest version of the limit is reflexive and is itself the strongest finding about the kernel's 'public form' thesis: the method — fan out searches, pull every source into one centralized, legible, English-language report — IS an instance of C2 (one primary store) and C3 (the back-arrow: everything streamed into one truth sink). It therefore cannot, even in principle, faithfully represent knowledge whose whole character is to live distributed, embodied, restricted, situated, and untranslatable — it can only extract a lossy, decontextualized projection of it, which is precisely the dispossession Scott, CARE, Haraway, and the songlines tradition warned against. The run demonstrated this by 'reaching' Aboriginal songlines only through a settler academic's book — converting restricted, terrain-bound, performance-only knowledge into open indexed prose, re-performing the extractive centralization under critique. So the method's blind spot and the kernel's blind spot are the same blind spot: both assume that what matters can be made public, legible, and centralized without losing what made it matter. Any knowledge for which that assumption is false is invisible to BOTH — which means this entire research program is constitutionally unable to find its own most important counterexamples, and the absence of a finding can never be taken as the absence of the thing.

## R5 Appendix B — Conflicts between adopted primitives (design forks)

- **Colimit/pushout (Leinster) vs Judgment-aggregation 'synthesis preserves plurality' (List-Pettit) / CRDT join — all on C8 synthesis**
  - Category theory models synthesis as a COLIMIT — a forced quotient that COLLAPSES disagreeing elements into one object (asserts s(x)∼t(x)). But the kernel's intended synthesis-from-disagreement (SOUL, List-Pettit) and the CRDT join both want synthesis to HOLD both sides without identifying them. Colimit is the right operation for preserving disagreement (retain the diagram, don't take the colimit) but the WRONG operation for the positive act of synthesis. Two disciplines prescribe incompatible structure for the word 'synthesis'.

- **CRDT join-semilattice (no arbiter, coordination-free) vs CALM / Bailis I-confluence (synthesis is non-monotone → arbiter mandatory) — both on C8/C2 merge**
  - CRDT says concurrent claims merge by least-upper-bound with NO coordination (Strong Eventual Consistency). CALM/Bailis say any non-monotone operation (the collapse-synthesis, claim arbitration, deletion) PROVABLY requires a coordination seal. Resolvable by partition (union-preserve = monotone/free; collapse-synthesis = non-monotone/sealed) but the kernel must not let the CRDT 'no arbiter needed' license leak onto the synthesis step where an arbiter is mandatory.

- **Vector-clock partial order (Lamport) vs BCDM linear-total-order time — both on C7 'semantic time'**
  - Vector clocks insist concurrency = incomparability and must NOT be linearized (else false causality between parallel agents — Lamport converse-failure). BCDM requires a LINEAR total order for snapshot/timeslice to be well-defined. So 'semantic time' cannot simultaneously be a partial-order DAG (for honest concurrency) and a linear axis (for snapshot reducibility). Danto's narrative truth-maker compounds this: it is 'later in real/total time' yet need not be a causal descendant, so a concurrent event can be a later truth-maker — breaking any attempt to localize description-growth to the causal up-set.

- **Vector clock 'DAG = causal happened-before' vs Pearl d-separation / Global Markov bound — both on C7**
  - The vector-clock primitive names the DAG as CAUSAL precedence. The causal-inference primitives prove happened-before is NOT structural causation and the DAG cannot license any causal or conditional-independence claim without a SEPARATE declared structural-causal graph plus faithfulness. Adopting both requires explicitly typing the DAG as temporal-causal-precedence only and forbidding it from being read as a structural causal model.

- **Provenance-semiring graded credential (min/max algebra) vs Dempster-Shafer corroboration-boosts-confidence — both on C4/C3 'estimation+corroboration'**
  - Semiring trust evaluation propagates credentials by idempotent ops (e.g. max/min) that CANNOT make two independent corroborating sources yield higher standing than either alone. Dempster-Shafer / probabilistic combination is exactly the confidence-boosting operation C3's 'corroboration' needs — but the only semiring image that does it (probability) is the known-unsafe, independence-requiring, non-injective step. The two prescribe incompatible algebra for combining corroborating evidence.

- **Diplomatics reliability⊥authenticity orthogonality vs single graded-credential primitives (provenance semiring / MDL / DST discount) — all on C4**
  - Diplomatics proves authenticity (un-tampered custody) and reliability (truth-capacity at creation) are ORTHOGONAL and that append-only custody vouches ONLY for authenticity. The graded-credential primitives compute a single scalar 'standing'/trust that propagates through the fold, which structurally tempts collapsing these two orthogonal axes into one number. Adopt with the guard: keep reliability and authenticity as separate fields; a credential may gate acceptance-WEIGHT but the custody chain must never be read as evidence of reliability.
