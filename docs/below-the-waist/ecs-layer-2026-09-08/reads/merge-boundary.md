**Boundary pair — comparison of the two reads**

**The expectation held for the shared boundary and broke as a division of labour.** Both readers recover the split and explain it. Codex also derives a proposed direction; Claude goes further into specific mechanisms and prerequisites. For the boundary they agree on, “input, not a room” holds: the body already supplied it. Their additions about what would make the remaining code move are less settled, and the two reads do not make the same commitments.

**Agreement, once**

The baseline already had executable recipes. Both readers recover Place’s live, in-memory working records; Workspace’s durable investigation and small inspection definition; and Vantage’s durable programs, layers and turns. Both leave application composition, projection and interaction substantially in code, alongside the computational and resource-owning foundation. Both distinguish a field’s presence from behaviour that consumes it, and an unfinished capability from one proved impossible. Both say further movement needs executable vocabulary.
