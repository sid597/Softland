The feedback evaluates this session independently against Sid's exploration brief.

Your strongest organizing idea is the seam as a pair of maps: the picture outward and the addressed query inward. The four nouns separate content from its presentation, and the stationary-hover and screenshot cases explain why the downward route must remain attached to content. One waist with shared services and separate planar/spatial libraries is a useful position. Preserve that picture while qualifying three parts of its definition.

**1. One answer vocabulary should not require every representation to have every answer.** Your input admits points, splats, volumes and arbitrary occupancy functions, while the shared answer set asks all of them for inside, distance, a surface and sections. A point set without a reconstruction rule has no declared inside. A function's zero set does not make its function values distances. Allow capabilities, unsupported answers and explicit interpretation/conversion operations. You already make hit policy explicit for volumes and splats; apply that discipline across the answer set. The common interface can name operations without pretending each stored representation supplies them intrinsically.

This also qualifies the CPU-oracle position. A query needs declared semantics, an addressed result and an accuracy relationship to the displayed view. CPU execution is a plausible reference implementation; the processor alone does not establish determinism or agreement. Shared camera matrices establish a coordinate relation, not complete agreement across tessellation, coverage, transparency and residency. The bench handover's agreement claims remain bounded to its described construction.

**2. Separate authoritative information from derived work, rather than banning categories of data.** “The picture is never data” is too broad for the screenshot example you correctly describe as bytes with a digest. Captured images are legitimate stored values. Authored normals can also carry appearance intent; a construction's modeling tolerance can change its geometric result. Their legitimacy does not depend on looking unlike renderer input.

The durable rule is that a replaceable display derivation must not displace the source needed for intended edits. Likewise, distinguish a transient preview from a deliberately saved render or simulation result. The kind can return reusable image values while keeping physical target allocation and automatic frame caches in the runtime. This matters to the library question: another saved construction may legitimately consume a rendered result.

**3. Derived-part identity needs correspondence and attachment policy, not a promise that a new F always equals the old F.** Your exact open question is well chosen. Extend its test beyond an extrusion whose top face happens to survive: split, merge or delete the supporting face. The result must be able to describe unchanged, generated, split, merged, deleted or ambiguous parts, with parameter mappings where available.

Attaching to a construction step and attaching to a resulting face are not necessarily exclusive choices. An attachment can retain construction provenance, the current resolved support and a policy for interpreting correspondence. A fixed frame at the construction step does not by itself decide what should happen when the surface changes. Preserve the distinction between geometric correspondence and the user's intended response.

Your next exercise should remain the derived-part case you named: a constructed host with ink, a topology-changing edit, the resulting attachment and an addressed query from both views. Make the emitted data changes explicit. For Position 9, separate the decision that constructions are authorable from the question of which engine entry point consumes them: an evaluator can produce typed shape values for geometry, rendering and queries. That keeps the input/output decision precise without making the renderer interpret the whole authoring history.

The planar-face, portal, screenshot and stationary-hover examples are useful evidence for the scope described in your handover. Curved attachments, recursive rendering, travel and the real path filler remain the handover's stated extensions; this review does not turn those omissions into a verdict against the exploratory model.
