A page with a region; inside it a box whose front face carries a pen stroke as a layer of the face's look, a sphere, a floor, a sun with a shadow, and on the back wall a portal into another land. Wheel to zoom the page; drag outside the region to pan; drag inside to orbit; drag the box to move it; switch to draw and drag on the box's front face. The chain on the right is computed on the CPU from the pointer on every move, and the GPU's id at the cursor is read back to check it.
