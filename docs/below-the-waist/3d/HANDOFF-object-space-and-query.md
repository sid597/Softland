Original objective: an engine plus primitives below one waist, with tools, constructions and reusable results editable as data above. Humans and agents share that data; the merging store stays above the kind. The library owes stable identity, useful source correspondence and fine-grained changes. The question is what is necessary for every 2D/3D tool, judged from meaning rather than today's renderer.

Candidate picture:

- An identified definition retains its authored meaning. Occurrences place/reuse it. Mesh, B-rep/history, subdivision cage, field and capture data are not interchangeable truths.
- A scene composes occurrences; a view observes a scene. A page and a room can both host views. Geometric embedding, view portals and geometric derivation are separate operations.
- One waist for executable constructions, typed values, identity/changes, frames and queries; multiple geometry and imaging libraries. A general evaluator does not itself supply robust domain algorithms.
- 3D imaging input: named evaluated occurrences and typed representation references plus view/time/quality. Output: images and source-related query results. Histories are evaluated through shared geometry libraries, not interpreted by the painter.
- A live view differs from a screenshot by its retained dependencies and query capability even when their pixels match. Colour/depth/ID do not reconstruct the object.
- Hover depends on scene changes as well as pointer changes. A stationary pointer must lose the stroke when an occluder passes in front. Route/source IDs remain distinct from GPU slots and triangle indexes.
- The four path responsibilities transfer, but geometry is plural and image formation includes visibility/material/light transport. Rates also include time, query demand and residency. Curved attachments and exact CAD curves qualify the planar/cubic path picture.
