The Seam Bench

The hardest case as a thing to feel. A page holds a portal onto a spatial space. In the space, a box carries a pen stroke on its top face and a portal to another land on its front face; a sphere can pass in front. Zoom the page, orbit the portal, draw on the face, hover and click, paste a screenshot beside the portal. The panel prints the hit address the seam walker returns, the diff against the last answer, and which rate each change ran at.

The value never holds the picture. Zooming the page re-leases the portal's surface at a new pixel size and re-runs the face's filler at a new tolerance; the stroke's stored samples do not change. Orbiting is a view change, not an edit. The screenshot is bytes: it scales, blurs, and answers hover as pixels.
