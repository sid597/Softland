## 3. Where the problem-solution space stands (all POSITION unless quoted)

1. **Four nouns.** A space is the entity (planar or spatial, with a unit); things live in spaces, each a placement of a definition (Sid's copies; the definition owns the edits, the placement its place); a view is a camera onto a space and belongs to a session; a portal is a thing in either kind of space showing a view of another space. The surface a portal shows is a lease, derived, worn visibly.
2. **The seam is a pair of maps, crossed three ways.** Picture up, pointer down, and they agree about *where* by sharing one camera; about *what* (which face, inside which stroke) each side has its own definition, checked apart (the bench found both sides wrong together, twice). Three crossings, each a pair: a portal (a view and a route), an attachment (a planar space on a face by a saved rule), a derivation (new geometry with provenance). The page first said "one nesting primitive"; the fold corrected it.
3. **The general 3D object** is a placed shape with a material, stored as its source made it (an artist's faceted mesh included), under one question protocol; each representation declares which answers it gives, and an answer may be a result, candidates, unsupported, or needs a policy. The triangle is the leak only when it stands in for another source; segment counts in the value and normals *required* of every mesh are the leak today (CHECKED component.cljc:53-60, :373-384). The picture rule is rewritten: a rendered picture does not replace the editable meaning of the thing pictured; a saved render, a scan, a painted texture are data with provenance; a lease, a frame cache, a tessellation in the value would be the leak.
4. **Ink on a face** is a planar surface: a planar space attached to a named face by a saved rule; the path kind draws in it unchanged; the placement zoom of 1.0 dies (CHECKED on_plane.cljc:20). The attachment is a noun of its own: face, authored domain, anchor rule, a clause per lineage, what the content reads as, whose it is.
5. **The hit is an address**, with each part's lineage and the mode it was asked in. Hover is a value of (pointer, data, views), re-walked when data or a view moves under a still pointer. The hit is defined by the question (mode, policy, tolerance) over the data; the CPU walker is its reference implementation, not the definition; a GPU answer must agree with the reference at a tolerance, refreshed when its inputs change.
6. **Two cameras compose** by function composition; the page's zoom magnifies the placed view with the framing kept (the portal's aspect, never the lease's); the region's camera is per view; a lens zoom and a dolly are its own operations; a crossing is travel, a declared reaction that re-roots.
7. **The axis** is per edit · per view · per frame · per time, and derived pictures (a shadow map, a fixed portal) on their own inputs; per scale is the planar case of per view.
8. **One waist, two floors of libraries.** Shared: identity and diffs, the seam walker, the compositor, the evaluator, and three requests: evaluate a construction (no camera), render an evaluated space through a view (the request names the quantity), query it with a point or ray (under a declared mode). Floors: planar (the path picture) and spatial (representations under one protocol; a packer per view with a budget; a view render). A scheduler does not force two engines; incompatible guarantees needing a bridge would.
9. **The evaluator** is forced to a decision by 3D. Position 9 on the page: a node vocabulary as data with an executor, code-as-data as the escape hatch; one language, several runners; what the runners exchange has a first receipt on the bench (parts named by the construction, each with a lineage and a frame; the definer's `execute(recipe, inputs, state) → named results + next state`). Sid's to close; asked once.
10. **A derived part's identity** (Position 10, new in the fold, worked on the bench): what the construction returns, a name, a lineage against the previous run (unchanged, modified, split, merged, generated, deleted, ambiguous) and a frame; where the ink belongs is the attachment's rule (origin corner, proportions, an edge; on split clip or detach), not the face's. Naming the face is necessary and not enough: on the bench three rules put the same stroke in three places while the face's identity never wavered.
11. **Today's code**, read at the level of inputs and outputs: the region row is a portal with the world stored inline (CHECKED component.cljc:601); the view and moved transforms already live in the session (renderer.cljs:1087, scene.cljc:1041-1060); pick is meshes only, no page-point map, ink not in the BVH (scene.cljc:965-982); any static edit is a full rebuild (scene.cljc:1088-1105); the composite is colour only through the page's group transform (renderer.cljs:270-275); `:extent` is required and read by nothing; the camera's aspect comes from the encode pixel size, within a pixel of the rect's (renderer.cljs:1093-1095).

**The decision that remains:** Position 9, whether constructions are the kind's input and in which language.

**The exact question that remains** (the review Sid pasted, adopted on the page): what must a construction return about part lineage and coordinate correspondence so a saved attachment rule can preserve, clip, split or explicitly detach the stroke when its support changes? For the box: a lineage relation and a frame per face. For a kernel: OCAF's history plus the parameter map for a modified face; where there is none, the lineage says ambiguous and the rule has a clause. **The next construction after it:** an implicit or sampled object the engine does not know as a hardcoded kind (the Codex lane and the review of the working model asked for it): its saved definition, the operations it supplies, the kernels they call, what its editable hit addresses when there is no face.

## 7. Guards, from this chair

- A sibling's uncommitted files in a shared tree are not ground; `git status --short` before deriving anything from a working-tree file, and before every commit; stage exact paths only; never edit another chair's files.
- "Agree by construction" covers the coordinate relation only. The bench's renderer and picker agreed with each other through a wrong aspect and a wrong nib; meaning is checked apart, by a second formula or a brute-force reference, never by the two halves matching.
- Reproduce a reviewer's probe against the committed functions before changing anything; the receipt goes in the fact base with the reviewer's figures beside yours.
- A GLSL identifier starting with `gl_` fails silently as "WebGL2 unavailable"; the bench prints the shader log into `#glstat`.
- The bench came before the section; every number on the page is a dump-dom readout, none was estimated. After a repair, re-dump and re-write the numbers; do not keep the old ones.
- Every seam is two maps; a proposal that names only the picture half has left the hit and the diff unsaid. And a slogan that bans a category ("the picture is never data") is one counterexample from wrong; state the rule as what must not be displaced.
- Split every review finding want-vs-is before it changes the page; reviewer verdict words never transfer.
