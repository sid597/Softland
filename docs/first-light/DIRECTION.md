## The question it answers

Substrate complete, old UI discarded as past. What is the first thing that lives in Softland — what does login show, and how does building-Softland-in-Softland actually start?

## The law (the day's core yield — the evolution engine)

> An inhabitant may express friction in the land. The land may propose a repair. A repair begins locally, keeps its complete provenance, and earns broader scope only through recurrence and explicit, scoped human ratification. Every inherited behavior remains explainable, branchable, and reversible.

The engine spelled out: care → friction → wish → local repair → recurrence → scoped promotion → inheritance → trail. Consequences:

- **Components are exhaust, never prerequisites** — the fossil record of felt frictions. No component is designed ahead of a friction.
- **Types late-bind** — promote by recurrence, never resemblance alone. Instance → variant → type, climbing only as far as wear proves. The land proposes the generalization (silver); the human scopes and ratifies (gold).
- **Promotion is a logged event, never mutation** — a promotion knows its originating friction, the repaired instance, author + proposing agent, recurrence evidence, proposed scope, blast radius shown at ratification, the ratifying human, and any later revision/withdrawal. Instance-repair = branch; promotion = merge (Sid's 07-11 branch-from-base line is this).

## The package boundary — alive before it reproduces

Abiogenesis proves **metabolism**; evolution proves **inheritance**. They are two proofs, not one slice:

- **first-light A (metabolism):** real use → real friction → targeted visible wish → functioning candidate repair → worn → locally applied → explainable → reversible → continued use in the changed land. One local worn repair. This package.
- **first-light B (inheritance):** opens only when a repair **genuinely recurs** — never from the calendar. Then: recurrence evidence → proposed shared scope → human ratification → future instance inherits → promotion explainable and withdrawable. A gate that *required* recurrence would fabricate it, violating the law above; so B waits for the land to produce its own second friction.

## The unit

The genesis face's atomic unit is **wish + addressed material + response/proposal + decision** — not "message." A wish is a first-class visible object: what the inhabitant wants different, coupled to the exact material it is about, `asserted-by` its wisher, with candidate responses related back to it.

**Homologous, not identical, to ActionRequest.** The kernel's request → decision → event lifecycle (`architecture/action-request-kernel-routing.md` — verified) is the same request-shaped pattern at kernel grain, but the altitudes stay distinct: ActionRequest remains the lifecycle envelope; the human-language wish is durable material; the visible wish is a **product projection** over request + material + target + context. An accepted first request means "the land accepted that Sid asked for this" — never "the change is accepted." The causal chain has two requests: wish material → request-to-attempt → decision → **agent proposal as separate material** → worn as candidate → request-to-install → accepted event → face revision changes → re-render. Product semantics never leak into transport fields.

Guard this genome: if the first face's unit is "message," instance→type promotion compounds a chat app ("spatial chat" — the named grave). Every utterance and response gets stable identity, addressable blocks, actor provenance, causal parents, and the standing ability to be pointed at, related, and targeted by a later wish — so the face can evolve out of "conversation" without re-platforming its material.

## Genesis (the adoption move)

Two login moments, never conflated: at **world-state zero** the ground is genuinely blank — no sidebar, no chrome, no create-your-first-project ceremony; Sid types directly into the ground, and that first utterance becomes durable addressable material (`asserted-by: sid`), opens the first episode, and summons the resident agent. **Every later login resumes the living episode** or its current projection — blankness belongs to birth only, never imposed as a minimalist aesthetic on return. The land is an **unprojected world, not an empty one**: the trail of Softland-so-far stays one keystroke from lighting up.

Genesis is moving one beloved practice into the land: the daily working conversation with agents, because that is where Sid's care already discharges every day with no added ritual — but its material enters the same system trails, blocks, relations, and faces inhabit, or Softland has merely grown a prettier terminal.

Day-one verbs ride existing organs, with the missing **product joints** named honestly at contract time (the contract's Phase 0 carries the full organ-vs-joint map): **write** (block-write, CLOSED 07-13) · **arrange** (faces as pure-EDN assemblies, total compiler, revisioned source) · **relate** (relation kernel; no settled "wish" relation kind yet — mark ≠ write) · **point** (scene pick/context seam; requires the P3c main-face flip below) · **inspect** (`:assembly/src-path` is stored provenance today — surfacing it as a lived point-and-ask is the work).

**The membrane ladder:** first-light A's membrane is the **draft face** — the agent's candidate assembly compiles through the existing total compiler and renders against the *real* conversation material without mutating the active face; accept = new active revision through Rama; reject = discard the projection, keep the proposal in the trail. ## The first repair (first-light A's proof)

**One real, felt arrangement-level friction** over the live conversation — width, rhythm, grouping, placement, density, reading order — expressible in the existing assembly language. Not a fabricated styling exercise: Sid wears the conversation for real work until a friction appears. Then:

1. Sid points at the affected material or face region (pick supplies material address + `:assembly/src-path`).
2. Sid says the wish in-land; it lands as visible material on that target.
3. The resident agent returns candidate assembly source; the existing compiler validates it.
4. The candidate renders against the actual conversation — the active face unchanged.
5. Sid wears it; accepts or rejects. Accept = new active assembly revision through Rama truth; the inhabited conversation re-renders.
6. On the changed region, "**Why is this here?**" answers with the trail: wish → proposal → decision → revision.
7. Reversal restores the prior revision without deleting history. Restart preserves conversation, revision, and trail.

If the first real friction is **behavioral** ("reply to just this block"), the wish still lands — and the land says honestly that it needs a new capability rather than pretending an arrangement patch satisfies it. That wish becomes the originating pressure for the code lane. Two evolutionary routes, one gesture: arrangement wish → candidate assembly → wear/accept; behavior wish → candidate code/primitive → test → available to assemblies → wear/accept. The inhabitant expresses friction the same way; Softland determines which stratum must change.

**No-fabrication clause (symmetric with B):** the arrangement gate waits for a real arrangement friction to occur in wear, exactly as B waits for genuine recurrence. If early frictions are all behavioral, they land and route honestly while the gate waits. A staged or invented friction fails the gate by definition — the gate measures the land's ability to metabolize real pressure, not our ability to supply specimens.

Pass = an inhabitant's care modified the medium from within, without leaving the land. Fail = any beautiful agent UI is camouflage.

## The measures

**first-light A metric: wish-to-worn-local-repair latency**, staged — wish → visible request · → first candidate · → functioning preview · → accepted revision · → truth echoed into the inhabited face. Plus **terminal-escape count**: how many times Sid had to leave Softland (file edit, CLI, hand-carried context) to complete the repair — target **zero** for an arrangement repair.

**The long-term family (activates at the first genuine promotion, first-light B):** wish→local-repair · local-repair→safe-inheritance · inheritance→explanation · reversal latency · false-promotion rate. Optimizing speed alone breeds premature generalization; the last three are the brakes. This family is the operational form of "the medium is becoming self-improving."

## Axes kept separate (constitutional)

**Authorship · epistemic status · warrant · stewardship** — four independent axes, never conflated.

- **Gold stays narrow:** a named human ratified this, at a time, under a scope, on a basis. Ratification adds a human act; it never launders authorship (agent-authored stays agent-authored). Gold does not mean true/good/important/loved/final.
- **Stewardship is rendered separately** — recorded acts (return, repair, endorsement, withdrawal, maintenance), not measured "care." Gardens / fields / wilderness is a **view over stewardship history, never an authority system**. Care is the human condition the land is hospitable to; stewardship is its recordable shadow; judgment is the scarce act; ratification is the protocol event.
- **Right to wilderness:** unconnected ≠ broken; unvisited ≠ forgotten; silence is not a request for completion; no nudging to process; machine activity must not manufacture the appearance of stewardship. The frontier renders where attention has actually accumulated without pretending the remainder is failure.

## Guard rules

- **No machine convenience may require the inhabitant to destroy meaningful ambiguity at capture time.** Agents adapt to the human record; the human never becomes the clerk producing agent-ready thought.
- Every agent proposal is visibly a proposal (silver) with preview, provenance, undo. Human silence and agent non-inference stay distinguishable facts.
- **Semantic reader = replaceable boundary now:** model/provider/version receipts on every semantic output; evaluable against recorded corrections; swappable cloud/local/hybrid. Local execution is a sovereignty milestone, not a genesis blocker.
- **Provenance + point-of-use legibility together:** the record existing is not honesty; interrogating it at the point of use is. The constitutional gesture is "Why is this here?" on anything visible — who/what introduced it, inputs, status (quoted/inferred/generated/ratified), participating rule or model, the event that promoted it to this scope, how to inspect/branch/reverse.
