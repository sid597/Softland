## The criterion

When something in Softland creates pressure — human or machine — that
pressure can travel through public, truthful, reversible material and return
as a perceivable, attributable change, without leaving the land and without
charging anyone's flow a ceremony. ("Ritual" = ceremony charged to the
inhabitant's flow; the lawful request→decision→event machinery under the
gesture is not ritual.)

  base stay separately addressable, distinct in actor and grounds, visible
  simultaneously; one scoped activation never deletes the other. Activation
  is not consensus and not deletion; no last-write-wins.

  resident works INSIDE a layer, briefed by the same projection the human

  floor is dull: total render (error card, never black) + previous revision

## Paradigm rulings

- **Describe, never gate.** THE GATE TEST: does this description ever refuse
  an instance? Then stop. An untyped block renders, forever. Totality gates
  on the meta-material's OWN form (error cards) are the dull floor, not a
  violation. Types here are memoir, never prophecy.
- **Masters ≠ kinds.** Masters are mechanical (which material renders/behaves
  me). Kinds are semantic (#TASK — predicates, many-per-block, fluid,
  hand-or-query assigned). Never fused. Two separate far-proofs of life: the
  first unplanned semantic kind, and — deeper — the first unplanned material
  type, condensed from recurrent lived deviations, ratified by care.
- **Precedent, not statute.** Instance repair → visible recurrence → scoped
  ratification → inheritance, reversible with record. Recurrence informs the
  caretaker; it never auto-legislates. Prescription is a dial indexed to
  stranger-count (conformance LENSES may arrive with future inhabitants;
  refusal never).
- In-paradigm proof: masters are values, promotions are events, records are
  queries, kinds are predicates — more log vocabulary, not a new semantics.

## The architecture

- **Facets** (ECS's "component", renamed — the word was taken): entity = the
  durable address; facet = one attached fact; facet material = the editable,
  revisioned policy behind it; recipe = the worn list; system = eyes on a
  standing query — **accepted events are the pulse, deterministic transitions
  the muscle, derived workers the hands. Reactive queries NEVER carry side
  effects.**
- **Four stations of input:** gesture (kernel) → one pick over the containment
  path (kernel) → innermost matching material claim (binding rows, material)
  → named verb (code registry). Unclaimed falls through to the space.
  Bindings are a closed data grammar — material never names arbitrary code.
  Verbs carry effect classes: pure/projection · durable-via-request ·
  external-via-derived-worker.
- **Kernel criterion:** mechanism in code, policy in material. Code owns:
  gesture normalization, pick, containment math, total interpreter, floors,
  logging, authoritative transitions, verb implementations. Kernel surface
  shrinking over time is the self-hosting gauge.
- **Propagation by reference, never fan-out writes.** One active-pointer
  change; wearers re-resolve. `master → wearers` indexes exist for queries
  and blast-radius display only. Global epoch until the echo gate complains;
  then cause-tagged narrow invalidation (already a standing board item).
- **Latest ≠ candidate ≠ previewed ≠ active ≠ pinned ≠ previous.** Deployment
  = an accepted, scoped activation of an immutable revision. Rollback is
  another activation event. "Deploy to all" honestly means: all unpinned
  wearers within the named scope, including future wearers.
- **Policy change vs structural change:** policy propagates by reference;
  structural change (new stateful facet on existing entities, shape splits)
  is an explicit, recorded migration — recipes never silently migrate state.
- **Composition honesty (binds at facet #2):** contributions carry subject,
  attachment, master+revision, role, and stamp; merge behavior is declared;
  same-slot conflicts render as lint in the land (deterministic temporary
  winner allowed, silent winner never); the resolver can explain its output.
  Role/merge vocabulary comes FROM the first extractions.
- **Render provenance grows from src-path to causal trace:** a pick can
  answer why-this-pixel — entity, placement, attachment, master revision,
  recipe revision (when one exists), override/pin that won, source path.

## Aliveness laws

- **Deviation cheap, scoped, visible, queryable** — seconds, or the type
  never learns. Recurrence views preserve DISTRIBUTION, not just count:
  distinct entities/worlds/actors, strata, time span, retained-vs-reversed.
  Three retries by one machine are not three precedents.
- **Change announces itself at three scales:** a local breath at affected
  appearances · a recoverable trace (revision, scope, actor, reversal path) ·
  ambient material-weather (RecentChanges). Preview, deviation, scoped
  activation, canonical activation, rerender, rollback, recovery stay
  distinguishable.
- **Case reports are derived, never authored** — and may claim only grounds
  explicitly declared on the proposal (grounded-in experience/deviation,
  responds-to conflict). Ungrounded exploratory revisions are legal and
  render as ungrounded. "Experienced around" is broader than "cited as
  grounds."
- **History is standable:** the material world projects at any historical
  revision/causal cut — what was worn, which cases existed, what followed.
- **The machine's mouth is calibrated:** resident frictions enter the same
  intake, stratum-marked, observation ≠ interpretation ≠ proposal; repeated
  identical machine failures condense rather than inflame.
- **Gauges measure conductivity and are log-derived,** exposing window,
  denominator, receipt/silver/gold composition, coverage, truncation.
  Terminal-escape has a mechanical detector: a code-lane commit changing
  material-owned policy with no activation event = one detected escape.
