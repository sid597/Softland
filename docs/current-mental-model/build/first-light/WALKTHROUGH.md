## Laws derived

1. **Speech lands at the point YOU chose — the land never proposes a position.** At arrival there is no caret anywhere: a pre-placed cursor is the substrate choosing your first position for you, and the first position is itself the first act of taste (Sid, 07-18). Click → a caret at that spot, an acknowledgment, never a proposal. Type without ever clicking → the words land under the pointer — attention made literal; the land never swallows speech waiting for a ceremony (no-ritual). Focused on old material → speech attaches THERE as reply/mark; a selection held → the wish targets it.
2. **There is no top-left.** Documents have edges; an infinite land has a "here" — where the camera looks.
3. **The camera is the inhabitant's; material never moves on its own.** Pan, zoom, and drag are capabilities of the space (real estate on demand — Sid, 07-18). There is no structural tip for the camera to follow: the conversation is a pattern that forms where you speak, not a rail the camera rides.
4. **Truth is the raw stream; blocks are the default view over it.** Stored exactly as streamed (log-primary); pointable parts at paragraph seams (same grain as md ingest — one grammar for all material); re-chunkable later without loss. Geometry is projection: position is meaning, but time-order is never lost — any spatial scatter re-projects as a trail on demand. Pointability provenance: Sid 07-14 — "I want to only reply to certain blocks of text, or extract some of the blocks."
5. **Streaming renders live; parts firm up as they complete.** "Ask for more" = point at a part and wish on it.
6. **Machine-written and Sid-written are different strata forever.** At genesis the mark is the block's border tint — a quiet provenance mark, not avatars, not bubbles, no speaker sides (SMS heritage; sides don't scale past two minds). Must still read at far zoom — turns as rhythm/texture from altitude.
7. **Spacing encodes containment, nothing else.** paragraph-gap < turn-gap < episode-gap — the visible containment tree.
8. **Waiting is honest.** An open agent turn shows its real streamed activity (one current-activity line; full trace folded, pointable later). Failure closes the turn with a visible mark; retry is a new turn; nothing erased.
9. **The camera serves attention, never the stream.** Growth never steals the view: if attention is elsewhere, new material lands without moving the camera (the classic streaming-chat theft, outlawed).
10. **The block is a grabbable object; its boundary shows only on attention.** Hover/selection reveal the bounding box; at rest the land reads as material, not form-widgets. Press-and-drag moves a block; a clean click enters it (edit caret). Positions settle as truth at release — the settled state of a burst, never per-event.
11. **Reply and wish are the same act:** speech at attention. A wish is speech that asks for change; it lives AT its target, carrying status: asked → proposal → accepted/rejected.
12. **Candidates are never screenshots.** A proposal face renders the SAME living material with a different arrangement — both faces stay live (keep talking; both update). Compare by inhabiting, not by looking at a picture.
13. **Ratification is a signing act.** Deliberate, attributable, never a stray keypress: explicit affordance on the proposal (plus the literal word "accept" as a wish-reply). Gold cannot be fat-fingered.
14. **Every remaining default is named wish-fodder** — reply placement (beneath the spoken block, left-aligned), block measure (~70ch, overflow visible), the parked-pointer landing, camera-at-return, border tints, agent verbosity. Shipped with reasons, expected to be re-tuned through the loop itself.
15. **Permanence without ceremony.** Words are durable as they appear — no save, no draft state, no submit. What you see renders from the committed echo ("write directly to rama and then stream from there" — Sid's law), so what you see is by construction what's durable. This is what makes leaving safe.

## The moments (world-zero → return)

0. **Arrival: nothing.** No sidebar, no chrome, no bottom bar, no hint text, no watermark, no caret ("discard any ui that we have now its all past"). Any hint is chrome answering a question no inhabitant has asked; at N=1 the only inhabitant is the founder, who arrives knowing. Honest assumption, named: this genesis is designed for Sid; a visitor's first arrival is an H3-era question, later by his own ladder. The symmetry is broken by the inhabitant — literally, with a click.
1. **You point, and speak.** Click anywhere — that spot, because you chose it; pointing-then-saying is Sid's stated law ("get to some area and then say 'make a new xyz here'"). A caret appears where you clicked (Law 1). Type: the words render as a block at that spot — block because the atomic-unit work already ruled that grain, not a bordered text-field widget; boundary on attention only (Law 10). Enter = newline at the block's left edge; the block autogrows; Escape or click-elsewhere leaves it. The words are durable as they appear (Law 15) — the first words of the universe are permanent as they render.
2. **Ctrl+Enter — speak TO the resident.** The input surface and the world are one surface; there is no send button and no addressing UI. Ctrl+Enter sends the block to the resident mind (durable-before-agent, unchanged); "just noting" = never press Ctrl+Enter (today's convention — the ambient-reader era, where everything written is read by the resident, iterates this from inside the land; the interposition law already commits to a reader in the medium). The reply precipitates as a block beneath your words, left-aligned (default; wish-fodder), border-tinted as machine stratum (Law 6), streaming in through the same committed path yours took — same citizenship, same permanence. While the turn is open, the honest activity line shows (Law 8).
3. **The space is yours.** Pan and zoom for real estate; drag blocks to arrange (Law 10). The camera obeys Laws 3/9. Camera and positions settle as truth at burst-end — never per-event.
4. **The frictions arrive — they are the point.** Thoughts at different levels (vision, decision, implementation, design) land as blocks and form a trail; wishes get spoken in the land and are automatically trail, because everything is. The instance→type mechanic starts eating: each spoken wish is an instance-level friction; the recurring ones become organs; work on this chat-instance upgrades the chat-type for every future instance. The interface is not designed before habitation; it condenses out of habitation. This is where Softland starts building Softland.
5. **You leave.** Nothing to do — no save, no close. The first leaving is a trust moment: walking away from an unsaved-looking world. Law 15 is what makes it safe; the return is what proves the medium.
6. **You return.** The scene is exactly where you left it — same place, same zoom, yesterday's words where they were (camera + positions are settled truth, restored at boot). The "where was I, what now" friction is welcome fodder; the morning-answer briefing stays a later organ, named deferral.

## Decision points

1. **Utterance close — ANSWERED (Sid; built in P2):** Enter = newline; **Ctrl+Enter speaks to the AI**. (The earlier Enter-sends pick is dead.)
2. **Click vs drag on a block:** default — press-and-move beyond a small threshold = drag the block as object; a clean click = edit caret at that point. Named default, wish-fodder.
3. **Where wish text lives (P3):** at the target, as speech-at-attention (Law 11); exact rendering is P3 design.
4. **Pre-send durability — ANSWERED (Sid, 07-18):** text persists as typed; Ctrl+Enter only addresses the resident. The lane pick + position truth home remain contract-level — CONTRACT §9.4–9.5.
