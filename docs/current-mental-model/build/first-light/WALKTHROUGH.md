# first-light — lived walkthrough (pre-contract seed)

2026-07-15 · Born from Sid's interrogation of the step proposals ("cursor where? why? blocks why? arranged how, why?" — and the correction: run that interrogation over EVERYTHING yourself, bring back steps + decision points). Method: every moment answers **see / do / know / why** — each why is a derived principle, a citation of Sid's own words, or a named taste-call with a default-and-reason. A moment that can't answer its whys doesn't ship.

## Laws derived

1. **Speech lands at your attention point.** One law, four cases: world-zero → view center (a single caret, nothing else); at the tip → continues the conversation; focused on old material → attaches THERE as reply/mark; with a selection held → the wish targets it. Kills per-screen cursor decisions.
2. **There is no top-left.** Documents have edges; an infinite land has a "here" — where the camera looks.
3. **The camera follows the growing tip; material never moves.** Panning up = walking your history. No docked input bar — the input is the tip of the trail.
4. **Truth is the raw stream; blocks are the default view over it.** Stored exactly as streamed (log-primary); pointable parts at paragraph seams (same grain as md ingest — one grammar for all material); re-chunkable later without loss. Pointability provenance: Sid 07-14 — "I want to only reply to certain blocks of text, or extract some of the blocks."
5. **Streaming renders live; parts firm up as they complete.** "Ask for more" = point at a part and wish on it.
6. **Provenance lives in the margin, never in geometry.** No speaker sides (SMS heritage; sides don't scale past two minds). Differentiation must read at far zoom — turns as rhythm/texture from altitude.
7. **Spacing encodes containment, nothing else.** paragraph-gap < turn-gap < episode-gap — the visible containment tree.
8. **Waiting is honest.** An open agent turn shows its real streamed activity (one current-activity line; full trace folded, pointable later). Failure closes the turn with a visible mark; retry is a new turn; nothing erased.
9. **The camera serves attention, never the stream.** Auto-follow the tip only while attention is at the tip; if Sid is reading up-trail, growth happens below without stealing the view (the classic streaming-chat theft, outlawed).
10. **The margin mark is the block's handle.** Identity, provenance, selection grip, and status chips live in one place — meaning and mechanics unified. Click text = edit caret (block-write); click margin = grab the block as object.
11. **Reply and wish are the same act:** speech at attention. A wish is speech that asks for change; it lives AT its target as a margin note (wall-marginalia heritage), carrying status: asked → proposal → accepted/rejected.
12. **Candidates are never screenshots.** A proposal face renders the SAME living material with a different arrangement — both faces stay live (keep talking; both update). Compare by inhabiting, not by looking at a picture.
13. **Ratification is a signing act.** Deliberate, attributable, never a stray keypress: explicit affordance on the proposal chip (plus the literal word "accept" as a wish-reply). Gold cannot be fat-fingered.
14. **Every remaining default is named wish-fodder** — caret resting height, ~70ch measure (readability convention, stated), margins, ink, agent verbosity. Shipped with reasons, expected to be re-tuned through the loop itself.

## The moments (world-zero → return)

0. **Launch, world-zero:** ground at reading zoom (text ≈ the worn 14px — the first act is reading/typing; zoom is a capability, not a starting state), one caret at view center. Typing streams through block-write (durable as typed). Utterance close = DECISION 1.
1. **Agent turn opens** immediately below, margin-marked, live activity line (Law 8).
2. **Answer streams** per-paragraph (token-grain = feel-call during wear); camera per Law 9; long answers: no folding in slice one — zoom is the reader's tool; "too long" is expected early wish (prompt-lane fix available day one).
3. **Work continues.** No episode ceremony; nothing closes; the trail grows (no-ritual).
4. **Friction → wish:** select target (Law 10, DECISION 2) or speak untargeted at the tip (wish lands face-scoped — attention law); wish text at target as margin note (DECISION 3).
5. **Proposal:** candidate face spawns beside the live one (open ground to the right); camera pulls back to frame both (attention = comparison); silver-marked, one-line "what I changed"; same living material (Law 12). One candidate at a time; "show me another" = another wish.
6. **Accept/reject:** Law 13. Accept → arrangement re-flows in place through Rama truth; candidate face closes; event in trail. Reject → proposal stays in trail as rejected, feel-grounds recordable.
7. **"Why is this like this?"** — ask in conversation (agent walks the trail, ships first) + margin-chip → minimal chain view. **Undo** — revert affordance on the chip; a new event, history whole.
8. **Return next day:** opens at the tip, trail above, nothing synthetic. (Morning-answer briefing = later organ, named deferral.)
