# first-light — lived walkthrough (pre-contract seed)

2026-07-15 · Born from Sid's interrogation of the step proposals ("cursor where? why? blocks why? arranged how, why?"). Method: every moment answers **see / do / know / why** — each why is a derived principle, a citation of Sid's own words, or a named taste-call with a default-and-reason. Grows moment-by-moment as the contract is written; a moment that can't answer its whys doesn't ship.

## Laws derived so far

1. **Speech lands at your attention point.** One law, three cases: world-zero → attention defaults to view center (a single caret there, nothing else on screen); at the tip → typing continues the conversation; focused on old material → typing becomes a reply/mark attached THERE (point-and-say). Kills "where does the cursor go" as a per-screen decision.
2. **There is no top-left.** Top-left starts are document/terminal heritage; an infinite pan/zoom land has edges nowhere and a "here" everywhere — "here" = where the camera looks.
3. **The camera follows the growing tip; material never moves.** Each turn lands below the last (prose reading direction); the camera drifts to keep the tip comfortable. Consequence: the conversation becomes literal terrain — panning up = walking your own history. There is NO docked input bar (that's chrome); the input is the tip of the trail itself.
4. **Truth is the raw stream; blocks are the default view over it.** The agent's output is stored exactly as it streamed (log-primary). The default view makes parts pointable at paragraph seams — same grain the md ingest already uses (one grammar for material however it arrived). Re-chunking later (semantic breaking, Sid's unlock #3/#4) is a re-view, never a rewrite. Provenance of the pointability requirement: Sid's 07-14 voice note — "I want to only reply to certain blocks of text, or I want to extract some of the blocks."
5. **Streaming renders live; parts firm up as they complete.** No dead waiting, no artificial withholding. "Ask for more" = point at a part and wish on it.
6. **Provenance lives in the margin, never in geometry.** No left/right speaker sides (SMS heritage; sides don't scale past two minds and the land will hold many). Every turn carries its asserted-by mark at the margin; visual differentiation must stay legible at far zoom (turns read as rhythm/texture from altitude).
7. **Spacing encodes containment, nothing else.** paragraph-gap < turn-gap < episode-gap — the visible form of the real tree (paragraph ⊂ turn ⊂ episode). No decorative spacing.
8. **Waiting is honest.** An agent's in-progress turn is an open block showing its real streamed activity (observations already flow into Rama) — never a spinner pretending. Failure closes the turn with a visible failure mark; retry is a new turn; nothing is erased.
9. **Every remaining default is named wish-fodder.** Exact caret resting point (optical center vs higher), column measure (~70ch default — readability convention, stated not assumed), margins, ink contrast: taste-grain, shipped with a reason, expected to be re-tuned by Sid through the loop itself.

## Deliberate deferrals (named, not forgotten)

- Visitor discoverability (how a stranger knows what to do at a blank caret): H3 concern by Sid's own sequencing — world-zero's only audience is its builder.
- Multi-column / non-linear conversation arrangements: wait for a real friction; the trail-column is the default, not a dogma.
- Token-grain streaming through Rama vs per-paragraph arrival: feel-call during wear (start per-paragraph; upgrade if it feels dead).
