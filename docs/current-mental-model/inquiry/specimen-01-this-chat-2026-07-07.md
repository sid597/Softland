## 2 · Where sense held — and what was holding it

Beats 0–9, roughly ten hours: five course corrections from Sid, EACH fixed
in one round. Sense held not because nothing went wrong but because the
thing he was testing against was vivid and instantly available: the moment
an artifact violated it, he could name the violation AND the test
("what is the current trail-view problem?" / "how a designer would
understand" / "keep the example simple only use titles"). Corrections were
cheap exactly where his held-thing had authority.

## 3 · The dissolution, located precisely

Every correction in beats 4–8 tested an ARTIFACT against the PLAN. The
crisis tested the PLAN against the NEED — "what will I even be able to USE
with this?" — and it happened only after he LEFT the production loop (went
to the design chat, imagined use, drew in the notebook). Two facts stand
out:

- **Level shift.** Same man, same day: total adjudicating authority at the
  artifact-vs-plan level (14:00), "I am not able to make sense of this now"
  at the plan-vs-need level (20:32). The held-thing had answers at one level
  and a felt absence at the other.
- **Offline discovery.** The dissolution was not discovered inside the
  work loop. It required stepping out — different medium (notebook),
  different posture (imagining use, not reviewing output). No artifact in
  the loop could have surfaced it; every artifact was passing its local
  test.

## 4 · What restored sense — and what failed to

**Failed:** more information (the Rama-connections explainer — correct,
consumed, didn't help); machinery answers (three minting lanes); a schema
(five fields — "no proof"); an authoritative borrowed frame (DG — "was
never about discourse graphs"). Each wrong-level repair DEEPENED the
frustration. The failures share a shape: each CLOSED something he was
holding open.

**Began restoring:** (a) his own medium-switch — the notebook drawings;
(b) being heard back accurately without addition ("you're not stupid, here
is what you found" — he re-engaged); (c) decisively, HIS move of converting
the confusion itself into the object of work — "what is that something
structure is our inquiry that we have to do." Sense returned the moment
not-making-sense became a subject instead of a failure state. He made that
move, not me.
