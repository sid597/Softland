# Softland — North & Bets

One page. North is the destination; bets are routes. Vision-stubborn, route-flexible.

## Authority rules
- **North** is Sid's commitment — where this project is going, against all odds.
  It is NOT a hypothesis: no kill-conditions, no evidence requirement. Only Sid
  rewrites it. Visioning sessions are legitimate work; their output lands here.
  North is a *projection*: the verbatim source is `vision/LOG.md` (Sid's words,
  append-only) — capture there first, synthesize here second.
- **Bets** are route hypotheses toward North. Anyone may propose one; only
  pre-registered evidence may kill or confirm one. **One bet ACTIVE at a time.**
  Killing a bet never touches North — it demands a better route.
- **Reviews are calendar-shaped, work is evidence-shaped.** Monthly/quarterly
  sitting, two parts: (1) evidence review — does the active bet survive?
  (2) vision sitting — has North moved? (Sid's authorship only.)
- Pace layers: North (slowest, founder-owned) → bets (evidence-paced) → work
  packages (contract/gate-paced; see `decisions.md` + `docs/sessions/next-prompt.md`).
- Verdicts append to the log at the bottom; bets are never silently edited.

## North — Sid's commitment
*(Drafted by Fable from Sid's own words — this section is yours; rewrite freely.
Expanded 2026-07-03 from the first vision sitting: terminal purpose + collective scale.)*

Softland is a land made of software — a place you inhabit, not a tool you use.
A world for holding understanding in public form: trails that don't fade, a map
that doesn't lie, one land at every zoom from the knowledge landscape down to
the code that renders it — visitable by other minds, human and machine, and
renovatable by its inhabitants down to bedrock. It should exist even if the
route is long: first the wall made alive, then a settlement, then a world.

**The question the land exists to answer, about any xyz:** what are the known
knowns, the known unknowns — and where do the unknown unknowns announce
themselves? Rendered as a knowledge earth: zoomed out, topics are nations with
visible borders and connections; zoomed all the way in, the ground is made of
questions, claims, evidence, sources. Every zoom level is a level of
understanding, with AI holding the context of that level — and every visitor
arrives with their own assistant, tuned to how *they* understand. Symbiotic,
not AI-dependent: machines carry the boring weight; humans at every level of
understanding explore and contribute at theirs. The substrate already speaks
this question's grammar: claims-with-evidence are the known knowns; first-class
questions are the known unknowns; the frontier — where trails end, evidence
thins, and dense nations have no roads between them — is where unknown unknowns
announce themselves. (The map renders the shoreline of ignorance, not the
contents of the dark.)

**Not one person's tool:** a vibrant ecosystem of humans and agents on
different layers — Rama the scalable ground truth, Electric the reactive
tissue, WebGPU the visible world (one day 3D interactive models: drug
structures, living systems, local minds running beside you), LLMs and humans in
symbiosis. A social network for collective intelligence: the idea map for
builders of what can now be built on top of science — and when they get to
building, the paradigm is the same land, one layer down.

**Why it must exist:** the map humanity has of what it knows is fragmented,
hard to evaluate, and loses track of its own progress. Sid wants a reasonable
attack on the outcome of **humans living healthy forever** — the map is the
instrument; longevity is the first territory it must serve.

**Packages serving H1:** relation-kernel (running), transcript→commit/doc join
extractor + git-commit-metadata adapter, first face render.

### H2 — QUEUED (needs H1) · Relations make agent handoff cheaper
*Serves North: "legible to machine minds" (View 3).*

Evidence field: our own work packages. Kill-conditions pre-registered at

### H3 — QUEUED (needs H1) · Public form: a second mind can enter
*Serves North: "visitable by other minds."*
**Claim:** a local world can be handed to another mind — another person, or
Sid's other domain (DG day-job) — without that mind rebuilding context. First
real test of the public-form center.
**Sharpened 2026-07-03:** H3 is the generalization experiment — the
VisiCalc-vs-Engelbart test. What it measures is **transfer cost**: how much of
Sid (discipline, ontology, practices) must travel with the tool. The
"explosion" thesis (every org needs this; dream tool for metascience) is true
exactly to the degree the tool encodes the discipline instead of requiring it.
Candidate second mind: a metascience / discourse-graph researcher from Sid's
existing network — the community the data model descends from, mission-aligned,
tolerant of rough tools. Reality check: generalization arrives
adapter-by-adapter (a VC's memos ≠ Sid's transcripts); the SourceArtifact
adapter architecture is already the right shape for that rollout.
**Re-sequenced 2026-07-04 (Sid, in-session; verbatim in `vision/LOG.md`
2026-07-04 entry):** H3 activates only after Softland is used to make sense of
Softland and to build Softland from Softland — the solo builder is the first
visitor class, and working on-the-fly UIs are part of that gate; the
metascience second mind comes after. (Rhyme worth noting: this gate is a weak
form of the D-003 Regime-2 self-hosting test, arriving on the ladder from

**Sharpened 2026-07-04, later same day (Sid; verbatim in the second
2026-07-04 LOG entry):** the second mind will NOT be a metascientist — "it
will be agents and llms": a benchmark-style evaluation where a model, with

by default rather than compound.

## Candidates — the claims inbox (added 2026-07-03, Sid's intake idea)

Any claim, hypothesis, or bet Sid holds can land here raw — from `vision/LOG.md`
entries, wall panels, or a session spark. **Entry is cheap; activation is
rationed.** On intake, a session (Fable-grade for the sharpening) converts each
raw claim into: a falsifiable statement, pre-registered kill/confirm evidence,
and its dependencies — then files it here. Rules:

- Candidates NEVER enter the ladder by accumulation. The ladder keeps **one
  ACTIVE bet**; promotion happens at a review sitting, by Sid's call, when a
  slot opens.
- A candidate may resolve WITHOUT ever activating (evidence arrives from
  elsewhere) — record it in the verdict log and strike it.
- The verbatim source stays in `vision/LOG.md`; only the sharpened form lives
  here. If candidates pile up unsharpened or unreviewed, say so at the next
  sitting — this section is an inbox, not a graveyard.
