Softland — a dummy you can walk

This is a land made of software — one world from the map of human knowledge down to a single claim. Not an app with pages: one ground, every zoom.

Scroll to zoom. Drag to pan. Head for the nation called fusion energy and keep zooming — the map becomes trails, the trails become claims. Then take the morning walk (top left) and do what inhabitants do: read what changed, judge it where it lives, sign your name.

prefilled demo data — the real land renders all of this live from an append-only event log. amber = someone staked their name. fog = honestly unknown. nothing here nags.
