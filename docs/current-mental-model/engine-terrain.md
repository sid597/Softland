## The topology — five cores, a rim, a spine

The cores are truth/serve-side; none needs the rim; every core has a
cheap now-form (the three-slice ladder).

- **CORE P — the pulse** (multi-cascade): table → runner + topology →
  clock → rows-as-material. Mouths: autotag · episode-retry · band-acts ·
  the consolidator · announces. Sits under three other cores' tempos —
  goes first, and already is first.
- **CORE A — altitude, UNBUNDLED**: one shared CONTRACT
  (faithful-smaller-representation, tier-marked, drillable to grounds);
  three separate MACHINES — geometric condensation on the scroll wheel ·
  consolidation on the clock · briefing assembly on demand. Sharing
  emerges by second-wearer or not at all ("one organ, three mouths" was
  the coherence attractor; retired 2026-07-26).
- **CORE M — membership**: contains-edges (cheap; arrives with the first
  authored thing) + durable frames — positions become per-frame facts —
  the monster's ONE scary migration, cutover-class, last.
- **CORE X — arrows**: the closed arrow-kind enum in the relation kernel;
  every cross-boundary flow (harvest, fork, portal, briefing) stamps its
  crossing here. "No side-channel state" at world scale.
- **CORE D — diff/merge**: one family at three grains — deviation-diff
  (EXISTS) → scene-diff (staged) → world-diff + three-way merge (needs
  M's ids + X's base refs).

**The rim** — expensive input/render machinery; each piece waits on a
named lived want; none is on any core's critical path:

|---|---|
| D1 cross-container pick-chain | a thing needs interior MOTION (own scroll/zoom) |

**The spine:**

    V (marks) ──honesty──▶ IV-a (condensation) ──▶ VII (diff-at-altitude) ──▶ the meet
    III (pulse) ──▶ consolidator · band-acts · episode-retry · announces

    IV ∧ VIII converge at altitude briefings

**Derivation heuristics** (kept so future amendments argue with the
rules, not just the output): five moves at many grains — the work graph
is move-shaped, not organ-shaped · truth-side before input-side · the
three-slice ladder (declaration → durable mechanism at its first honest
customer → editable material at 2–3 real rows) · shared-core extraction
bounded by second-wearer (extract contracts, not machines) · the
reversibility gradient (hard-to-reverse late, after evidence) ·
**topology constrains, contact schedules** — the map never owns the
calendar.

## The five moves (why this is weeks-of-packages, not years)

Give a thing an address · move its policy into material · put its changes
on the pulse · make crossings ride declared arrows · brief every mind at
its altitude. Blocks have all five; the space has its first two. Things,
worlds, and minds are the same five moves at bigger grains. The chopping
question is never "which organ" — it is **"which grain gets the next
move."**
